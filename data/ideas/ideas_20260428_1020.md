After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their features.
	2. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to partner for cashback offers.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some marketing expenses).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks).
