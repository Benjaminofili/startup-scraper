After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and businesses that want to advertise on the platform.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with a 5% commission on cashback rewards.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
