After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Review**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaaSReview
2. **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and frustrated users.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based review platform that evaluates and provides feedback on SaaS landing pages, helping businesses improve their conversion rates.
4. **WHO PAYS**: SaaS businesses and entrepreneurs looking to improve their landing pages.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per review (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Set up a basic website using a free template and AWS free tier.
	* Step 2: Create a review template and criteria for evaluating SaaS landing pages.
	* Step 3: Reach out to 10 SaaS businesses and offer free reviews in exchange for feedback and testimonials.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from the Reddit user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a review platform that can help businesses improve their landing pages.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for website setup and marketing).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for reviews, but potential for automation).
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional reviewers).
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic website and review template).

**2. Open-Source Alternative to Google Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians rely on Google products, but some users are looking for alternative, open-source solutions.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform that offers open-source alternatives to popular Google products, such as search, email, and cloud storage.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual users and businesses looking for alternative solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional premium features for ₦2,000 per month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and identify open-source alternatives to Google products.
	* Step 2: Set up a basic website and integrate the open-source alternatives.
	* Step 3: Promote the platform on social media and online forums.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about Google products and the interest in open-source alternatives (e.g., LibreTaxi) suggest a growing demand for alternative solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (no costs for development, as open-source solutions are already available).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (potential for automated revenue streams through premium features).
	* Team Size: 9/10 (can be managed with a small team, as the platform is based on existing open-source solutions).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some customization and testing).

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some customization and testing).
