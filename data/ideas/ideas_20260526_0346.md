**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
* **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
* **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with mindless scrolling on social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their scrolling habits, providing tips to reduce screen time.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals ( ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction)
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
  2. Design a basic UI/UX for the app.
  3. Develop a prototype using existing APIs (e.g., Google's Digital Wellbeing API).
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (can use existing APIs and free resources)
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
