**1. Alternative to Garbage SaaS Landing Pages**
* **IDEA NAME**: LandingPageNG
* **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly landing page builder with templates and a drag-and-drop editor.
* **WHO PAYS**: Small businesses and entrepreneurs in Nigeria who need a professional online presence.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month (basic plan) or ₦10,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing landing page builders and identify key features to include.
	2. Design a basic template and user interface using Figma or Adobe XD.
	3. Set up a basic landing page using AWS free tier and test with a small group of users.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about garbage SaaS landing pages on Reddit highlights the need for a better solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but can start with a basic version)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)
