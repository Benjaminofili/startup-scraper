**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Users get stuck in infinite scrolling on social media, wasting time and decreasing productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their scrolling habits, providing tips to break the cycle.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦500/month subscription)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's scrolling habits.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about this issue.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
