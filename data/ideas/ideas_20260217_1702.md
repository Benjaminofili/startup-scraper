After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
2. **PROBLEM**: Customers feel disrespected by businesses, leading to negative reviews and loss of trust.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review businesses based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer complaints about businesses in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
	* Step 3: Reach out to 10 local businesses to test the app and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about bad service and disrespect for customers highlights the need for a platform that promotes customer respect.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, existing AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for business outreach and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
