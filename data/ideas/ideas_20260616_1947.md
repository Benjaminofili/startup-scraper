After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market with zero-capital and passive income potential. Here are the ideas:

**1. Personal Finance Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: NairaTracker
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to keep track of their expenses and manage their finances effectively.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that allows users to track their income and expenses, set budgets, and receive alerts for overspending.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians aged 25-45 who are looking to manage their finances better.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing open-source templates.
 3. Develop a basic version of the app using the AWS free tier and test it with a small group of users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing personal finance and budgeting, indicating a growing interest in this area.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using open-source templates and AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

* **IDEA NAME**: SwapNG
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for affordable options to buy and sell second-hand items, but existing platforms are often unreliable or expensive.

* **WHO PAYS**: Low- to middle-class Nigerians who are looking for affordable options to buy and sell second-hand items.
* **PRICE**: ₦100/transaction (flat fee) or ₦500/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing online marketplaces and identify areas for improvement.
 2. Develop a basic version of the platform using the AWS free tier and test it with a small group of users.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the platform and attract early users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing online marketplaces and second-hand items, indicating a growing interest in this area.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, using open-source templates and AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (transaction fees can generate passive income)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but may require more time to attract users)
