**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score management, tips, and alerts to help users improve their creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100/month (basic features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify potential partners.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as credit score tracking and alerts.
 3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit thread "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?", indicating a need for a credit management solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but potential partnerships may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the app, but may require additional staff for customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature development may take longer)
