**1. NavGuard**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NavGuard
2. **PROBLEM**: Poor navigation and mapping services in Nigeria, leading to frustration and wasted time.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides alternative, community-driven navigation and mapping services, leveraging user feedback and reviews.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual users, particularly drivers and commuters, who can subscribe to premium features or pay per transaction for advanced navigation services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦50 per transaction
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify existing navigation and mapping services in Nigeria and their limitations.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) for NavGuard, focusing on core features and user feedback mechanisms.
	* Step 3: Launch a social media campaign to raise awareness and gather user interest.
7. **WHY NOW**: User complaints about poor navigation services, such as the issue with Uber Maps, highlight the need for alternative solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
