After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores, as well as partner stores themselves who pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to download), with cashback rewards ranging from 1-10% of purchase value.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cashback rewards.
 2. Develop a basic mobile app using existing APIs and frameworks.
 3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, cashback rewards apps are in high demand, and users are looking for ways to save money.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, existing APIs)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team, but requires partnerships with stores)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development, but requires partnerships)
