**1.**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle with managing and improving their credit scores, as seen in the complaint "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit score changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100/credit score report.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on existing credit score management apps in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
 3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The user complaint highlights the need for credit score management, and the Nigerian market lacks a user-friendly and affordable solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model with potential for automated credit score reports)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (depending on the complexity of the MVP and partnerships)
