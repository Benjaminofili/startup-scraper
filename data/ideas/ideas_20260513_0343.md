After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income due to limited financial resources.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items, from partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases and partner stores that pay for advertising and promotions.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with a 5% commission on cashback rewards.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, making this a timely opportunity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and development)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage partner relationships and app development)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but some external partners required)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (quick launch possible, but some development and testing required)

* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find the best prices for everyday items, leading to overspending.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to compare prices for groceries, household items, and other essentials across different stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Users who make purchases through the app and partner stores that pay for advertising and promotions.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with a 2% commission on sales generated through the app.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Develop a web scraper to collect price data from popular online stores.
	2. Create a basic web and mobile app using the web and Android developers on the team.
	3. Partner with 3 local stores to offer exclusive discounts to app users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are looking for ways to save money and make informed purchasing decisions, making this a relevant solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low capital needed, with potential for revenue through commissions)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (mostly automated, with some manual work required for development and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team needed, with some external partners required)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (quick launch possible, with some development and testing required)
