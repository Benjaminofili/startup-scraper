After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Digital Credit Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals repair their damaged credit scores, which can affect their financial stability.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform that provides personalized credit repair plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the credit repair landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic website with a lead capture form to gauge interest.
 3. Partner with a credit reporting agency to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial investment in website development and marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 people needed for initial launch)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)

**2. E-paper Dashboard for Nigerian Families**
* **IDEA NAME**: FamilyBoard

* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows families to create a customized dashboard for sharing notes, calendars, and to-do lists.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerian families with multiple members.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct user research to understand the needs of Nigerian families.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using existing frameworks.
 3. Test the app with a small group of families.
* **WHY NOW**: The idea of a family e-paper dashboard is inspired by the HackerNews post "I built Timeframe, our family e-paper dashboard".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low initial investment required)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 people needed for initial launch)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within 1-2 months)
