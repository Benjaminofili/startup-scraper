After analyzing the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback and rewards platforms for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback and rewards for users who shop at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores and brands pay for advertising and promotions.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 spent).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and list potential partner stores and brands.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Reach out to partner stores and brands to discuss collaboration opportunities.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, and CashBackNG can capitalize on this trend.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs can be low, but partnerships may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback and rewards system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but partnerships may take time to establish)
