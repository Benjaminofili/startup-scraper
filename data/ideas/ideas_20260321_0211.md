**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app, focusing on simplicity and user-friendliness.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimal viable product (MVP) using the Android developer's skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing interest in solutions to combat excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing Android developer skills and AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused development effort)

**2. LibreRide Nigeria**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide Nigeria
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face challenges with transportation, including high costs and limited options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting drivers with passengers in Nigeria.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Drivers and passengers who use the platform ( commission-based revenue model)
5. **PRICE**: 10% commission per ride (average ride fare: ₦500)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic web platform using the web developer's skills, focusing on user registration and ride booking.
	* Step 3: Integrate APIs for payment processing and map integration.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft" post on HackerNews highlights the potential for a similar solution in Nigeria.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (requires some initial investment for marketing and driver onboarding)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (commission-based revenue model, but requires ongoing platform maintenance)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a team with diverse skills, including web development, marketing, and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (may take several months to launch and gain traction)
