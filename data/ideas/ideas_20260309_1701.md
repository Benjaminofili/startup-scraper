**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and user-friendly app interface using existing templates.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are seeking solutions to mitigate its negative effects.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources and free tier AWS)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for personalized coaching)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Alternative**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with the high costs of ride-hailing services like Uber and Lyft.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, using a peer-to-peer model.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform is free, and drivers can set their own fares).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free for passengers, drivers set their own fares).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform using web and API technologies.
	* Step 3: Recruit a small group of drivers to test the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable transportation options is high in Nigeria, and a free ride-hailing platform can capitalize on this need.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing open-source resources and free tier AWS)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through advertising or partnerships)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team to manage the platform)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within months)
