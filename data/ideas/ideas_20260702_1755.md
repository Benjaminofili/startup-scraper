After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on everyday essentials like groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Reach out to potential partner stores to discuss integration.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, and with the growing e-commerce market in Nigeria, there's a need for a cashback app.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some investment for marketing).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback earnings can be automated).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and app development).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but may take months to scale).
