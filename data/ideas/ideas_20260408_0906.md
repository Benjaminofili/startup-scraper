After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **DoomScroll**
* **PROBLEM**: Users get stuck in infinite scrolling on social media, wasting time and reducing productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, with features like time tracking, app blocking, and personalized productivity tips.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals who want to improve their productivity and focus.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
 2. Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with core features.
 3. Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling on HackerNews ("Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass") indicates a growing concern about social media addiction.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic MVP and iterate)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)

**2.** **E-Payment Helper**
* **PROBLEM**: Drivers and merchants struggle with card payments, finding them inconvenient and time-consuming.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that simplifies card payments for drivers and merchants, with features like easy payment processing, transaction tracking, and customer support.
* **WHO PAYS**: Drivers and small business owners who want to accept card payments easily.
* **PRICE**: ₦100 per transaction (flat fee) or ₦5,000/month (unlimited transactions).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing payment solutions and their limitations.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
 3. Partner with a payment gateway to facilitate transactions.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about card payments on the PlayStore ("Drivers hate the idea of card payments. They say it's really inconvenient for them") highlights the need for a convenient payment solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (requires some investment for payment gateway integration and marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (transaction fees can generate passive income)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team with development and marketing expertise)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch within months, but requires payment gateway integration)
