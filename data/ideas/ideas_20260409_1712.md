After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research on existing screen time tracking apps.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing the need to limit social media usage, and an app like this can help address that concern.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and Play Store account).
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (in-app subscription, but requires occasional updates).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires at least 2 developers, 1 marketer).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype).
