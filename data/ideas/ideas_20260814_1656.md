After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Notion Alternative for Nigerian Students**
* **IDEA NAME**: EduNote
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students need a free, open-source alternative to Notion for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that offers a similar column-based note-taking system like Notion, with features tailored to Nigerian students' needs.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and small businesses in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the specific needs of Nigerian students.
	2. Design a minimum viable product (MVP) with core features.
	3. Set up a GitHub repository for open-source collaboration.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion, and Nigerian students need a tailored solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source and free resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (premium features and potential ads)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (core team of 2-3 developers and a marketer)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched within weeks)
