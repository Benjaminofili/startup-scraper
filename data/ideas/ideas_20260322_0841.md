After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps that offer rewards for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards to users for their purchases.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local businesses in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage partnerships and app updates)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with 3 developers and 1 marketer)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)
