4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses will pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their service.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
