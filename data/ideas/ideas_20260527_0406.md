After analyzing the problems and complaints from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **NaijaSpend**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find affordable alternatives to expensive products and services.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that aggregates affordable products and services from local salvage stores, markets, and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Low-to-middle-income Nigerians looking for affordable options.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦100 per transaction.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify affordable product and service providers.
 2. Develop a basic web scraper to aggregate data from online platforms.
 3. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for affordable alternatives, and the current economic situation in Nigeria makes it an ideal time to launch such a platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some marketing budget)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to update listings and manage user accounts)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)

**2.** **CreditPal**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to manage their credit scores and find affordable credit options.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, credit monitoring, and connects users with affordable credit options from local lenders.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history looking to improve their credit scores.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month or ₦500 per credit report.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with local credit bureaus and lenders.
 2. Develop a basic credit scoring algorithm and integrate it with the app.
 3. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, and the app can help them manage and improve their creditworthiness.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (requires partnerships with credit bureaus and lenders, which may involve some costs)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (credit report sales and affiliate marketing can generate passive income)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team with expertise in credit scoring and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (requires some time to establish partnerships and develop the algorithm)
