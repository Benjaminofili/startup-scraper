**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all transactions (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using AWS free tier.
 3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, using AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
