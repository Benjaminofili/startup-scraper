**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste hours scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features like screen time tracking, alerts, and personalized tips.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio, focusing on core functionality.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time (e.g., "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources)

	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a single developer, but marketing help is needed)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
