After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, especially when affected by others' actions.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score monitoring, alerts, and personalized advice to help users improve their credit health.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) who are concerned about their credit scores and willing to pay for premium features.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and existing solutions.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of credit score mismanagement is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential partnerships can reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, but full feature set may take months)
