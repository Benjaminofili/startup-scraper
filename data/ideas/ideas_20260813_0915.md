After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students and professionals need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals who currently use paid note-taking apps or services.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free), with optional donations or premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and their features.
 2. Define the core features and user interface for NoteHub.
 3. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and NoteHub can fill this gap.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 10/10 (no initial investment needed)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and premium features can generate income)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a minimal viable product)
