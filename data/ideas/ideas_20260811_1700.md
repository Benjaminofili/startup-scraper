After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with expensive or limited note-taking apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features similar to Notion or Outline, available on the web and Android.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and small businesses who need a reliable note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦100-₦500 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include.
	* Step 2: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
	* Step 3: Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and flexible note-taking solutions is high, as seen in the HackerNews discussion about open-source alternatives to Notion.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (only requires development time and existing open-source libraries)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team or solo)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (depending on the complexity of features)
