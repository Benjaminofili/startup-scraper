After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (e.g., ₦500 cashback on a ₦10,000 purchase).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and list potential partner stores.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
	* Reach out to partner stores to discuss commission structures.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, but requires continuous improvement).
