After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic Android app using the existing Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback is a passive revenue stream).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).

**2. PriceCompareNG**

* **PRICE**: Free for users.
* **FIRST WEEK**:

	2. Create a simple web and mobile app to display price comparisons.
	3. Partner with 3 stores to integrate their pricing data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and price comparison apps are in demand.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (zero initial investment).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (affiliate marketing revenue).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team to manage development and partnerships).
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly).
