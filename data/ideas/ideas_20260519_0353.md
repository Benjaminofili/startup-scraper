After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at local stores and online platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and online platforms.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local stores and online platforms.
	* Develop a basic mobile app using existing APIs.
	* Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit posts about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback rewards can be automated).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic app).
