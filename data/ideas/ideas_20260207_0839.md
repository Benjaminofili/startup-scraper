**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback apps that offer rewards for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards for users.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research local businesses and potential partners.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic cashback features.
 3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, and the Nigerian market lacks a reliable solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and advertising revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partnerships and customer support).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional staff for growth).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP quickly, with iterations and improvements over time).
