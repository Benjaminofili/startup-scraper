* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app providing credit score monitoring, financial education, and personalized advice for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on credit score services in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the mobile app using existing APIs.
 3. Partner with financial experts to create educational content.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score management.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing APIs and free resources).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and customer support).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for growth).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks).
