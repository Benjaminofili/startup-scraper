After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Preventer**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Preventer
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling mindlessly on social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing features like website blockers, time trackers, and motivational quotes.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals in Nigeria who want to improve their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year (approximately $1.25/month or $5/year).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to promote the app on social media and blogs.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly common, and users are looking for solutions to help them stay focused.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be built using existing APIs and tools)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and marketing)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team of 2-3 people)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Alternative**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians cannot afford the high costs of ride-hailing services like Uber and Lyft.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, using a peer-to-peer model.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform is free, and drivers can choose to accept tips or donations).
5. **PRICE**: Free
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a community of drivers and passengers to test and provide feedback on the platform.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the platform and attract more users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The high costs of ride-hailing services are a major pain point for many Nigerians, and a free alternative could gain significant traction.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (can be built using existing open-source platforms)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (some manual work required for community management)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of 3-4 people to manage the platform and community)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months)

2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy product reviews, leading to poor purchasing decisions.
3. **SOLUTION**: A platform that allows users to review and rate products, with a focus on durability and quality.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses and manufacturers who want to promote their products on the platform.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month (approximately $12.50/month) for businesses to list their products.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype of the platform using existing APIs and tools.
	* Step 3: Reach out to businesses and manufacturers to partner with the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The lack of reliable product reviews is a significant pain point for many Nigerians, and a platform that addresses this issue could gain significant traction.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (requires some investment in marketing and partnerships)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (some manual work required for content moderation and partnerships)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a team of 2-3 people to manage the platform and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within a few months)
