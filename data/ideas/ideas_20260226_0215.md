2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.

	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using AWS free tier services.
