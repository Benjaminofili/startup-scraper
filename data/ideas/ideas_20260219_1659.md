After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative to SaaS Landing Pages**
* **IDEA NAME**: EasyLanding
* **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, template-based landing page builder for SaaS companies.
* **WHO PAYS**: Small to medium-sized SaaS businesses in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing landing page builders and identify key features to include.
	2. Design a basic template library using open-source resources.
	3. Set up a simple payment gateway using a Nigerian payment processor.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews have expressed frustration with poorly designed SaaS landing pages.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources and open-source tools)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for template updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. Personalized Product Review Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: ReviewHub
* **PROBLEM**: Consumers struggle to find trustworthy product reviews, especially for durable goods.
* **SOLUTION**: A platform that aggregates reviews from multiple sources and provides personalized product recommendations.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers seeking reliable product reviews, as well as businesses looking to promote their products.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month (consumer subscription), ₦10,000 per month (business listing).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing review platforms and identify key features to include.
	2. Develop a basic algorithm for aggregating and personalizing reviews.
	3. Partner with a few local businesses to list their products on the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on HackerNews have shown interest in alternative review platforms, such as Rotten Tomatoes-style platforms.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (requires some development effort)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can be automated with algorithms and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months)
