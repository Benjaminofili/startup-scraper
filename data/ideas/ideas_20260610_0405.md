After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
	* Develop a basic app with the Android developer.
	* Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for marketing and development).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for marketing and development).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch in a few weeks).

6. **FIRST WEEK**:
