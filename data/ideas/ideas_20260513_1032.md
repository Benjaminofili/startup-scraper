**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, with features like website blocking and reminders to take breaks.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and frameworks.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with core features.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be built with existing resources and APIs).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for maintenance and updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).
