After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and budget. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and rewarding cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	* Develop a basic app using the Android developer on the team.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, and existing solutions are not reliable or rewarding.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and partnerships).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (cashback rewards can be automated).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).

**2. SaveNG**

2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money due to limited access to reliable and automated savings platforms.

4. **WHO PAYS**: Users who save money through the app (small monthly fee).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦1,000 per month (depending on the savings plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and develop a basic savings algorithm.
	* Design a user-friendly interface for the web and mobile app.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and existing solutions are not automated or reliable.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (no initial investment needed).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (savings can be automated).
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team to manage the app).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).
