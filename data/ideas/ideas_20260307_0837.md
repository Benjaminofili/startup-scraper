**1. RideTracker**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideTracker
2. **PROBLEM**: Scheduling a ride with unreliable drivers can lead to missed pickups and wasted time.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to track their scheduled rides in real-time, ensuring drivers arrive at the pickup location on time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) and individual drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (per driver) or ₦50/transaction (per ride).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify potential partners (ride-hailing companies) and competitors.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer's skills.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the app and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a reliable ride-scheduling solution, and the team can leverage the existing Play Store account to launch the app.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but can start with a basic prototype)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for customer support and marketing)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for marketing and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
