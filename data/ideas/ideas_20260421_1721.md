**1. LibreRide - Alternative to Uber/Lyft**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians cannot afford or access traditional ride-hailing services like Uber or Lyft.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with drivers in Nigeria.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay a small fee per ride, while drivers earn a commission.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (average)
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
    * Step 1: Research and understand the Nigerian transportation market and regulations.
    * Step 2: Develop a basic Android app for riders and drivers using the AWS free tier.
    * Step 3: Launch a social media campaign to promote LibreRide and recruit initial drivers and riders.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the success of LibreTaxi, there's a proven demand for alternative ride-hailing services, and Nigeria's large population presents a significant opportunity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
    * Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing)
    * Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and driver management)
    * Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers and support staff as the service grows)
    * Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version quickly, but may require several months to refine and expand the service)
