**1. Receipt Scanner App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ReceiptPal
2. **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to keep track of their expenses and receipts, leading to missed savings opportunities.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to scan and digitize their receipts, categorize expenses, and earn cashback rewards.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, particularly those who frequently shop at participating stores.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing receipt scanning apps and their features.
	* Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users have shown interest in receipt scanning apps, and the Nigerian market lacks a dominant player.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing AWS free tier and developer resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires partnerships with stores for cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for marketing and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)
