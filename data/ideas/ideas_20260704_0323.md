After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. Digital Credit Score Builder**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, limiting their access to loans and financial services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users monitor and improve their credit scores through personalized financial advice, credit monitoring, and loan recommendations.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in the 25-45 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,500 per month (basic plan) or ₦5,000 per month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify potential partners.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit monitoring and advice features.
	3. Establish partnerships with local credit bureaus and financial institutions to access credit data and offer loan recommendations.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like the 25-year-old whose mother damaged their credit score (My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?) need a solution to recover from credit score damage.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships to reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (core team of 2-3, with potential for expansion)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched within 2-3 months)

**2. Online Marketplace for Second-Hand Items**
* **IDEA NAME**: SwapNG
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to find reliable platforms to buy and sell second-hand items, leading to a lack of trust and limited market access.
* **SOLUTION**: A peer-to-peer online marketplace for buying and selling second-hand items, with features like user reviews, ratings, and secure payment processing.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals looking to buy or sell second-hand items, particularly students and low-income households.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per transaction (flat fee) or 5% commission on sales.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the demand for second-hand items in Nigeria and identify potential competitors.
	2. Develop an MVP with basic buying and selling features, user reviews, and ratings.
	3. Establish partnerships with local logistics companies to facilitate secure and reliable delivery.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like the one who's frustrated with their Buy Nothing group (Anyone else just over their Buy Nothing group?) need a reliable platform for buying and selling second-hand items.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, with potential for revenue from transaction fees)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (mostly automated, with some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (core team of 2-3, with potential for expansion)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (MVP can be launched within 1-2 months)
