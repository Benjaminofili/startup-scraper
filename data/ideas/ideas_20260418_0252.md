Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, freelancers, and small business owners in Nigeria.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations (₦500/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Canva.
	* Step 3: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many users are looking for alternatives to Notion or Outline, and an open-source solution can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, except for potential server costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by 1-2 developers)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched in a few weeks)

**2. Personal Finance Tracker**
1. **IDEA NAME**: FinancePal
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to track their expenses and stay on top of their finances.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly personal finance tracker app with features like budgeting, expense tracking, and bill reminders.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small business owners in Nigeria.
5. **PRICE**: Free (basic version), ₦1,000/month (premium version with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing personal finance apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Canva.
	* Step 3: Set up a basic backend using AWS free tier and start building the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many users are looking for ways to manage their finances, and a simple, user-friendly app can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, except for potential server costs)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be built by 1-2 developers)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched in a few weeks)

**3. E-Learning Platform for Nigerian Students**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LearnNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerian students lack access to quality educational resources and study materials.
3. **SOLUTION**: An e-learning platform with a library of study materials, video lectures, and practice quizzes for Nigerian students.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, parents, and schools in Nigeria.
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic subscription), ₦5,000/month (premium subscription with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing e-learning platforms and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Partner with educators and content creators to develop study materials.
	* Step 3: Set up a basic platform using WordPress or a similar CMS.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many students are looking for online learning resources, and a platform focused on Nigerian students can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some investment needed for content creation and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be built by 2-3 developers and content creators)
	* Time to Market: 5/10 (can be launched in a few months)
