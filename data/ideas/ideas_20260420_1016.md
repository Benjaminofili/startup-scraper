After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, freelancers, and small business owners who want a free or low-cost note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and intuitive UI/UX for the app.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using existing open-source libraries and frameworks.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternatives to Notion and Outline, and an open-source solution can gain traction quickly.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no initial investment needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through donations or premium features)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by a small team or solo)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (may take a few weeks to build and test)
