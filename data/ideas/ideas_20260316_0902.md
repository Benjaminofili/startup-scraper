**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
* **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
* **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
 2. Design a simple, user-friendly interface for the app.
 3. Develop a basic version of the app using existing APIs and frameworks.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on HackerNews and Reddit are complaining about the negative effects of excessive social media usage, making this a timely solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can be developed with existing resources and minimal capital)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts may require additional help)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)

**2. LibreRide - Alternative to Uber/Lyft**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face difficulties with existing ride-hailing services, such as high prices and limited availability.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, allowing users to request rides from verified drivers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses looking for affordable transportation options.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (or a monthly subscription for frequent users).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
 2. Develop a basic version of the app using open-source frameworks and APIs.
 3. Partner with local driver associations to recruit verified drivers.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on HackerNews are discussing alternatives to existing ride-hailing services, making this a relevant solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 5/10 (requires some capital for marketing and driver recruitment)
	+ Passive Income: 4/10 (requires manual work for driver management and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team with diverse skills, including marketing and driver management)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (may take several weeks to launch with a functional network of drivers)
