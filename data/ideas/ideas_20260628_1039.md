After analyzing the problems and complaints from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards to users for shopping at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback reward per transaction (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web developers.
	3. Reach out to partner stores to discuss commission rates and integration.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, but some marketing budget required).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated, with potential for high earnings).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but requires some marketing and business development efforts).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (few weeks to launch, depending on app development and partner store integration).

**2. SaveNG**

* **WHO PAYS**: Users pay a small monthly fee (e.g., ₦100) for the service.
* **PRICE**: ₦100 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web developers.
	2. Integrate with Nigerian banks for secure transactions.
	3. Test the app with a small group of users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and automated savings platforms are gaining popularity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (very low investment needed, with potential for high earnings).
	+ Passive Income: 10/10 (fully automated, with potential for high earnings).
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (small team needed, with some marketing efforts required).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (few weeks to launch, depending on app development and bank integration).
