**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with frequent logouts and login issues on various apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: An Android app that automatically logs users back into their accounts after a logout, using secure token storage and renewal.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual app users, particularly those who use multiple apps with frequent logouts.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research existing login management solutions and identify potential security risks.
	* Day 3-4: Design a user-friendly interface for the app and plan the token storage and renewal system.
	* Day 5-7: Begin building the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about an app logging users out and not allowing them to log back in highlights the need for a solution like AutoLogin.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (no significant capital needed, but some potential costs for security audits)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and token management)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for security and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but may require some time for testing and security audits)
