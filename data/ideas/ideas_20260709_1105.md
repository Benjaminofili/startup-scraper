4. **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians ( ₦5,000 - ₦20,000 monthly income) seeking to improve their credit scores.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features), ₦0 (basic features)

	* Research existing credit scoring systems in Nigeria (e.g., Credit Bureau Nigeria).
