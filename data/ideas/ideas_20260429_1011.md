**1. DoomScroll**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScroll
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media scrolling time, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based model)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) for the app, focusing on core features.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to raise awareness about the app's benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of excessive social media scrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to improve their mental health.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused development effort)

**2. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with the high costs and inefficiencies of traditional taxi services.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting drivers with passengers and promoting a sharing economy.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers who use the service (pay-per-ride model)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride ( competitive with traditional taxi services)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms and adapt them to the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a community-driven approach to recruit and onboard drivers.
	* Step 3: Establish partnerships with local organizations to promote the service.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and efficient transportation services is high in Nigeria, and a free and open-source solution can gain traction.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, as the platform can be built on existing open-source solutions)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (some manual work required for driver onboarding and customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and driver recruitment)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months with a focused development effort)
