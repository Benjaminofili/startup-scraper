**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to maintain a good credit score, leading to difficulties in accessing loans and other financial services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history, as well as financial institutions looking to promote credit literacy.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit tracking features.
	3. Partner with financial institutions to offer exclusive promotions to their customers.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score management tools.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and revenue sharing).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for coaching and support).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, with continuous iteration and improvement).
