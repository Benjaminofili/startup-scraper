After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Demerit System for Ride-hailing Apps**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideSafe
2. **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers often drop fares without a valid reason, causing inconvenience to passengers.
3. **SOLUTION**: An app that introduces a demerit system to deter drivers from dropping fares without a valid reason, ensuring a smoother ride-hailing experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) would pay for the integration of the demerit system into their platforms.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (for 100 drivers) or ₦50 per transaction (for each ride completed without issues).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing apps in Nigeria and their APIs.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype of the demerit system.
	* Step 3: Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential integration.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore comment suggests that users are frustrated with drivers dropping fares, making this a pressing issue to solve.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but potential for revenue from ride-hailing companies)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers and a marketer can handle the project)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (a few weeks to develop and test the prototype)

**2. End-to-End Encrypted Photo Storage**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SecurePix
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are concerned about the security of their photos stored on cloud platforms like Google Photos.
3. **SOLUTION**: A secure, end-to-end encrypted photo storage app that protects users' photos from unauthorized access.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual users would pay for the service, with a focus on professionals and individuals with sensitive information.
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month (for 1GB of storage) or ₦500 per year (for 100MB of storage).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing end-to-end encryption technologies and libraries.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype of the SecurePix app.
	* Step 3: Create a landing page to promote the app and gather interest from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post about an end-to-end encrypted alternative to Google Photos highlights the demand for secure photo storage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, using existing encryption libraries)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers can handle the project)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (a few weeks to develop and test the prototype)
