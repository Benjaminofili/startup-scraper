**1. BugFix**
1. **IDEA NAME**: BugFix
2. **PROBLEM**: Many app users experience frustration with bugs and glitches, such as logging out issues, which can lead to a poor user experience.
3. **SOLUTION**: BugFix is a simple, user-friendly app testing and feedback platform that helps developers identify and fix bugs quickly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and small businesses with limited budgets.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month or ₦1,000 per bug fix.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify potential clients and competitors.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	* Step 3: Create a social media presence and start promoting the service.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the PlayStore highlights the need for reliable app testing and feedback solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost marketing and development)

	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible)
