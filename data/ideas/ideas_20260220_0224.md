Based on the user complaints and the team's resources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: Durable Product Review Platform
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to review and compare durable products, leading to poor purchasing decisions.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app and web platform where users can review and rate durable products, similar to Rotten Tomatoes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers (e.g., manufacturers of durable products) and affiliate marketing.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free for users), ₦5,000 - ₦10,000 (monthly advertising packages).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular durable products in Nigeria.
	2. Design a basic prototype of the app and web platform.
	3. Reach out to potential advertisers and affiliate partners.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint on HackerNews about building a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products highlights the need for such a platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but advertising and marketing may require some investment).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (advertising and affiliate marketing can generate passive income, but user engagement and content moderation may require manual work).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the platform, but more developers and marketers may be needed as the platform grows).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (the platform can be launched quickly, but advertising and marketing efforts may take time to generate revenue).
