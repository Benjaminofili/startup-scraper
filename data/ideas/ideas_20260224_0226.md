After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. RideHail Alternative**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Uber turns drivers into non-thinking drones and has high fees.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app connecting riders with local, independent drivers, offering better rates and more human interaction.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders and drivers (commission-based)
5. **PRICE**: ₦50-100 per ride (commission)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing ride-hailing apps and their limitations
	* Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer's skills
	* Conduct surveys to gauge interest among potential drivers and riders
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are dissatisfied with Uber, and a local alternative could fill the gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some marketing costs, but can start small)
	* Passive Income: 4/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can launch a basic version quickly, but may need to iterate)
