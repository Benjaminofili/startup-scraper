* **IDEA NAME**: Frugal
* **PROBLEM**: Many individuals struggle to save money and find affordable alternatives for their daily needs.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates discounts, coupons, and affordable alternatives for groceries, household items, and other essentials.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class individuals and families in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦50/per transaction (discounted purchases).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify popular discount platforms and affordable stores in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs and frameworks.
 3. Partner with local stores to offer exclusive discounts to Frugal users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and the app can capitalize on the demand for affordable alternatives.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can start with minimal investment, leveraging existing APIs and frameworks)

	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, with continuous iteration and improvement)
