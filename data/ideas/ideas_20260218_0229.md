After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and budget, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**
* **IDEA NAME**: Respect Meter
* **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service from businesses, such as the one mentioned in the PlayStore complaint.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review businesses based on their customer service experience.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses will pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their service.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most common customer service pain points in Nigeria.
	2. Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to gather interest and feedback on the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a platform to hold businesses accountable for their customer service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal development cost)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work to maintain and update the app)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. Alternative to Google Products**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
* **PROBLEM**: Dependence on Google products and lack of alternatives for Nigerian users.
* **SOLUTION**: A suite of open-source, Nigeria-focused alternatives to Google products, such as search, email, and cloud storage.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual users and businesses will pay for premium features and support.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month (individual), ₦10,000 per month (business)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing open-source alternatives to Google products.
	2. Develop a customized version of these alternatives for the Nigerian market.
	3. Create a community forum to gather feedback and suggestions from users.
* **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews discussion on alternatives to Google products highlights the demand for such solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (uses existing open-source resources, minimal development cost)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can be automated with subscription-based model)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team with technical expertise)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months)
