After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily expenses, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for marketing and partnerships).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick launch possible with existing APIs).
