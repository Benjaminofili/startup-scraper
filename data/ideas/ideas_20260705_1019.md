**1. Credit Score Repair Guide**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with poor credit scores, which can be due to various factors, including misinformation or lack of knowledge on credit management.
* **SOLUTION**: Develop a mobile app or web platform that provides personalized credit score repair guidance, including tips, resources, and recommendations for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range, who are seeking to improve their creditworthiness.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month ( subscription-based model)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features and content.
 3. Create a social media presence to promote the platform and engage with potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the increasing importance of credit scores in Nigeria, there is a growing need for reliable and accessible credit score repair guidance.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs can be low, with potential for future investments)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content updates and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional expertise in credit management)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic MVP)

**2. Buy Nothing Nigeria**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with financial constraints, making it difficult to purchase essential items, and may benefit from a platform that facilitates sharing and exchange of goods.
* **SOLUTION**: Develop a mobile app or web platform that connects people who want to give away items with those who need them, similar to the Buy Nothing group concept.
* **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform can be free to use, with potential revenue streams from advertising or sponsored content)
* **PRICE**: ₦0
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing Buy Nothing groups and similar platforms to understand the concept and potential challenges.
 2. Develop a basic platform with features for user registration, item posting, and messaging.
 3. Promote the platform through social media and online communities to attract initial users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the growing awareness of sustainability and sharing economies, a platform like Buy Nothing Nigeria can capitalize on this trend.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (can be developed with minimal initial investment)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (potential for advertising revenue or sponsored content)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional support for moderation and community management)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic MVP)
