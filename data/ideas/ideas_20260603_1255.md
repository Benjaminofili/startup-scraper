**1. CreditPal**

2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to maintain good credit scores due to lack of financial education and resources.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score tracking, financial education, and alerts for credit score changes.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) who want to improve their credit scores.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with personalized financial coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.

   - Step 3: Partner with financial institutions to access credit score data.
