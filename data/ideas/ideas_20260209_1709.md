After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on Nigerian retailers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and earn cashback.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (e.g., ₦50 cashback on a ₦1000 purchase).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local retailers in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using existing cashback platforms as a reference.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit discussed the benefits of cashback apps, and with the growing Nigerian market, there's an opportunity to create a localized solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
