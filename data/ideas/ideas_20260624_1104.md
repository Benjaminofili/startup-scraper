After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on everyday items like groceries and household essentials.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, and CashBackNG can capitalize on this demand.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback is a passive income stream).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch in a few weeks with a basic app).

**2. PriceCompareNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to compare prices of everyday items across different stores.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to compare prices of items across different stores, with a focus on groceries and household essentials.
* **WHO PAYS**: Stores that want to advertise on the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month for stores to advertise (free for users).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Develop a basic web scraper to collect price data from partner stores.
	2. Create a user-friendly interface for the web and mobile app.
	3. Reach out to 10 local stores to partner and advertise on the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are looking for ways to save money, and PriceCompareNG can help them make informed purchasing decisions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low capital needed, with potential for advertising revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (advertising revenue is a passive income stream).
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development).
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (can launch in a few weeks with a basic app).
