**1. LibreNotes - Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps, seeking an open-source alternative to Notion or Outline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features like organization, tagging, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free (donations accepted), with optional premium features (₦500/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing open-source note-taking apps (e.g., Simplenote, TiddlyWiki).
	* Set up a GitHub repository for LibreNotes and create a basic prototype.
	* Share the idea on social media and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to Notion and Outline is increasing, and Nigerian students can benefit from a free, customizable note-taking app.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, using open-source resources).

	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team).
