After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores and brands pay for advertising and promotions.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and list potential partner stores and brands.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
	* Reach out to partner stores for initial discussions.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and the need for passive income, a cashback app can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with partners)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be solo)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (depends on partner acquisition and app development)

**2. Nigerian Culture Hub**

4. **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers, sponsors, and users who pay for premium content.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦1,000 per month (premium content)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and curate existing content (music, art, literature, etc.).
	* Develop a basic website and social media presence.
	* Reach out to cultural influencers and content creators for partnerships.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of cultural gatekeeping and the need for cultural preservation, a platform that showcases Nigerian culture can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for revenue from advertising and sponsorships)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated content curation and advertising)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be solo)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (depends on content curation and partner acquisition)
