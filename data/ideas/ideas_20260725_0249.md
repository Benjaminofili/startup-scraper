**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score recovery plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per month)
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing credit score recovery solutions and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the CreditPal app using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and the solution offered by CreditPal.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" are seeking help with credit score recovery, indicating a need for a solution like CreditPal.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing efforts may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
