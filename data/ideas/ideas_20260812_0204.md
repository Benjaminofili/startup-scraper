After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for a free and customizable note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations or premium features (₦500/month).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	2. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app with the development team.
	3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of remote work and online learning, note-taking and organization tools are in high demand.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low costs, open-source)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (donations, premium features)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (small team, solo-friendly)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (weeks to launch)

**2. Alternative to Google Photos**
* **IDEA NAME**: PhotoSafe
* **PROBLEM**: Users are concerned about data privacy and security with Google Photos, and are looking for end-to-end encrypted alternatives.
* **SOLUTION**: A photo and video storage app with end-to-end encryption, automatic backup, and sharing features.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and families concerned about data privacy (₦1,000/month or ₦50/GB).
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month or ₦50/GB.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing end-to-end encrypted photo storage solutions and identify key features to include.
	2. Set up a secure server using AWS free tier and start building the app with the development team.
	3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With growing concerns about data privacy, an alternative to Google Photos is timely and relevant.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (low costs, using AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team, some expertise required)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (weeks to launch)

**3. Rotten Tomatoes-Style Product Review Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: ReviewHub

* **SOLUTION**: A platform for users to review and rate durable products, with features like filtering, sorting, and searching.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses and manufacturers looking to promote their products (₦5,000/month or ₦1,000/product listing).
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month or ₦1,000/product listing.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing product review platforms and identify key features to include.
	2. Set up a database and start building the platform with the development team.
	3. Reach out to potential partners and businesses to gather interest and feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of e-commerce in Nigeria, a trustworthy product review platform is essential.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some costs, partnerships required)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (listing fees, advertising)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team, some expertise required)
	+ Time to Market: 5/10 (weeks to launch)

**5. LibreTaxi Alternative**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideSafe
* **PROBLEM**: Users are looking for free and open-source alternatives to Uber and Lyft, with a focus on safety and security.
* **SOLUTION**: A ride-hailing platform with features like real-time tracking, emergency alerts, and driver verification.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders (₦50-₦200 per ride) and drivers (₦1,000-₦5,000/month).
* **PRICE**: ₦50-₦200 per ride, ₦1,000-₦5,000/month for drivers.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing ride-hailing platforms and identify key features to include.
	2. Set up a development environment and start building the platform with the development team.
	3. Reach out to potential drivers and partners to gather interest and feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of ride-hailing in Nigeria, a safe and secure alternative is essential.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 5/10 (some costs, partnerships required)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (transaction fees)
	+ Team Size: 4/10 (small team, some expertise required)
	+ Time to Market: 4/10 (months to launch)

These ideas prioritize zero-capital, passive income opportunities, and focus on the Nigerian market. They also address real problems and pain points, increasing their potential for success.
