**1. Alternative to Google Products (Search Engine)**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaSearch
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google products due to privacy concerns and data ownership.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, privacy-focused search engine that indexes Nigerian websites and provides relevant results.
* **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers targeting the Nigerian market (₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per month)
* **PRICE**: Free for users, advertisers pay per click
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing open-source search engine projects (e.g., YaCy) and evaluate their feasibility for the Nigerian market.
	2. Set up a test server on AWS free tier to experiment with the search engine.
	3. Reach out to potential advertisers to gauge interest and discuss pricing.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for alternatives to Google products is increasing, and a search engine focused on the Nigerian market can capitalize on this trend.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses open-source software and existing infrastructure)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual maintenance and advertising sales)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed by a small team, but may require additional help for advertising sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with existing open-source software)
