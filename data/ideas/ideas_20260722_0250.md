After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Digital Credit Score Builder**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals recover from damaged credit scores, like the 25M user whose mother took their 700+ credit to a 580.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and alerts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly young adults (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing credit score models and APIs.
	2. Design a user-friendly mobile app interface.
	3. Develop a basic credit score tracking feature.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users are struggling with credit score issues, and a digital solution can provide convenience and accessibility.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (subscription-based model, but may require occasional updates)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 team members for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)
