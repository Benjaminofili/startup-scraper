**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Review**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LandingPageNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and frustrated customers.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based review and feedback platform for SaaS landing pages, providing actionable insights and suggestions for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: SaaS companies and entrepreneurs looking to improve their landing pages.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per review (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify potential customers and competitors.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic website using the AWS free tier and a simple review form.
	* Step 3: Reach out to 10 SaaS companies to offer free reviews in exchange for feedback and testimonials.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from a user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a review and feedback platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost website development)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (manual review process, but potential for automation)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed by 1-2 people)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (quick website development and launch)
