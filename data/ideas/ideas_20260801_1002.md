After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals need a free, open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for digital note-taking.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals who need a digital note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the needs of Nigerian students and professionals.
 2. Design a basic prototype of the app using existing open-source note-taking software.
 3. Create a social media campaign to gather feedback and interest from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and existing solutions like Notion or Outline may be too expensive for Nigerian users.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (using existing open-source software reduces development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (premium features can generate revenue, but may require manual support)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team of 2-3 people)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
