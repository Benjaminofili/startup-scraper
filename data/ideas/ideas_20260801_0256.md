Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking a free and customizable note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations (₦100-₦500 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a basic user interface and user experience (UI/UX) for the app.
	* Step 3: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are seeking alternatives to Notion and Outline, and an open-source solution can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (zero capital needed, using open-source libraries)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by a solo developer or a small team)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched in a few weeks with a basic version)

**2. Personal Finance Tracker**
1. **IDEA NAME**: FinancePal

3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to track their income, expenses, and budgets, with features like categorization, alerts, and financial insights.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking to manage their finances effectively, particularly those in the middle class.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500-₦1,000 per month ( subscription-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing personal finance apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly UI/UX for the app.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using a framework like React Native.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are seeking alternatives to traditional banking apps and spreadsheets for personal finance management.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched in a few months with a basic version)
