4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchases).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and develop a basic prototype using existing APIs.
	* Step 3: Test the app with a small group of users to gather feedback and iterate.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
