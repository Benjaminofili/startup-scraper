Based on the user complaints and problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps to earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on daily purchases, such as groceries and household items, from partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who make frequent purchases and want to earn cashback.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback.
 2. Develop a basic app using the AWS free tier and Android/web development skills.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, using AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with 3 developers and 1 marketer)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
