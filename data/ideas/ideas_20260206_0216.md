After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. RespectMe**
* **IDEA NAME**: RespectMe
* **PROBLEM**: Customers are dissatisfied with the poor customer service of electronic device retailers.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows customers to rate and review electronic device retailers, promoting accountability and better customer service.
* **WHO PAYS**: Retailers pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their services.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify target retailers and customers.
	2. Design a user-friendly app interface and rating system.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer's skills.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint on the Play Store about poor customer service highlights the need for a platform that promotes accountability and better customer service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage retailer subscriptions)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with the current team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)

* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per product listing
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing review platforms and identify areas for improvement.

	3. Develop a basic prototype using the web developer's skills.

* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with the current team, but may require additional help for content creation)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within days with a basic prototype)
