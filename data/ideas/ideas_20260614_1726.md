Based on the user complaints and problems presented, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cash back on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered stores, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back earned (₦50 per ₦1000 cash back).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cash back.
   - Develop a basic app using AWS free tier and Android/web APIs.
   - Create social media buzz using the marketer's social media skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, leveraging existing infrastructure)
   - Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system)
   - Team Size: 7/10 (small team, but needs some marketing and partnership efforts)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version quickly)
