Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for a free and customizable note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦0 - ₦1,000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include.
 2. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app with the development team.
 3. Create a social media page to gather feedback and interest from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of remote work and online learning, note-taking and organization have become essential skills, and an open-source solution can fill the gap.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 10/10 (₦0 needed, using existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be built with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched in a few weeks)
