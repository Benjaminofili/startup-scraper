After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are dissatisfied with current ride-hailing services like Uber, seeking alternatives.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform, connecting drivers and passengers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers pay per ride, while drivers pay a commission on each fare.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦1,000 per ride (depending on distance), 10% - 20% commission for drivers.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Set up a basic website and social media presence to promote LibreRide.
   - Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the AWS free tier.
   - Step 3: Recruit a few drivers and passengers to test the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the user complaint about Uber, there's an opportunity to create a better alternative.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier, minimal marketing budget)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and driver management)
   - Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members as it grows)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version quickly, but may require more time for full features)
