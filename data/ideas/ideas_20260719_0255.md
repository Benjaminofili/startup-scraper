After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to monitor and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those seeking loans or credit cards.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit tracking features.
	3. Partner with local financial institutions to access credit data and expertise.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and data access)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing development and support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature set may take longer)

**2. Digital Savings Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNaija
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money due to limited access to reliable and user-friendly savings platforms.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to set savings goals, track progress, and earn interest on their deposits.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking to save money and earn interest on their deposits.
* **PRICE**: ₦100/month or 2% interest on deposits
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing savings platforms in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a user-friendly interface and basic savings features.
	3. Partner with local banks or financial institutions to access secure payment processing.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of limited savings options is highlighted in the Reddit post "10 Ways to Save Money Passively (Best Passive Money Saving Apps/Sites 2018)".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, potential for partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (interest on deposits and subscription fees)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed for development and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, with full feature set following soon)

**3. Online Marketplace for Freelancers**
* **IDEA NAME**: GigNaija
* **PROBLEM**: Freelancers in Nigeria struggle to find reliable and well-paying gigs, while clients struggle to find skilled and trustworthy freelancers.
* **SOLUTION**: An online platform that connects freelancers with clients, offering a range of services from writing to web development.
* **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers (commission on earnings) and clients (service fees)
* **PRICE**: 10% commission on freelancer earnings, ₦1,000/service fee for clients
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing freelance platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic platform with user profiles, service listings, and payment processing.
	3. Reach out to local freelance communities and client networks to promote the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of unreliable freelance work is highlighted in the Reddit post "WTF is up with companies (esp. startups) expecting people to work for free?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (initial development costs, potential for revenue sharing)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (commission on freelancer earnings and service fees)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature set may take longer)

* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking financial advice and support (affiliate marketing, sponsored content)
* **PRICE**: Free (affiliate marketing and sponsored content)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Create a basic blog with personal finance content and resources.
	2. Establish a social media presence and online community.
	3. Reach out to local financial experts and influencers for contributions and partnerships.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of limited financial literacy is highlighted in the Reddit post "Who Paid You For/In April. Here is My List. And some thoughts about that oft men".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 10/10 (zero development costs, potential for affiliate marketing and sponsored content)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (affiliate marketing and sponsored content)
	+ Team Size: 4/10 ( solo-friendly, potential for guest contributors)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched immediately, with content and community building over time)

**5. E-paper Dashboard for Nigerian News and Events**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaNewsBoard
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to stay up-to-date with local news and events, particularly in rural areas with limited internet access.
* **SOLUTION**: An e-paper dashboard offering curated news, events, and resources for Nigerians.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses seeking to reach a targeted audience (advertising)
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (advertising) or free (basic news and events)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing news and event platforms in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic e-paper dashboard with curated news and events.
	3. Reach out to local news outlets and event organizers to partner on content and advertising.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of limited access to local news and events is highlighted in the HackerNews post "I built Timeframe, our family e-paper dashboard".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, potential for advertising revenue)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (advertising revenue, but requires ongoing content curation)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and content curation)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature set may take longer)
