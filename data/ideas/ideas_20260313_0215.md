After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic app prototype using Android Studio.
	* Step 3: Develop a content strategy for the app's recommendation feature.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic app and iterate).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual content curation).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 people).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks).

**2. LibreRide Nigeria**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide Nigeria
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable transportation options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting drivers with riders.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders (per transaction) and drivers (commission-based).
5. **PRICE**: ₦50-₦200 per ride (depending on distance).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing transportation options in Nigeria and identify potential partners.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic web platform for driver registration and ride requests.
	* Step 3: Design a mobile app for riders to request and track rides.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft" post on HackerNews shows interest in alternative transportation solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 5/10 (requires some initial investment for marketing and infrastructure).
	* Passive Income: 4/10 (requires manual management of drivers and riders).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of 2-3 people).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (may take months to launch and gain traction).
