After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback apps that offer significant rewards.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides cashback on everyday purchases, partnering with local businesses to offer exclusive deals.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 purchase).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
  2. Identify potential partner businesses in Nigeria.
  3. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web development skills.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for cashback apps is high, and the Nigerian market lacks a reliable and user-friendly option.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and partnerships may require some investment)
  - Passive Income: 7/10 (commission-based revenue, but some manual work required for partnerships and customer support)
  - Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the app, but partnerships and marketing may require additional help)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (a basic app can be launched quickly, but partnerships and marketing take time)
