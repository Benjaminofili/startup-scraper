**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income due to limited financial resources.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items, through partnerships with local retailers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, retailers, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research local retailers and potential partners.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
	* Create a social media campaign to raise awareness and generate interest.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing APIs).
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for partner management).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can start with solo founder).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch in weeks with a basic prototype).
