**1. DoomScrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling mindlessly on social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for productivity and mental well-being.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based model)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and user-friendly app interface using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web development skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and design may be required)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and design)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
