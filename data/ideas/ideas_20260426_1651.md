After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback apps that offer significant rewards.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates cashback offers from various online stores and provides users with a simple way to earn passive income.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Online shoppers, particularly students and young adults.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earnings (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing cashback apps and identify potential partners.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Reach out to online stores and negotiate commission rates.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but partnerships may require some investment)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback tracking and payout system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (launch can happen within weeks, but partnerships may take time)
