* **WHO PAYS**: App developers and companies (e.g., Nigerian banks, e-commerce platforms) with buggy apps.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per bug fix (transaction-based).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing bug tracking tools and identify the target market.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic bug reporting and feedback features.
 3. Reach out to local app developers and companies to offer BugFix services.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are frustrated with buggy apps, and developers need a reliable testing and feedback platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing resources and free tier AWS).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for bug fixing and feedback).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers for scaling).
