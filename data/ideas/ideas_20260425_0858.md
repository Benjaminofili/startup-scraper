**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste hours scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users to their excessive social media scrolling, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35 who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design templates.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic prototype using Android Studio and test with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing the need to reduce screen time and increase productivity, making this a timely solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
