**1.**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle with damaged credit scores and need guidance on improvement.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score monitoring, tips, and alerts for Nigerians.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores ( ₦500/month subscription)
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on credit score management in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier.
 3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about CreditPal.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score management.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work for customer support)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires at least 2 team members for development and marketing)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
