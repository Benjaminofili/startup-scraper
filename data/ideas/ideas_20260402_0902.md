After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. BugFix**
1. **IDEA NAME**: BugFix
2. **PROBLEM**: Many app users experience frustrating bugs and glitches, such as logging out issues, which can lead to a poor user experience.
3. **SOLUTION**: BugFix is a simple, user-friendly app testing and feedback platform that helps developers identify and fix bugs quickly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and small businesses with limited testing resources.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦1,000 per transaction (pay-per-test).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the app testing and feedback landscape in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as bug reporting and feedback mechanisms.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local app developers and small businesses to gather interest and feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the recent app update bug reported on the PlayStore, there's a clear need for a reliable app testing and feedback solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for testing and feedback)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional testers)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with an MVP)
