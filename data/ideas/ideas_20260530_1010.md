After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, and some have experienced a significant drop in their credit score due to others' actions.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and sends alerts for potential credit score drops.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with a medium to high credit score, particularly those who have experienced a drop in their credit score.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score tracking landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs for credit score tracking.
 3. Reach out to potential partners, such as credit bureaus, to discuss integration and data access.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are experiencing credit score drops due to others' actions, and there is a need for a personalized credit score tracking solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (existing APIs can be used, reducing development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can handle development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (a basic prototype can be developed within a few weeks)
