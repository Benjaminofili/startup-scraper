Based on the user complaints and problems presented, here are the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. Credit Score Manager**
* **IDEA NAME**: Credit Score Manager
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to limited access to loans and financial services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and improve their credit scores by providing personalized advice, credit monitoring, and alerts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or limited credit history, as well as financial institutions looking to offer credit services to a wider audience.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking and advice features.
 3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data and improve the app's accuracy.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but partnership with credit bureau may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can manage the app, but may require additional staff for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)

**2. Personal Finance Assistant**
* **IDEA NAME**: Personal Finance Assistant
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to manage their finances, leading to overspending and financial stress.
* **SOLUTION**: A chatbot-based personal finance assistant that helps users track expenses, create budgets, and set financial goals.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals looking to improve their financial management skills, as well as financial institutions offering personal finance services.
* **PRICE**: ₦200/month or ₦1,000/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current personal finance landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a chatbot MVP with basic expense tracking and budgeting features.
 3. Integrate with popular payment platforms to enable seamless transaction tracking.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of financial stress is highlighted in the Reddit post "My friend on SSI is now doing much better than I am"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs are low, and chatbot technology is widely available)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (chatbot can handle most customer interactions, but some manual work required for data updates)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can manage the chatbot, but may require additional staff for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few days with a basic MVP)

* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals buying and selling second-hand items, as well as businesses looking to reach a wider audience.
* **PRICE**: ₦100/listing or 5% commission per sale

 1. Conduct market research to understand the current second-hand market landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic MVP with product listing and review features.
 3. Partner with local logistics companies to enable secure and efficient delivery of second-hand items.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of finding reliable platforms for buying and selling second-hand items is highlighted in the Reddit post "If you're thinking of selling your old devices for money, think twice before using"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (initial development costs are moderate, and partnership with logistics companies may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for customer support and dispute resolution)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 3-4 people can manage the marketplace, but may require additional staff for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)
