After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who are concerned about their social media usage.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as screen time tracking, personalized recommendations, and reminders.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their screen time.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscriptions can generate passive income, but user engagement and retention are crucial)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can develop and maintain the app, but additional help may be needed for marketing and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (an MVP can be launched within a few weeks, but further development and refinement may take longer)

**2. Freelance Work Platform**

1. **IDEA NAME**: FreelanceNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Many freelancers in Nigeria struggle to find clients and get paid fairly for their work.
3. **SOLUTION**: A platform that connects freelancers with clients, providing a secure payment system and a rating system to ensure quality work.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers and clients (businesses and individuals) looking for freelance services.
5. **PRICE**: 10% commission on each transaction (freelancers pay ₦1,000 per transaction, clients pay ₦1,500 per transaction)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing freelance platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic platform using the web developer on the team, focusing on user registration, job posting, and payment processing.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential clients and freelancers to test the platform and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The freelance market is growing, and a platform that addresses the specific needs of Nigerian freelancers and clients can fill a significant gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs are low, and the platform can generate revenue through commissions)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (the platform can generate passive income through commissions, but user engagement and retention are crucial)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can develop and maintain the platform, but additional help may be needed for marketing, customer support, and moderation)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (an MVP can be launched within a few weeks, but further development and refinement may take longer)
