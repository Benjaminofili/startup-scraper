After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. BudgetPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: BudgetPal
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to manage their finances and stick to a budget, leading to financial stress and difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track their expenses, create a budget, and set financial goals, with features like automatic expense categorization and personalized financial recommendations.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians, students, and young professionals who want to manage their finances effectively.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's financial pain points and behaviors.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features like expense tracking and budgeting.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and gather feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing financial struggles, budgeting, and saving, indicating a need for a personalized financial management tool.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)
