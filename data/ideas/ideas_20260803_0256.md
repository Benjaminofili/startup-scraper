After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with organizing their notes and finding a suitable alternative to Notion or Outline.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with tagging, searching, and organization features.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals in Nigeria, particularly those in universities and colleges.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the note-taking habits of Nigerian students and professionals.
 2. Design a basic prototype of the app using existing open-source note-taking software.
 3. Create a social media campaign to gather feedback and interest from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and existing alternatives like Notion or Outline may not be affordable or suitable for the Nigerian market.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can be built using open-source software and minimal capital)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team of 2-3 people)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
