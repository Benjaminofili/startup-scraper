* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides cash back rewards on everyday purchases, such as groceries, laundry, and other essentials.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cash back rewards.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify popular cash back reward programs in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the app using the Android developer.
	3. Partner with local businesses to offer cash back rewards to users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, making this a timely opportunity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships)

	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch within weeks with an MVP)
