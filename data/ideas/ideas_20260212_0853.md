After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals need a free, open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile and web app for note-taking, organization, and collaboration, with features like tagging, searching, and offline access.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for a free, customizable note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations or sponsored features (₦0 - ₦500/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing open-source note-taking apps and their features.
	* Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing frameworks.
	* Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and NoteHub can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (₦0 needed, using open-source frameworks and free resources).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and sponsored features can generate revenue).
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built and maintained by a small team).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (requires some development time, but can be launched in a few weeks).

**2. Personal Finance Tracker**

3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly mobile app for tracking expenses, creating budgets, and setting financial goals.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses looking to manage their finances better.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features (₦500 - ₦2,000/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing personal finance apps and their features.
	* Design a user-friendly interface and core features.
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) for testing and feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the increasing use of mobile devices in Nigeria, a well-designed finance app can fill a significant need.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, using free resources and existing frameworks).
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (premium features and ads can generate revenue).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be built and maintained by a small team).
	* Time to Market: 5/10 (requires some development time, but can be launched in a few weeks).
