After analyzing the problems and complaints from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle with poor credit scores, which affect their financial stability.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and provides tips to improve credit scores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1,500/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system.
	2. Develop a basic prototype using the AWS free tier.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" highlights the need for credit score management.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier, minimal development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires periodic updates and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
