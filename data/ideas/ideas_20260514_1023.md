**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users when they've exceeded a certain amount of time on social media, encouraging them to take breaks and focus on their tasks.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, focusing on simplicity and ease of use.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web development skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed, using existing AWS free tier resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
