After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back rewards (e.g., ₦50 cash back on a ₦1000 purchase).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cash back rewards.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, but some costs for marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partnerships and customer support).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the app, but more help may be needed for partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but more features and partnerships will take time).
