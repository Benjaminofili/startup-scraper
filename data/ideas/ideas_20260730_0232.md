After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are dissatisfied with existing ride-hailing services like Uber, seeking alternatives with better pricing and service.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform connecting drivers and passengers, with features like real-time tracking and in-app payment.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers and drivers (commission-based)
5. **PRICE**: ₦50-₦100 per transaction (commission)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features, using the AWS free tier.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are seeking alternatives to Uber, and a free and open-source platform can capitalize on this demand.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source technology and AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (commission-based, but requires ongoing maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers and marketers as the platform grows)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP quickly, but may require several months to refine and scale)
