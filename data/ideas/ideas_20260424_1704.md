After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app, as well as partner stores who pay a commission for each sale made through the platform.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases, with a minimum payout of ₦1,000.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cashback rewards.
 2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, a cashback platform can help consumers save money and encourage more purchases.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partnerships and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the platform, but more staff may be needed as it grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic app and partnerships)

**2. SaveNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to save money due to a lack of discipline and limited access to savings tools.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money by setting financial goals and automatically deducting a fixed amount from their account each month.
* **WHO PAYS**: Users who set up a savings plan and pay a small monthly fee.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month for the basic savings plan.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the savings habits of Nigerians.
 2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
 3. Partner with a local bank to enable automatic deductions.
* **WHY NOW**: With the increasing awareness of financial literacy in Nigeria, a savings app can help users develop healthy savings habits.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low initial investment required)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (automated deductions and minimal manual work)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic app)
