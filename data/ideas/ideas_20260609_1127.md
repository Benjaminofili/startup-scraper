After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
2. **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to maintain good credit scores, especially when others have access to their credit information.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score monitoring, alerts, and personalized advice to help users improve their credit scores.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the current credit score landscape in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score monitoring features.
	* Step 3: Partner with a credit bureau to access credit data.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial investment required for development and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team of 2-3 developers and 1 marketer)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (launch possible within 3-6 months)
