After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
2. **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score recovery plans and connects users with financial advisors.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per month).
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦5,000 per month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with financial advisors to offer expert advice.
   - Develop a basic mobile app with a user-friendly interface.
   - Create a marketing campaign targeting individuals with damaged credit scores.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" are seeking help to recover from damaged credit scores.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 6/10 (some investment needed for marketing and partnerships)
   - Passive Income: 4/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 team members needed for development and marketing)
   - Time to Market: 6/10 (2-3 months to launch)

**5. LibreTaxiNG**
