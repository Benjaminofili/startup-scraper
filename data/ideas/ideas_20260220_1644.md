**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
2. **PROBLEM**: Customers feel disrespected by companies, and there's no easy way to provide feedback.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review companies based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Companies will pay to access feedback and improve their customer service.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for basic feedback reports.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer complaints.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface for customers to leave feedback.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic feedback reporting system for companies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about bad service and disrespect for customers highlights the need for a platform like RespectMeter.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some marketing budget)

	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and sales)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
