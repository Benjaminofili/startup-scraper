After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Manager**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and improve their credit scores by providing personalized advice and alerts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or limited credit history, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features such as credit score tracking and alerts.
	3. Partner with financial institutions to access credit data and provide more accurate scores.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, as seen in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential partnerships can reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, but more advanced features may take months)

**2. Personal Finance Assistant**
* **IDEA NAME**: FinanceFriend
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to manage their finances, leading to overspending and debt.
* **SOLUTION**: A chatbot-based personal finance assistant that provides users with personalized budgeting advice and expense tracking.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-40 who are active on social media and interested in personal finance.
* **PRICE**: ₦200/month or ₦1,500/year
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Develop a basic chatbot using existing platforms like Dialogflow or ManyChat.
	2. Integrate the chatbot with popular messaging apps like WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger.
	3. Create a content calendar to provide users with regular financial tips and advice.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to manage their finances, as seen in the Reddit post "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back (Best Passive Saving Apps 2020)"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, can use existing platforms)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can automate many tasks, but some manual work required for content creation)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a solo founder, but may require additional hires for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch a basic chatbot within days)
