**1.** **[IDEA NAME]**: CashBackNG
**PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or offline.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
**WHO PAYS**: Consumers, through a percentage of their cash back earnings.
**PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back earnings (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cash back).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with local stores to offer cash back.
	2. Develop a basic app with a user-friendly interface.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
**WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and CashBackNG fills this gap.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for app development and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
