**1. RideHailFix**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideHailFix
* **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing services often fail to deliver on scheduled pickups, causing frustration for users.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple app that allows users to report and track no-shows, providing a rating system for drivers and incentivizing reliable service.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) could pay for access to the ratings and user feedback.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (for ride-hailing companies)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to gauge interest in the solution.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about the unreliability of ride-hailing services, and this solution addresses a specific pain point.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for user support and ratings moderation)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional staff for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
