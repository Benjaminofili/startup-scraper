After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer's skills.
	* Step 3: Create a social media marketing campaign to raise awareness about the app's benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints about doomscrolling and the need for alternatives to social media addiction make this a timely solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal capital needed)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for coaching and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts may require additional help)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused effort)

**2. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable transportation options, leading to frustration and wasted time.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers and promoting community-driven transportation.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders who use the platform, with optional tips for drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use, with optional tips)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing transportation options in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic platform using the web developer's skills, integrating with existing mapping services.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local driver communities to gauge interest and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints about expensive transportation options and the success of LibreTaxi make this a promising opportunity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing resources, minimal capital needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through tips and advertising)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to develop and maintain the platform)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (may take a few months to launch, depending on the complexity of the platform)
