* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	2. Design a user-friendly app interface and alert system.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is mentioned in the HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass", indicating a need for a solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for app development and marketing).

	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a solo developer, but marketing and support may require additional team members).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).
