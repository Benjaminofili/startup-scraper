**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the AWS free tier.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, leveraging AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback is a passive income stream)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and app development)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic app)
