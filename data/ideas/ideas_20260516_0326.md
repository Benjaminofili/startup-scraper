**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for productivity and mental well-being.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are frequent social media users.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (freemium model with in-app purchases).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app, focusing on simplicity and user-friendliness.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about social media addiction, making this a timely solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with the existing team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to stay up-to-date with current events, news, and information due to limited access to physical newspapers.

4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses looking for a convenient and affordable way to stay informed.
5. **PRICE**: ₦200 per month (basic plan) or ₦500 per month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 3: Develop a basic MVP using the APIs developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "I built Timeframe, our family e-paper dashboard" post on HackerNews shows interest in digital newspaper solutions, making this a relevant opportunity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and web developer)

	* Team Size: 9/10 (can be developed with the existing team)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
