2. **PROBLEM**: Users are concerned about the security and privacy of their photos on Google Photos.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides end-to-end encrypted photo storage and sharing.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses concerned about data privacy (e.g., ₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦2,000 per month (depending on storage capacity).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing open-source encryption solutions (e.g., LibrePhotos).
	* Set up a basic infrastructure using AWS free tier.
	* Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternatives to Google Photos due to privacy concerns, and the team can leverage the AWS free tier to launch quickly.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source solutions and AWS free tier).
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for customer support).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires at least 2-3 team members for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks).
