After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all transactions (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps and their features.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
 3. Reach out to local businesses to partner for cashback offers.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, making this a timely solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months).

* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing savings platforms and their features.
 2. Design a secure and user-friendly interface for the app.

* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are looking for ways to save money and earn interest, making this a timely solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (automated interest payments).
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within a few months).
