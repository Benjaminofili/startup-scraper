After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a simple user interface.
	* Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive cashback deals.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated, with potential for high earnings).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but requires some marketing and partnership efforts).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (weeks to launch, depending on MVP development and partnerships).
