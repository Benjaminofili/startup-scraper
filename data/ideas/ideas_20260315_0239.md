**1. RideHail Feedback**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideHail Feedback
2. **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers often reject rides, leaving passengers stranded and frustrated.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows passengers to rate and provide feedback on their ride-hailing experiences, promoting accountability and better service.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) can pay for access to feedback data to improve their services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for access to feedback data.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the pain points of ride-hailing passengers and drivers.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly mobile app for passengers to provide feedback.
	* Step 3: Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a solution to address the poor service quality of ride-hailing drivers.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, potential for partnerships)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for data analysis and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (small team can handle development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible)
