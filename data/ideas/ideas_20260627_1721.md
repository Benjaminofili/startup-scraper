After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (₦50 per transaction)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app with the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign with the marketer to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for marketing and app development)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
