After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback rewards.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, a cashback platform can help users save money and incentivize purchases.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partnerships and customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with the current team)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires some development and testing)
