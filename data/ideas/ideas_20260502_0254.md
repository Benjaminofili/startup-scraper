**1. DoomScroll**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScroll
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month ( subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) for the app, focusing on core features such as usage tracking and personalized recommendations.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of excessive social media scrolling is timely, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic MVP and iterate)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)

2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face challenges with transportation, including high costs and limited options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers and providing a more affordable and community-driven transportation solution.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders who use the platform, with optional tips for drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use, with optional tips)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing transportation solutions in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic platform for connecting riders and drivers, using open-source technologies.
	* Step 3: Establish partnerships with local driver associations and communities to promote the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The idea is inspired by the "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft" post, and Nigeria's transportation challenges make it a timely opportunity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
