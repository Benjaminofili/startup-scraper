Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users when they've spent too much time scrolling through social media, providing tips and resources to help them manage their time more effectively.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and user-friendly app interface using existing design templates.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio and AWS free tier services.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to manage their social media addiction (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Platform**

2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with the high costs of ride-hailing services, and existing alternatives are often unreliable or unsafe.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, using a peer-to-peer model and community-driven moderation.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform is free, with optional donations or tips for drivers)
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform using web and API development skills.
	* Step 3: Establish a community of drivers and passengers to test and provide feedback on the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The need for affordable and reliable transportation is pressing, and users are looking for alternatives to existing ride-hailing services (e.g., "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, using existing open-source resources)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (some manual work required for community moderation and updates)

	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months with a basic version)
