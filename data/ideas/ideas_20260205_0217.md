After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback reward on every purchase (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback rewards.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are looking for ways to earn passive cashback rewards, and CashBackNG can fill this gap in the Nigerian market.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some costs for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated, but some manual work for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with the current team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but depends on partnership negotiations)

**2. SaveNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and need a platform to help them save passively.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that helps users save money passively by rounding up their purchases and investing the spare change.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to save money.
* **PRICE**: 1% monthly fee on savings (₦50 per month).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Develop a basic web and mobile app using the web and Android developers on the team.
	2. Integrate a payment gateway to enable users to link their bank accounts.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
