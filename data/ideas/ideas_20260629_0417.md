After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market with zero-capital and passive income potential. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on salvage grocery stores and other local retailers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local salvage grocery stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed for marketing and app development)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback earnings are passive)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and app development)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
