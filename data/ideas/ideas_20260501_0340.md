Based on the user complaints and problems presented, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback and rewards platforms for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback and rewards for users who shop at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores and brands pay for advertising and commission on sales.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on sales (₦50 per transaction, on average).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback rewards.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships).

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but may require months to refine and expand).
