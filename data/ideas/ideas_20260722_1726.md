After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1.** **CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to find reliable and rewarding cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	2. Develop a basic app using Android and web developers.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but no significant capital required)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partner management and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic app)
