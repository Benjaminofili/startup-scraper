**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling mindlessly on social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦500-₦1,000 per month).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 (monthly subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing solutions and identify unique features.
	* Design a basic UI/UX for the app.
	* Develop a prototype using Android and AWS free tier resources.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about wasting time on social media (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal costs).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for support and updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks).
