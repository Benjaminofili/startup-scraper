After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimal viable product (MVP) using Android Studio.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to stay focused (e.g., "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing Android development skills and AWS free tier).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires occasional updates and maintenance).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months).

**2. Product Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: ReviewHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Consumers in Nigeria struggle to find reliable product reviews, leading to poor purchasing decisions.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web platform that aggregates user reviews for various products, similar to Rotten Tomatoes.
4. **WHO PAYS**: E-commerce businesses and product manufacturers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (listing fee) or ₦500 per transaction (affiliate marketing).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing review platforms and identify gaps in the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic web scraper to collect product data from e-commerce sites.
	* Step 3: Design a user-friendly interface for users to leave reviews.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for reliable product reviews is high, and a platform like ReviewHub can fill this gap (e.g., "Show HN: I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing web development skills and AWS free tier).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through affiliate marketing).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires 2-3 team members for development and maintenance).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within 3-4 months).

**3. LibreTaxi Alternative**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to find affordable and reliable transportation options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, similar to Uber or Lyft, but with a focus on affordability and community engagement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers and drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride ( commission-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing platforms in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic driver and passenger app using Android and web technologies.
	* Step 3: Establish partnerships with local driver associations and transportation companies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable transportation is high, and a platform like RideNaija can provide a community-driven alternative (e.g., "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 5/10 (requires some initial investment for marketing and driver acquisition).
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (requires ongoing maintenance and customer support).
	* Team Size: 4/10 (requires 3-4 team members for development, marketing, and operations).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within 4-6 months).
