5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing open-source resources.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential users for feedback and testing.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them stay focused (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
