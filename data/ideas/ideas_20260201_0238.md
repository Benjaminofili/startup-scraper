**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct a survey among Nigerian students to understand their social media habits and gather feedback on the app idea.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer's skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work required)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional design help)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. Nigerian Product Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to find trustworthy reviews of local products.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web platform that allows users to review and rate Nigerian products, with a focus on durability and quality.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and manufacturers of Nigerian products.
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month (business listing), ₦500/month (premium user features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing review platforms and identify the unique selling points for NaijaReviews.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic web platform using the web developer's skills.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local businesses and manufacturers to list their products on the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products" post on HackerNews shows interest in review platforms for products.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing resources, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (business listings, premium user features)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional content creators)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires content creation)
