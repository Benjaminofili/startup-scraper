After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditScoreNG
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to maintain a good credit score, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and alerts users to potential credit risks.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) with existing credit histories.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100/transaction (credit report requests).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system.
	2. Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a minimum viable product (MVP).
	3. Partner with local credit bureaus to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score management.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential partnerships can reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 team members can handle development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within 2-3 months)
