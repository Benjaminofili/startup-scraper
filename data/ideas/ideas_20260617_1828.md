**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all transactions (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify popular stores and products in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Partner with local stores to offer cashback rewards.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with a basic app).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback rewards can be automated).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for partnerships).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks).
