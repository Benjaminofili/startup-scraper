After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, and some have experienced a significant drop in their credit score due to others' actions.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and provides tips to improve credit scores, with alerts for suspicious activity.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with a stable income, aged 25-45, who want to maintain a good credit score (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score tracking landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
 3. Partner with a credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of credit score maintenance is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to improve their credit scores.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income).
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing maintenance).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team, but requires expertise in credit scoring and app development).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few months).
