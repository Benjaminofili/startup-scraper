**1. CashBackNG**

2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money due to limited financial resources.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered with local businesses.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research local businesses for partnerships (Day 1-2).
	* Develop a basic app prototype (Day 3-4).
	* Launch a social media campaign to gather user interest (Day 5-7).
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with businesses).

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, with continuous improvement).
