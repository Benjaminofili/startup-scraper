After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to maintain good credit scores due to lack of financial literacy and poor credit management.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score tracking, financial education, and alerts for credit score changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking to improve their credit scores, particularly those applying for loans or credit cards.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit management is evident in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential partnerships can reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, but more complex features may take longer)
