After analyzing the user complaints and problems from various sources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback rewards.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the existing Android developer.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and the need for passive income, a cashback app can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and track their expenses.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track their expenses, set savings goals, and earn interest on their savings.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers (premium features) and banks (partnership fees).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand user needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the existing Android developer.
	* Step 3: Partner with a local bank to offer interest on savings.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the increasing awareness of financial literacy, a savings app can help Nigerians manage their finances better.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some initial investment for development and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (interest on savings and premium features)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within a few months)
