**1. Customer Respect Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: Customer Respect Tracker
* **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service is a common issue in Nigeria, and customers have no platform to rate and review the service quality of businesses.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review the service quality of businesses, helping others make informed decisions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses will pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their service quality.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most common customer service pain points in Nigeria.
	2. Design a simple and user-friendly app interface.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer on the team.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about poor customer service highlights the need for a platform to hold businesses accountable.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, existing Play Store account)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
