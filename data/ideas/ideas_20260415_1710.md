Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps for online shopping.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases made through partner merchants.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Online shoppers in Nigeria, particularly students and young adults.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local online merchants (e.g., Jumia, Konga).
	* Develop a basic app using AWS free tier and Android/web development skills.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing infrastructure).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (small team, but requires marketing and merchant partnerships).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick launch possible with existing development skills).
