After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their features.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier services.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, using AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with existing resources)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
