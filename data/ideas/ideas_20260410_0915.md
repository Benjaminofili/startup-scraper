**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste hours scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35 who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to help them manage their screen time.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month ( subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to promote the app on social media platforms.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" indicates a growing interest in solutions to mitigate excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be developed with existing resources and APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for content creation and marketing)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
