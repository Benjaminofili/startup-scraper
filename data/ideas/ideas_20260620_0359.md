After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **CreditPal**
* **PROBLEM**: Poor credit score management and lack of financial literacy.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score tracking, financial education, and alerts for credit score changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor credit scores or those seeking to improve their financial literacy.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦0 (basic features with ads).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Partner with financial institutions to offer exclusive discounts to users.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit are struggling with poor credit scores, and there's a need for a user-friendly platform to manage and improve credit scores.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (premium features and ads can generate revenue)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be solo)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full features may take time)
