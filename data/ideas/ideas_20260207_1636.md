Based on the user complaints and feedback, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **EduReview**
* **PROBLEM**: Students struggle to find reliable reviews of educational products and services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows students to rate and review educational products, such as textbooks, online courses, and tutoring services.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, schools, and educational institutions (through advertising and sponsored reviews).
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (for premium features) or ₦100 per review (for businesses).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify popular educational products and services in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Reach out to 10 educational institutions to gather feedback and interest in the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about "bad service" on the Play Store and the need for alternatives to Google Products (on HackerNews) highlight the demand for reliable reviews and educational resources.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with a basic app prototype)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional developers for scaling)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)

**2.** **NaijaTaxi**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and affordable transportation options.
* **SOLUTION**: A ride-hailing app that connects passengers with verified drivers, similar to LibreTaxi (mentioned on HackerNews).
* **WHO PAYS**: Passengers (through ride fares) and drivers (through commission fees).
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (minimum fare) or 10% commission per ride (for drivers).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android and web developers on the team.
	3. Reach out to 10 potential drivers to gather feedback and interest in the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The mention of LibreTaxi on HackerNews and the need for alternatives to Uber and Lyft highlight the demand for affordable and reliable transportation options.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some capital needed for marketing and driver verification)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through commission fees)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of developers, marketers, and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (may require several months to launch and scale)
