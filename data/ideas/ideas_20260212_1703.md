After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app for Android and web, with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals who need a reliable note-taking app.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦100-500 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
   - Step 2: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
   - Step 3: Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of remote learning and work, note-taking apps are in high demand, and an open-source alternative can fill a gap in the market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 10/10 (no initial investment needed)
   - Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
   - Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by a small team)
   - Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a minimal viable product)

4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses who want to improve their customer service and increase visibility.
5. **PRICE**: Free for customers, ₦5,000-10,000 per month for businesses to claim and manage their listings.
