**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and small businesses in Nigeria who need a cost-effective note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations (₦500-₦1,000 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a basic user interface and user experience for the app.
	* Step 3: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps like Notion is increasing, and a note-taking app can cater to the needs of Nigerian students and professionals.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no initial investment needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by a solo developer or a small team)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
