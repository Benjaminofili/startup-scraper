**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, focusing on simplicity and ease of use.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio, integrating APIs for social media tracking.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires ongoing development)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a small team, but requires marketing efforts)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, with a basic version)

4. **WHO PAYS**: None (free for users, relying on donations and community support).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free, with optional donations).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

7. **WHY NOW**: The need for affordable transportation options is pressing, and users are looking for alternatives to expensive ride-hailing services, as seen in the "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft" post.
