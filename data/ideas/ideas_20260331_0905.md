**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with apps that log them out and don't allow them to log back in due to bugs.
3. **SOLUTION**: An app that automatically logs users back into their accounts after a bug or crash.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and users who experience frequent logouts.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month or ₦50 per transaction.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing solutions and identify the most common logout bugs.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer.
	* Step 3: Test the prototype with a small group of users and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The most recent app update on the Play Store has a bug that logs users out, indicating a need for a solution like AutoLogin.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal additional investment needed)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with the existing team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
