After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreNotes**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features similar to Notion or Outline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free), with optional donations or premium features.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and their features.
	* Step 2: Create a basic prototype using existing frameworks or libraries.
	* Step 3: Share the prototype on social media and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many users are seeking alternatives to expensive note-taking apps, and the Nigerian market is ripe for a free, open-source solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no capital needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations or premium features can generate income)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic prototype)

**2. NaijaRadar**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaRadar
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a reliable platform to track and discover local events, news, and opportunities.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that aggregates local events, news, and opportunities, with features for users to submit and share content.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Local event organizers, businesses, and individuals seeking to promote their events or services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 (monthly subscription) or ₦100 (per event listing).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing event listing platforms and their features.
	* Step 2: Create a basic prototype using existing frameworks or libraries.
	* Step 3: Partner with local event organizers to populate the platform with initial content.
7. **WHY NOW**: Many Nigerians are seeking ways to stay informed about local events and opportunities, and a platform like NaijaRadar can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low capital needed for initial development)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (subscription and listing fees can generate income)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
