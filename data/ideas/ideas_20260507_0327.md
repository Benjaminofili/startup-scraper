**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media without realizing it.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to be more productive.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android Studio and integrate a timer to track screen time.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about social media addiction.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be built with existing resources)

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)
