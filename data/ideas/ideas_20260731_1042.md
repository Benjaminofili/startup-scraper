**1. SafeRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SafeRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are experiencing traumatic experiences with ride-hailing services like Uber.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to rate and review their ride-hailing experiences, providing a safer and more trustworthy alternative.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies and users who want to access premium features (e.g., verified drivers, enhanced safety features).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦50/transaction (verified driver fees).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian ride-hailing market and user concerns.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the SafeRide app using the AWS free tier.
	* Step 3: Reach out to ride-hailing companies and potential users to gather feedback and validate the idea.
7. **WHY NOW**: Recent traumatic experiences with Uber (mid-July 2026) highlight the need for a safer alternative.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for user support and moderation)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (small team can manage, but may require additional help for marketing and user acquisition)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch MVP within weeks)

3. **SOLUTION**: A Nigerian-focused, open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers and passengers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Drivers (commission-based) and passengers (optional tips).
5. **PRICE**: 10% commission per transaction (drivers) or ₦0 (passengers).
