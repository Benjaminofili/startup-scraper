Based on the user complaints and problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps that offer significant rewards.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make online transactions and want to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE**: 5% - 10% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 - ₦100 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps in Nigeria and identify gaps in the market.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features and a user-friendly interface.
 3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive cashback deals.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and digital payments in Nigeria, there's a growing demand for cashback apps that offer significant rewards.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards and commission-based revenue)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a team, but can be maintained by a solo founder)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched within weeks, but full features may take months)
