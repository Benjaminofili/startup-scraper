**1. [IDEA NAME]: PricePal**
**PROBLEM**: Users struggle to find affordable prices for groceries and other essential items.
**SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that aggregates prices of various products from different stores, allowing users to compare and find the best deals.
**WHO PAYS**: Individual consumers, particularly low- to middle-income households.
**PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦50/per transaction (basic features).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular stores and products in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic web scraper to collect price data from online stores.
	3. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
**WHY NOW**: The user complaints about expensive groceries and the desire for affordable alternatives make this a timely solution.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for data collection and marketing).
