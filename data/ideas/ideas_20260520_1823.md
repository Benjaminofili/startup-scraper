**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste hours scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month ( subscription-based model)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as screen time tracking, personalized recommendations, and reminders.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web development skills, and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is highlighted in the HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass", indicating a need for a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing development and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but requires testing and iteration)
