After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Review Service**

1. **IDEA NAME**: LandingPageNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and wasted marketing efforts.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based review and feedback service for SaaS landing pages, providing actionable suggestions for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: SaaS companies and entrepreneurs in Nigeria and beyond.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per review (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Develop a basic website using the AWS free tier and a simple web framework.
	* Step 2: Create a review template and a feedback system.
	* Step 3: Promote the service on social media and relevant online communities.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from a user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a review and feedback service.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost website development)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (manual review work required)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed by 1-2 people)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (quick website development and launch)

6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 2: Design a simple algorithm to provide personalized recommendations.
	* Step 3: Launch a minimum viable product (MVP) and gather user feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about poor customer service from a Samsung store highlights the need for a reliable product review platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low-cost web development)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based revenue)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (requires 2-3 people for development and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (moderate development time)
