3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research on existing solutions and identify key features.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using Android and web technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing the negative effects of excessive social media usage, indicating a growing need for solutions like Doomscrolling Detector.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
