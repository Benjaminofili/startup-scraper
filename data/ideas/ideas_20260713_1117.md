**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, especially after experiencing a significant drop.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) with a stable income, concerned about their credit scores.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (premium plan) or ₦500/credit report (one-time payment)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and existing solutions.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking and educational resources.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data and provide accurate reports.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and data access)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, with continuous improvement and iteration)
