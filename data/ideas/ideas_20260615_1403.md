After analyzing the user complaints and considering the Nigerian market, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a reliable platform to earn passive cashback rewards.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards to users for their daily purchases.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Local businesses (merchants) pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission per transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 5 local businesses in Lagos.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using the AWS free tier.
	* Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" indicates a demand for cashback rewards, and the Nigerian market lacks a reliable platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for business development and app maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch within weeks with a basic prototype)
