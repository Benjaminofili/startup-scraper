**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Review**
* **IDEA NAME**: LandingPageNaija
* **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and frustrated customers.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based review and feedback platform for SaaS landing pages, providing constructive criticism and suggestions for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: SaaS companies and entrepreneurs looking to improve their landing pages.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per review (one-time payment).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify potential customers and competitors.
	2. Develop a basic website using AWS free tier and a simple review form.
	3. Reach out to 10 SaaS companies to offer free reviews and gather feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint from a user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a review platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low-cost website development)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (manual review work required)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (quick website development and launch)
