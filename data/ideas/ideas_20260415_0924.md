**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users when they exceed a certain amount of time spent on social media, encouraging them to take breaks and focus on more important tasks.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly common, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some costs for marketing and maintenance)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
