**1. [IDEA NAME] CreditPal**
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals recover from damaged credit scores caused by others.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit monitoring, alerts, and personalized advice to improve credit scores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit monitoring features.
 3. Partner with a credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about damaged credit scores, and there's a need for a personalized credit recovery solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment required for marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and credit advice).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP).
