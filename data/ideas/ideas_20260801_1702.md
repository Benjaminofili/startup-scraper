**1. Digital Note-Taking App (Open-Source Alternative to Notion)**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free, open-source, and user-friendly note-taking app to organize their notes and ideas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile and web app that allows users to create, edit, and sync notes across devices, with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses in Nigeria who need a reliable note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and user experience for the app.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using existing open-source libraries and frameworks.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and an open-source alternative to Notion can fill the gap in the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, using existing open-source libraries)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be built by a small team of 2-3 developers)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
