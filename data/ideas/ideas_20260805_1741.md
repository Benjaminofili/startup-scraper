After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students and professionals need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for a cost-effective note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	2. Design a basic user interface and user experience for the app.
	3. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
* **WHY NOW**: With the increasing popularity of digital note-taking and the need for affordable alternatives, NoteHub can fill the gap in the Nigerian market.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing and maintenance)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (premium features and donations can generate revenue, but may require manual effort)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers can maintain and update the app)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic feature set)
