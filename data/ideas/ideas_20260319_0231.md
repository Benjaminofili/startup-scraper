Based on the user complaints and the team's resources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, using existing social media APIs to track usage.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app, focusing on tracking and alerting features.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are complaining about wasting time on social media, and there's a growing concern about mental health and productivity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (using existing social media APIs and free development tools)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires marketing efforts)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, with a basic version)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable transportation options, especially in urban areas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform, connecting drivers with passengers, using a peer-to-peer model.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers and drivers, with a small commission per ride (₦50-₦100).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use, with optional premium features for drivers).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing platforms in Nigeria, identifying areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform, using open-source software and APIs.
	* Step 3: Recruit a small group of drivers and passengers to test the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on HackerNews are discussing alternative transportation options, and there's a growing demand for affordable and reliable ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (using open-source software and free development tools)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (commission-based, but requires a large user base)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and driver recruitment)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, with a basic version)
