After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. RideShare Price Checker**
* **PROBLEM**: Inconsistent prices for ride-hailing services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that compares prices across different ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing users (individuals and businesses).
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦50/per transaction.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the price comparison app.
	3. Reach out to potential partners (ride-hailing companies) to discuss integration.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about inconsistent prices, and a price comparison app can help them make informed decisions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, potential for partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (app sales and advertising revenue).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team, potential for solo development).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible).

**2. Nigerian Freelance Platform**
* **PROBLEM**: Limited access to freelance work opportunities for Nigerians.
* **SOLUTION**: A platform connecting Nigerian freelancers with clients (individuals and businesses).
* **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers (listing fees) and clients (project fees).
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month (freelancer listing fee) or 10% commission per project.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing freelance platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic platform prototype with key features (user profiles, project listings, payment gateway).
	3. Reach out to potential freelancers and clients to test the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternative income sources, and a freelance platform can provide opportunities.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (moderate development costs, potential for revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (commission-based revenue).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team, potential for solo development).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (moderate development time).

* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing personal finance apps and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic prototype with key features (expense tracking, budgeting, goal setting).
	3. Conduct user testing to refine the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to manage their finances effectively, and a personal finance app can help.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, potential for revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (app sales and subscription revenue).
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (small team, potential for solo development).
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (quick development and launch possible).

**4. Nigerian Business Directory**
* **PROBLEM**: Limited access to information about Nigerian businesses.
* **SOLUTION**: An online directory listing Nigerian businesses, including their services, prices, and reviews.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses (listing fees) and users (premium features).
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/year (business listing fee) or ₦1,000/month (premium features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing business directories and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic prototype with key features (business listings, search functionality, reviews).
	3. Reach out to businesses to list their information.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to find and review Nigerian businesses, and a directory can provide valuable information.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (moderate development costs, potential for revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (listing fees and premium features).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team, potential for solo development).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (moderate development time).
