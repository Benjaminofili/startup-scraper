**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app, focusing on simplicity and user-friendliness.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer's skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing Android developer skills and AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires marketing effort)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional design and marketing support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, depending on the complexity of the app)
