After analyzing the user complaints and considering the Nigerian market, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and expertise. Here are the ideas:

**1. FrugalBuy**
* **IDEA NAME**: FrugalBuy
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find affordable alternatives for everyday products.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates discounts, coupons, and cashback offers from local stores and online retailers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians looking to save money on daily expenses.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦50/per transaction (cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing coupon and discount platforms in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier resources.
 3. Partner with 5 local stores to offer exclusive discounts.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, as seen in the Reddit threads about salvage grocery stores and passive cashback apps.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (utilizing existing resources and free tier services)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partner management)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)
