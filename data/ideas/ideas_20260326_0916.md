* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (for ride-hailing companies)

	1. Conduct market research to understand the pain points of ride-hailing users in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic web platform for users to report issues and provide feedback.
	3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
