After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. FinSavvy**
1. **IDEA NAME**: FinSavvy
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with managing their finances and credit scores.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized financial advice, credit score tracking, and budgeting tools.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) who want to improve their financial literacy.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research existing personal finance apps and identify key features to include.
   - Design a user-friendly interface and develop a minimum viable product (MVP).
   - Reach out to financial experts and potential partners for collaboration.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" and "I feel like I need to get out of the corporate world" indicate a need for financial guidance and planning.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and affiliate marketing).
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some automated features, but human expertise required for premium services).
   - Team Size: 7/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be done with a smaller team).
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched within weeks, but full feature development may take months).
