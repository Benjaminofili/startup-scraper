**1. Doomscrolling Stopper**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Stopper
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with mindless scrolling on social media, wasting time and affecting their mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for productivity and relaxation.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and frameworks.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to promote the app on social media and relevant online platforms.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be built with existing resources and minimal investment)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
