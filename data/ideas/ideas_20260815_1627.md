**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling mindlessly on their phones, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their screen time, providing tips to reduce mindless scrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to improve their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as screen time tracking and alerts.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is mentioned in the user complaints, and with the rise of social media, it's an opportune time to create a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some costs for marketing and maintenance)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for updates and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a solo developer, but marketing and maintenance may require additional help)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians cannot afford the high fees charged by existing ride-hailing platforms, such as Uber and Bolt.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, with optional in-app donations.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers who use the platform and want to support the service with donations.
5. **PRICE**: Free (optional donations)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms, such as LibreTaxi, and adapt them to the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a community of drivers and passengers to test and provide feedback on the platform.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the platform and encourage donations.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints mention LibreTaxi, a free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, which indicates a demand for such a service in Nigeria.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, as it's open-source and free)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and community management)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a small team, but community management may require additional help)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within months)
