3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for productivity and mental well-being.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction for premium features).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to promote the app on social media platforms.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about social media addiction, making this a timely solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing APIs and free resources)

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks using existing resources)
