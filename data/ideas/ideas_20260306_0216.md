After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable platforms for earning passive cashback rewards.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates cashback offers from various online stores and provides users with a seamless way to earn rewards.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Online stores and advertisers pay for promoting their products through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local online stores to integrate their cashback offers.
	* Design a user-friendly app interface using existing templates.
	* Develop a basic app functionality using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for marketing and partnerships)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for maintenance and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
