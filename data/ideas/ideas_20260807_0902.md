**1. Digital Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with organizing their notes and finding a suitable alternative to Notion or Outline.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and syncing across devices.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals in Nigeria, particularly those in the education and consulting sectors.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the note-taking habits of Nigerian students and professionals.
 2. Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with core features.
 3. Set up a landing page to collect email addresses and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and existing alternatives like Notion or Outline may not be affordable or suitable for the Nigerian market.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, using open-source resources and free marketing channels)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic MVP)
