After analyzing the problems from real users, I have identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to make ends meet and need ways to earn passive cashback on their daily expenses.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on each purchase (₦50 per transaction on average).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback.
	* Develop a basic Android app using the AWS free tier.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the economic downturn, Nigerians are looking for ways to save money, and cashback apps are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, can start with AWS free tier).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work needed to manage partner stores).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch in a few weeks with a basic app).
