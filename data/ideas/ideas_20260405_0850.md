After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. App Update Bug Fixer**
1. **IDEA NAME**: FixIt
2. **PROBLEM**: Many app updates have bugs that log users out and prevent them from logging back in.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple app that helps users identify and fix common bugs in recently updated apps.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and publishers who want to resolve user complaints quickly.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per bug fix report (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common app update bugs.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic app that can detect and report bugs.
	* Step 3: Reach out to app developers and publishers to offer the FixIt service.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug complaint on the Play Store highlights the need for a solution like FixIt.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, can use existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to analyze bug reports)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team or even solo)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. Alternative to Google Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub

3. **SOLUTION**: A platform that offers alternative services to Google products, such as search, email, and cloud storage.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses who value data privacy and security.
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦5,000 per month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing alternative services to Google products.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) for NaijaHub.
	* Step 3: Launch a marketing campaign to attract early adopters.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Alternatives to Google Products post on HackerNews highlights the demand for alternative services.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some development costs, but can use open-source solutions)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team with diverse skills)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (may take a few months to develop and launch)
