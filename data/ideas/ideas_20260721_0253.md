After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards for purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cash back on every purchase (₦50 per transaction)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	3. Reach out to partner stores to discuss commission rates.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some marketing costs may apply)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated, but some customer support required)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can start with one person)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (weeks to launch, depending on partner store onboarding)

**2. Gatekeeper**
* **IDEA NAME**: Gatekeeper
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to preserve and share their cultural heritage, but gatekeeping can be a issue.
* **SOLUTION**: A social media platform that allows users to share and discover Nigerian culture, with a focus on community moderation.
* **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers pay to reach the platform's users.
* **PRICE**: Free for users, ₦5,000 per month for advertisers
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Develop a basic social media platform using AWS free tier.
	2. Create a community moderation system to prevent gatekeeping.
	3. Reach out to cultural influencers to promote the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are discussing the importance of preserving Nigerian culture, and social media platforms can help.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, but some marketing costs may apply)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (ad revenue, but some content moderation required)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed, but can start with one person)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (weeks to launch, depending on community growth)

**3. LibreRideNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRideNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want affordable and reliable transportation options.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, using a peer-to-peer ride-sharing model.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay a small fee per ride, which is split between the driver and the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦50 per ride (split between driver and platform)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and develop a basic ride-sharing platform using AWS free tier.
	2. Create a community of drivers and riders to test the platform.
	3. Develop a rating system to ensure quality and safety.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for affordable transportation options, and peer-to-peer ride-sharing can help.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some marketing costs may apply)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (transaction fees, but some customer support required)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can start with one person)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (weeks to launch, depending on community growth)
