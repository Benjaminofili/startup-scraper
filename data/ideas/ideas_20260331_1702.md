2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to help them manage their screen time.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as screen time tracking, personalized recommendations, and reminders.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using existing APIs and frameworks, focusing on core functionality.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem, and the Nigerian market is ripe for a tool that promotes digital wellness.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, existing APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases, potential for ads)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 team members, including a developer and marketer)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development, existing frameworks)
