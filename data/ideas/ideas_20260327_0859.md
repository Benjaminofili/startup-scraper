After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. BugFix**
1. **IDEA NAME**: BugFix
2. **PROBLEM**: Many apps, including popular ones, have bugs that log users out or prevent them from logging in, causing frustration and inconvenience.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly app that helps users troubleshoot and fix common app bugs, with a focus on Nigerian apps.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and owners, particularly those with popular apps in the Nigerian market.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per bug fix, or ₦20,000 per month for premium support.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common app bugs in the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the BugFix app using the team's existing resources (Android, web, APIs).
	* Step 3: Reach out to local app developers and owners to offer free trials and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store highlights the need for a reliable bug-fixing solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for bug fixing)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic version)

**3. LibreTaxiAlternative**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreTaxiAlternative
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to afford reliable and safe transportation, particularly in urban areas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, allowing users to book rides with local drivers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Drivers, who can pay a small commission per ride, or advertisers, who can pay for in-app advertising.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride, or ₦5,000 per month for premium advertising.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most popular transportation routes and pricing in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the LibreTaxiAlternative app using the team's Android development skills.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local drivers and transportation companies to offer free trials and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post about LibreTaxi highlights the demand for affordable and reliable transportation solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some capital needed for marketing and driver acquisition)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (commission-based revenue can be automated)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a moderate-sized team for driver acquisition and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic version, but requires driver acquisition and marketing efforts)
