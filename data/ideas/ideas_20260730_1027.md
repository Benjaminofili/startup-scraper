After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative Ride-hailing Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideReview
2. **PROBLEM**: Unsatisfied ride-hailing users lack a platform to review and compare services.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app and web platform for users to review and rate ride-hailing services, providing an alternative to Uber and other existing platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies will pay for premium listings and advertising on the platform.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium listings, ₦500 per transaction for advertising.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Conduct market research on existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
   - Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the review platform using the AWS free tier.
   - Step 3: Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss partnership opportunities.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from a user who has been using Uber for over 10 years but is now dissatisfied highlights the need for an alternative review platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier, minimal initial investment)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation)
   - Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with an MVP)
