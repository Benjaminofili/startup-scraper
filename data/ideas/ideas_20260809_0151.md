**1. [EduNotes]**

1. **IDEA NAME**: EduNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle to find reliable and organized notes for their courses.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides open-source, community-driven notes and study materials for Nigerian university students.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, potentially through in-app donations or sponsored content.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) or ₦500 (donation) per month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct a survey among Nigerian university students to identify the most in-demand courses and topics.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	* Step 3: Seed the app with initial notes and study materials, and invite a small group of students to test and provide feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital learning materials is increasing, and Nigerian students are looking for affordable and reliable solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (no initial capital needed, can start with a basic app)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through donations or sponsored content)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand to maintain and update content)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch a basic app within days)
