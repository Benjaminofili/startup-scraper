After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack access to cashback and rewards platforms for their daily expenses.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback and rewards for users who shop at partner stores, pay bills, or use specific services.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who use the app to earn cashback and rewards.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (e.g., if a user earns ₦100 cashback, the app takes ₦5).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with local businesses to offer cashback and rewards.
 2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit posts about passive cashback and savings apps indicate a demand for such services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback and rewards system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but partnerships may take time)

**2. BillTracker**

* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to keep track of their bills and payments, leading to late fees and penalties.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to track and pay their bills, receive reminders, and earn rewards for timely payments.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who use the app to pay their bills.
* **PRICE**: ₦50 per transaction (or a monthly subscription of ₦200).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Develop a basic web app using the web developer on the team.
 2. Integrate payment gateways to enable bill payments.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit posts about saving money and avoiding late fees indicate a need for a bill tracking and payment solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (automated payment system, but some manual work for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team to manage development and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)
