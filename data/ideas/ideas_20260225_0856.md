**1. Doomscrolling Stopper**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Stopper
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for healthier online habits.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-35) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app, focusing on user-friendly features and tracking capabilities.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to raise awareness about the app's benefits and encourage early adopters.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" highlights the growing concern about excessive social media usage, making it an ideal time to launch a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing AWS free tier and open-source resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)

4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers and businesses looking for authentic reviews and feedback.

7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "Show HN: I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products" demonstrates the demand for review platforms, and the Nigerian market is ripe for a similar solution.

	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
