**1. Alternative to Google Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google products due to privacy concerns and data ownership.
3. **SOLUTION**: NaijaHub is a web-based platform offering a suite of open-source, privacy-focused alternatives to Google products, such as search, email, and cloud storage.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses in Nigeria who value data privacy and security.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and select open-source alternatives to Google products.
	* Set up a basic website using the AWS free tier.
	* Create a social media presence to promote NaijaHub.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for alternative products is increasing, and NaijaHub can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source solutions and AWS free tier)

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
