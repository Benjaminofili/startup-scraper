**1. NaijaCulture**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaCulture
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find authentic and reliable sources of Nigerian culture, leading to gatekeeping and misrepresentation.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app and web platform showcasing Nigerian culture, traditions, and history, with user-generated content and community features.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers targeting the Nigerian diaspora and local businesses, as well as in-app purchase for premium content.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month for premium content, ₦1000 per ad placement.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Conduct market research on existing cultural platforms and identify gaps in the market.
   - Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features and content.
   - Step 3: Establish partnerships with local cultural organizations and influencers.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post on gatekeeping Nigerian culture highlights the need for a platform that showcases authentic Nigerian culture.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for advertising revenue)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (advertising and in-app purchases, but requires content updates)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of 2-3 for development, content creation, and marketing)
   - Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch an MVP within weeks, but requires continuous development)
