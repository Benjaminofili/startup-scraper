**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to monitor and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve credit health.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or limited credit history ( ₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month)
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing credit score tracking apps and their features.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the CreditPal app.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer's skills.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" are seeking help with credit score issues, indicating a need for a credit score tracking app.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)
