After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, affecting their financial stability.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering credit monitoring, reporting, and repair services, providing personalized advice to improve credit scores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month or ₦5,000 per credit report analysis.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs for credit scoring.
 3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit repair services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (utilizing existing APIs and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for credit analysis)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 team members can handle initial development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)
