After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. [EduNotes]**

1. **IDEA NAME**: EduNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students lack access to affordable and organized study materials, leading to poor academic performance.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides free, open-source notes and study materials for Nigerian students, curated by top performers and educators.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and institutions can donate or pay for premium features, such as personalized learning plans.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct a survey among Nigerian students to identify the most in-demand study materials and features.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using open-source notes and materials.
	* Step 3: Reach out to educators and top performers to collaborate on content creation.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and accessible study materials is high, and the Nigerian market is ripe for a solution like EduNotes.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, leveraging open-source materials)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members for content creation)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)

**2. [NaijaRide]**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians face challenges in finding reliable and affordable transportation options, especially in rural areas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers, using a peer-to-peer model and focusing on safety and affordability.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers pay for rides, while drivers earn a commission.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/ride (minimum fare), 10% commission for drivers
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify areas with high demand for transportation services.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using existing ride-hailing platforms as a reference.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential drivers and partners to collaborate on the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Nigerian market is in need of a reliable and affordable transportation solution, and the success of similar platforms like LibreTaxi can be replicated.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 5/10 (some initial investment required for marketing and driver acquisition)
	* Passive Income: 4/10 (manual work required for driver management and customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team to manage drivers, passengers, and platform maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (may take several weeks to launch and acquire drivers)
