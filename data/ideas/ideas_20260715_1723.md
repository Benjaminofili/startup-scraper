After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, and some have experienced a significant drop in their credit score due to unforeseen circumstances.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve credit health.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with a credit history, particularly those who have experienced a drop in their credit score.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing credit score tracking apps and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier resources.
* **WHY NOW**: The user complaint "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" highlights the need for a credit score tracking and improvement tool.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing and data partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can handle development and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype).
