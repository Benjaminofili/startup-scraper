2. **PROBLEM**: Users get stuck in infinite scrolling on social media, wasting time and affecting mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their scrolling habits, providing tips to break the cycle.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, active on social media, willing to pay for mental wellness tools.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing solutions and identify unique selling points (Day 1-2).
	* Design a basic UI/UX for the app (Day 3-4).
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using Android and web developers (Day 5-7).
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to improve their mental health, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post.
