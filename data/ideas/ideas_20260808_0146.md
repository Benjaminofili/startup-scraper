**1. Digital Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with organizing their notes and documents, leading to wasted time and decreased productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app for Android and web, with features like tagging, searching, and syncing across devices.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals in Nigeria, with a focus on universities and small businesses.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Conduct market research to understand the note-taking habits of Nigerian students and professionals.
  2. Design a basic prototype of the app using existing open-source note-taking software.
  3. Reach out to potential users for feedback and testing.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is increasing, and existing solutions like Notion and Outline are not fully meeting the needs of Nigerian users.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing open-source software)
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
