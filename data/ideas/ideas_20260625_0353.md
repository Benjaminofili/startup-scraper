**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to save money passively while shopping online.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases made through partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Online shoppers in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web developers.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract early users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and online shopping is becoming increasingly popular in Nigeria.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with a basic app)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
