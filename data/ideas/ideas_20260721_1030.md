After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals monitor and improve their credit scores, which can be affected by others, like the user's mother in the Reddit post.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, alerts, and personalized advice for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged or low credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on existing credit score apps in Nigeria.
 2. Define the app's features and user interface.
 3. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the AWS free tier.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post highlights the importance of credit scores and the need for a user-friendly app to track and improve them.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier, minimal marketing budget)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some customer support and updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused effort)
