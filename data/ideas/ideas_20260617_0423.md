After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve credit health.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
 3. Partner with local financial institutions to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score management tools.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP quickly, but full feature set may take longer)
