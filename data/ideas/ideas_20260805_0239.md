After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps, seeking an open-source alternative to Notion or Outline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features like organization, tagging, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦100-₦500 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Set up a GitHub repository and create a basic prototype using existing open-source libraries.
	* Step 3: Share the prototype on social media and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and NoteHub can fill this gap in the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, using open-source libraries and free resources)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads, but mostly free)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 developers needed for maintenance and updates)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (weeks to launch, depending on the complexity of features)

**2. Personal Finance Tracker**

1. **IDEA NAME**: FinancePal
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle with managing their finances, seeking a simple and affordable personal finance tracker.
3. **SOLUTION**: A user-friendly mobile app for tracking expenses, income, and budgeting.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small business owners seeking to manage their finances effectively.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500-₦1,000 per month (subscription-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing personal finance apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and create a basic prototype using Android or web development frameworks.
	* Step 3: Test the prototype with a small group of users and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for personal finance management tools is increasing, and FinancePal can cater to the Nigerian market's needs.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and development, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model with potential for automated payments)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (2-3 developers and 1 marketer needed)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (months to launch, depending on the complexity of features)

4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, parents, and educational institutions seeking affordable e-learning solutions.
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000-₦5,000 per course (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 3: Set up a basic website using web development frameworks and start creating course content.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for e-learning solutions is increasing, and LearnNaija can cater to the Nigerian market's specific needs.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some initial investment for content creation and marketing, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (course sales and potential ads, but mostly one-time payments)

	* Time to Market: 6/10 (months to launch, depending on the complexity of features and content creation)
