**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, freelancers, and small business owners who need a cost-effective note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations (₦500/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and create a prototype.
	* Step 3: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternatives to Notion and Outline, and an open-source solution can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no capital needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by 1-2 developers)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched in weeks)
