**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for healthier online habits.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (15-30 years old) who want to improve their mental well-being and productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app's benefits and encourage early adopters.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are seeking solutions to mitigate its negative effects (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (utilizing existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires iterative development and refinement)
