After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered with local businesses.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research local businesses for partnerships (Day 1-2).
	* Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs (Day 3-4).
	* Launch a social media campaign to gather user interest (Day 5-7).
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" and "10 Ways to Save Money Passively" posts.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed for marketing and app development).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (small team needed for app development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick launch possible with existing APIs).
