After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here they are:

**1.** **CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	2. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to partner for cashback offers.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
