After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Credit Score Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to repair their damaged credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform that provides personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and educational resources.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month (basic plan) to ₦20,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify potential partners.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking and educational resources.
	3. Establish a social media presence to raise awareness about the service.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit thread "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initially, a small team can manage the platform, but may require more staff as it grows)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, but full features may take months)
