After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score tracking, tips, and alerts to help users improve their creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or limited credit history, seeking to improve their financial health.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with local financial institutions to access credit data and expertise.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and data access)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (core team can handle development and marketing, but may require external expertise)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature set may take longer)
