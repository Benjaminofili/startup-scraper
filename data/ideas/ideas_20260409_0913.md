**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for productivity and mental well-being.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, young professionals, and individuals seeking to improve their mental health and productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing APIs and AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be built using existing resources and free APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for coaching and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
