After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback when shopping online or using certain services.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made through partner merchants.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who shop online or use services like food delivery, transportation, or bill payments.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on transactions (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local merchants (e.g., Jumia, Konga, Bolt).
	* Develop a basic app using the Android developer.
	* Create a social media presence to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some marketing expenses).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback is a passive income stream).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but may require iterations).
