**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

2. **PROBLEM**: Many students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features similar to Notion or Outline, available on Android and web platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations or premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify features to include in NoteHub.
   - Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, considering Nigerian users' preferences.
   - Step 3: Start building the app using existing open-source frameworks and libraries.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable note-taking solutions is high, and an open-source alternative can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

   - Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
   - Time to Market: 7/10 (depending on the complexity of features)
