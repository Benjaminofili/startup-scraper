After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Career Guidance App**
* **IDEA NAME**: CareerPath
* **PROBLEM**: Many young adults struggle to find a career path, leading to dissatisfaction and uncertainty.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides career assessments, job market insights, and personalized guidance.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per year)
* **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦2,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the career guidance landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a basic career assessment tool.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and gather feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of career uncertainty is prevalent, and many users are seeking guidance, as seen in the Reddit post "I’m a dishwasher at 27, where do I go from here?"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content updates and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the app, but may require additional help for marketing and content creation)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)
