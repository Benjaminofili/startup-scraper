After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and the need for passive income, a cashback platform can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with partners)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development, marketing, and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch with a basic prototype, but requires ongoing development and marketing)

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and find reliable savings platforms.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money by offering automated savings plans, budgeting tools, and investment options.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Users who save money through the app ( subscription-based or commission-based).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦1,000 per month (depending on the savings plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Conduct market research to understand user savings habits and preferences.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	* Partner with financial institutions to offer secure savings and investment options.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the increasing need for financial literacy and savings, a user-friendly savings platform can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for revenue through subscriptions or commissions)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated savings system, but requires ongoing marketing and user engagement)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development, marketing, and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can launch with a basic prototype, but requires ongoing development and marketing)

**3. PriceCompareNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: PriceCompareNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find the best prices for products online and offline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to compare prices for products across different stores and marketplaces.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Affiliate marketing revenue from partner stores.
5. **PRICE**: Free for users.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local online stores and marketplaces.
	* Develop a basic web and mobile app prototype using the web and Android developers.
	* Create a database of products and prices.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of e-commerce and online shopping, a price comparison platform can help users make informed purchasing decisions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for revenue through affiliate marketing)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated price comparison system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development, marketing, and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch with a basic prototype, but requires ongoing development and marketing)

**4. TaskNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: TaskNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy platforms for freelance work and task completion.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that connects users with freelance workers and task completers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Users who post tasks and freelance workers who complete tasks ( commission-based).
5. **PRICE**: 10% - 20% commission on task completion.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and develop a basic web and mobile app prototype using the web and Android developers.
	* Create a database of freelance workers and tasks.
	* Partner with local businesses to offer task completion services.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of the gig economy and freelance work, a task completion platform can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue through commissions)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated task completion system, but requires ongoing marketing and user engagement)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development, marketing, and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch with a basic prototype, but requires ongoing development and marketing)
