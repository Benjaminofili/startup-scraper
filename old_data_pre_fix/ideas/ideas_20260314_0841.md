5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to create awareness about the app's benefits.
