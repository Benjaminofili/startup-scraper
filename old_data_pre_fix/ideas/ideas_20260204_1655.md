**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**
* **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
* **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service from businesses, such as the Samsung store, leaves customers feeling disrespected and unheard.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review businesses based on their customer service experience.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their services.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify target businesses and understand their pain points.
 2. Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
 3. Reach out to 10 local businesses to pilot test the app and gather feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a platform to hold businesses accountable for their customer service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (utilize existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work to onboard businesses and manage feedback)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with the current team, but may require additional support for marketing and sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
