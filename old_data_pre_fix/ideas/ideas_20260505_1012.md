After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered stores, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local stores and online marketplaces.
	* Develop a basic app using AWS free tier and Android/web development skills.
	* Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post on Reddit.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal costs for marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
