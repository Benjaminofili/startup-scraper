After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years) who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing the negative effects of excessive screen time, indicating a need for a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-Hailing Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with the high costs of ride-hailing services like Uber and Lyft.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers with passengers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform is free, with optional donations).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform using the web developer on the team.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential drivers and passengers to test the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The success of LibreTaxi on HackerNews indicates a demand for free and open-source ride-hailing platforms.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing open-source resources, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through donations)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team to maintain and update the platform)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but may require additional time for testing and refinement)
