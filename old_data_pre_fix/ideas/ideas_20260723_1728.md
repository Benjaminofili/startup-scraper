* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's financial pain points.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness and generate interest.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing personal finance, credit scores, and money management, indicating a high demand for a user-friendly and accessible financial management tool.
