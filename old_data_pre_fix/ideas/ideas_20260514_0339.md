**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially freelancers and remote workers, struggle with doomscrolling, which affects their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users when they've spent too much time scrolling on social media, providing tips and resources to help them stay focused.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers, remote workers, and students who want to boost their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦50 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using Android Studio.
	* Step 3: Develop a content strategy for the app's resources and tips section.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to stay focused and productive.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, using existing resources and free design tools)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a solo developer, but marketing and content creation may require additional team members)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, using the AWS free tier and Play Store account)

2. **PROBLEM**: Freelancers struggle to find clients and get paid fairly for their work, while clients have difficulty finding reliable and skilled freelancers.

4. **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers (listing fees) and clients (transaction fees).
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month for freelancers (listing fee) or 5% transaction fee for clients.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing freelance platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the platform using web development frameworks.
	* Step 3: Develop a marketing strategy to attract freelancers and clients.
7. **WHY NOW**: The freelance market is growing, and users are looking for reliable platforms to find work and get paid fairly.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some investment required for marketing and server costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (transaction fees and listing fees can generate passive income)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a team of developers, marketers, and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can take months to develop and launch)
