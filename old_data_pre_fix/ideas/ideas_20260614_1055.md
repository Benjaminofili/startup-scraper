After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at local stores, restaurants, and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, merchants, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Partner with 5 local merchants to offer exclusive cashback deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive cashback, and the Nigerian market lacks a reliable and trustworthy cashback app.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing APIs)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 team members needed for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (2-3 months to launch)

* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on existing savings apps and their features.
 2. Develop a user-friendly interface and basic features (savings goals, expense tracking).

* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and track their expenses, and the Nigerian market lacks a user-friendly savings app.

	+ Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 team members needed for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (3-4 months to launch)
