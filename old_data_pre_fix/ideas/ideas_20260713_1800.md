**1. Digital Dishwasher Career Guide**
* **IDEA NAME**: CareerPath
* **PROBLEM**: Many young Nigerians struggle to find a fulfilling career path, as seen in the story of the 27-year-old dishwasher.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering career guidance, resume building, and job search resources tailored to the Nigerian job market.
* **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) seeking career guidance, with a potential market size of 10 million users.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the career aspirations and challenges of young Nigerians.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a team of 2 developers and 1 marketer.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and gather feedback from early users.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of career uncertainty is widespread, and the Nigerian job market is becoming increasingly competitive, making it essential to provide guidance and support to young adults.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue generation)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some automated features, but human coaching and support required)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team, but may require additional staff for coaching and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (rapid development and launch possible with a focused team)

**2. Buy Nothing Group Alternative**
* **IDEA NAME**: ShareHub
* **PROBLEM**: Frustration with existing Buy Nothing groups, as expressed by a Reddit user, indicates a need for a more efficient and user-friendly platform for sharing and exchanging goods.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app connecting people in local communities to share, exchange, or sell items, with a focus on ease of use and community building.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals looking to declutter, share, or acquire items, with a potential market size of 5 million users.
* **PRICE**: Free (basic features), ₦1,000/month (premium features, such as verified profiles and priority support)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing Buy Nothing groups and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a prototype with a team of 2 developers and 1 marketer.
	3. Launch a pilot program in a small community to test and refine the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The sharing economy is growing, and Nigerians are looking for alternative ways to acquire and dispose of goods, making it an ideal time to launch a platform that addresses the shortcomings of existing solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for revenue generation)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (mostly automated, with some moderation required)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (small team, with potential for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (rapid development and launch possible with a focused team)

**3. Credit Score Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Credit score damage, as experienced by a Reddit user, can have severe consequences, and many Nigerians lack access to resources and guidance to repair their credit.
* **SOLUTION**: A digital platform offering credit score monitoring, reporting, and repair services, with personalized advice and support.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, with a potential market size of 2 million users.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,500/month (basic plan), ₦5,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a partnership with a credit reporting agency to access credit data.
	3. Launch a marketing campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and the benefits of the CreditFix service.
* **WHY NOW**: Credit scores are becoming increasingly important in Nigeria, and the lack of resources and guidance for credit repair creates an opportunity for a dedicated service to fill this gap.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (initial costs for partnerships and marketing, potential for revenue generation)
	+ Passive Income: 5/10 (some automated features, but human support and coaching required)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team, with potential for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (moderate development and launch time due to partnerships and regulatory requirements)
