After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. LibreMarket**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreMarket
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable, free, and open-source alternative to existing e-commerce platforms.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source e-commerce platform for buying and selling goods, similar to LibreTaxi but for general products.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual sellers and buyers, with a focus on students and low-income individuals.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) for basic listings, ₦500 (per month) for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing open-source e-commerce platforms (e.g., OpenCart, Magento) and identify the best fit for LibreMarket.
	2. Set up a basic platform using the chosen open-source solution on AWS free tier.
	3. Create a social media presence (e.g., Twitter, Facebook) to promote LibreMarket and gather feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about "bad service" from a customer on the PlayStore suggests a need for alternative, user-friendly platforms.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (using open-source solutions and AWS free tier minimizes costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for platform maintenance and support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for scaling)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly using existing open-source solutions)

**2. DoomScroll**

* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with excessive social media use, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app (Android) that helps users track and limit their social media usage, with features like app blocking and customizable goals.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual users, with a focus on students and young professionals.
* **PRICE**: ₦200 (per month) for premium features, ₦0 (free) for basic features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing solutions (e.g., Freedom, SelfControl) and identify key features to include in DoomScroll.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app, with a focus on simplicity and ease of use.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio and test it with a small group of users.
* **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post suggests a growing interest in solutions for excessive social media use.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (can be developed with minimal costs using Android Studio and AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions can generate passive income)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly using existing development tools)
