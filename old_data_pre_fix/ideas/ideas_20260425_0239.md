**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling mindlessly on social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips and resources to help them manage their screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media and concerned about their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app, focusing on simplicity and user-friendliness.
	* Step 3: Develop a prototype using Android Studio, integrating APIs for social media tracking.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Reddit and HackerNews posts highlight the issue of excessive social media usage and the need for solutions to manage screen time.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing Play Store account and AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but some manual work required for updates and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a solo developer, but marketing and maintenance may require additional help)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
