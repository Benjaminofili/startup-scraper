**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for alternative activities.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦500-₦1,000 per month)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 (monthly subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic app layout and user interface using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts may require additional help)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)
