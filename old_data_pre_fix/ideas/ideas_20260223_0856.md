* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to review and rate their ride-hailing experiences, providing valuable feedback for other users.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Bolt, Uber) can pay for premium listings and advertising on the app.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month for premium listings, ₦100 per transaction for advertising.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify target audience and competitors.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing templates.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using the AWS free tier.
