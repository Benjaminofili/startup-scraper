2. **PROBLEM**: Passengers are charged high fares for trips due to inefficient routes taken by drivers.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that suggests the most cost-effective routes for passengers, allowing them to negotiate with drivers for cheaper fares.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers using the app (targeting students and low-income individuals).
5. **PRICE**: ₦50 per transaction (or ₦500 monthly subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the current ride-hailing landscape in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype of the app using existing mapping APIs.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential partners (e.g., ride-hailing companies) to discuss integration opportunities.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore comment highlights the need for cheaper trips, and the HackerNews discussion on LibreTaxi shows interest in alternative transportation solutions.
