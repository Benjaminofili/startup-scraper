After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Product Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: ReviewHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to find trustworthy reviews of durable products before making a purchase.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to rate and review products, with a focus on durability and quality.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers (e.g., manufacturers, retailers) and affiliate marketing partnerships.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free for users), ₦5,000 - ₦20,000 (monthly advertising packages).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most popular product categories in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic website and social media presence to promote the platform.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential advertisers and partners to discuss collaboration opportunities.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products" post on HackerNews indicates a demand for such a platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some costs for marketing and maintenance)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation and advertising sales)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members as the platform grows)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)

**2. Mental Health and Productivity App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: MindPal
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with mental health issues, such as anxiety and depression, and lack access to affordable support resources.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers guided meditations, mood tracking, and personalized productivity tips.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Users (in-app purchases or subscription model) and partnerships with mental health organizations.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦2,000 (monthly subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing mental health apps and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a content strategy and create a library of guided meditations and productivity tips.
	* Step 3: Design a user-friendly interface and begin building the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews highlights the need for mental health support.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (in-app purchases and subscription model can generate passive income)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members as the app grows)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within months)
