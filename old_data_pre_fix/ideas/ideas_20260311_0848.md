**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for healthier online habits.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are frequent social media users.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Conduct market research and analyze competitors.
	* Day 3-4: Design the app's user interface and user experience.
	* Day 5-7: Develop a basic prototype using Android and web technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions can generate passive income, but user engagement is crucial).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers and a marketer can handle the project).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched within a few weeks, but continuous updates and improvements are necessary).

**2. E-Paper Dashboard for Nigerian News**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaNewsHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to stay up-to-date with local news, as traditional newspapers are often expensive and inaccessible.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based e-paper dashboard that aggregates Nigerian news from various sources, providing users with a convenient and affordable way to stay informed.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses interested in staying informed about Nigerian news and current events.
5. **PRICE**: ₦200/month (subscription-based model) or ₦50 per transaction (pay-per-article).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research and partner with local news sources to aggregate content.
	* Day 3-4: Design and develop the e-paper dashboard using web technologies.
	* Day 5-7: Test and launch the dashboard with a limited set of features.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital news platforms is increasing, and an e-paper dashboard can cater to the growing need for accessible and affordable news in Nigeria.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs are low, and partnerships with news sources can be established at no cost).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscriptions and pay-per-article models can generate passive income).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers and a marketer can handle the project).
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (the dashboard can be launched within a few weeks, and updates can be made incrementally).
