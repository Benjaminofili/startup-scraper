**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on every purchase (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs (e.g., payment gateways).
	* Step 3: Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and attract early users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post on Reddit.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with existing resources).
