After analyzing the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and expertise. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, and poor credit scores can lead to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized financial advice, and offers resources to improve credit scores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or average credit scores, particularly those in the 25-45 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score tracking landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the mobile app.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using existing APIs for credit score tracking.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are concerned about their credit scores, and a credit score tracker can help them monitor and improve their financial health.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can use existing APIs and develop a basic version with minimal investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for personalized coaching, but can be automated to some extent)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
