**1. Doomscrolling Stopper**
* **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Stopper
* **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for alternative activities.
* **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	2. Design a basic UI/UX for the app, focusing on simplicity and user-friendliness.
	3. Develop a prototype using Android and web technologies, leveraging AWS free tier resources.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are seeking solutions to manage their social media usage.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging AWS free tier and existing development skills)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires testing and iteration)

**2. LibreRide - A Free Ride-hailing Alternative**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with the high costs of ride-hailing services, and some are concerned about the lack of transparency in pricing.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform that connects drivers and passengers, with transparent pricing and a focus on community building.
* **WHO PAYS**: None (the platform is free, with optional donations or advertising revenue)
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them to the Nigerian market.
	2. Develop a basic web and mobile interface for the platform, using existing development skills.
	3. Establish a community of drivers and passengers to test and provide feedback on the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and transparent ride-hailing services is high, and the open-source approach can help build a community-driven platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (leveraging existing open-source platforms and free resources)
	+ Passive Income: 5/10 (optional donations or advertising revenue, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires testing and iteration)
