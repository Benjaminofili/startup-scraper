**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for outdoor activities and mindfulness exercises.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)
