After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and businesses looking for a free, customizable note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦500-₦1000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
  2. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With many users seeking alternatives to Notion or Outline, an open-source note-taking app can fill this gap.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 9/10 (₦0 needed, except for potential domain registration)
  - Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by 1-2 developers)
  - Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. Personal Finance Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: FinancePal
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to track their expenses and stay on top of their finances.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly personal finance tracker app with features like budgeting, expense tracking, and bill reminders.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses looking to manage their finances effectively.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features (₦1000-₦5000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing personal finance apps and identify key features to include.
  2. Design a user-friendly interface and start building the app using existing libraries.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of digital payments in Nigeria, a personal finance tracker app can help users manage their finances effectively.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (some investment in marketing and potential server costs)
  - Passive Income: 7/10 (premium features and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can be built by 2-3 developers)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within months)

**3. Alternative to Google Photos**
* **IDEA NAME**: PhotoVault
* **PROBLEM**: Many users are concerned about the privacy and security of their photos on Google Photos.
* **SOLUTION**: A secure, end-to-end encrypted photo storage app with features like automatic backup and sharing.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and families looking for a secure and private photo storage solution.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium storage (₦1000-₦5000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing end-to-end encrypted photo storage solutions and identify key features to include.
  2. Set up a secure server using AWS free tier and start building the app.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With growing concerns about data privacy, an alternative to Google Photos can attract users seeking a secure solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (some investment in server costs and marketing)
  - Passive Income: 8/10 (premium storage and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can be built by 2-3 developers)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within months)

**4. Digital Marketplace for Local Services**
* **IDEA NAME**: ServiceHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy local services like plumbers, electricians, and cleaners.
* **SOLUTION**: A digital marketplace app connecting users with verified local service providers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Service providers (₦500-₦2000 per month) and users (optional premium features)
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features (₦1000-₦5000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing marketplaces and identify key features to include.
  2. Design a user-friendly interface and start building the app using existing libraries.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users and service providers.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of the gig economy, a digital marketplace for local services can fill this gap.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 7/10 (some investment in marketing and potential server costs)
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (commission-based and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 6/10 (can be built by 3-4 developers and marketers)
  - Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within months)

**5. E-Learning Platform for Nigerian Students**
* **IDEA NAME**: LearnNaija
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerian students struggle to access quality educational resources and study materials.
* **SOLUTION**: An e-learning platform offering video courses, study materials, and practice exams for Nigerian students.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students (₦1000-₦5000 per month) and educational institutions (optional)
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features (₦1000-₦5000 per month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing e-learning platforms and identify key features to include.
  2. Design a user-friendly interface and start building the platform using existing libraries.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online learning, an e-learning platform can provide quality educational resources to Nigerian students.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 6/10 (some investment in content creation and marketing)
  - Passive Income: 5/10 (subscription-based and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 5/10 (can be built by 3-4 developers and content creators)
  - Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within months)

These ideas prioritize zero-capital, passive income opportunities, focusing on the Nigerian market. By leveraging existing open-source libraries, free tiers, and strategic marketing, these startups can launch with minimal investment and generate revenue through donations, premium features, or advertising.
