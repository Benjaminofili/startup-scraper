After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Buster**
* **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for alternative activities.
* **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to improve their mental well-being.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	2. Design a basic prototype of the app using existing design tools.
	3. Develop a content marketing strategy to create engaging content around mental health and productivity.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is mentioned in the HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass", indicating a growing concern about excessive social media usage.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs can be low, but marketing and maintenance may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (the app can generate revenue through subscriptions, but may require ongoing development and marketing efforts)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can develop and maintain the app)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched within a few weeks, but may require ongoing iteration and improvement)

**2. Nigerian Product Review Platform**
* **PROBLEM**: Consumers in Nigeria lack a reliable platform to find reviews and ratings of local products, making it difficult to make informed purchasing decisions.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile platform that allows users to review and rate Nigerian products, providing a comprehensive directory of trusted products.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers and businesses looking for reliable product reviews and ratings.
* **PRICE**: Free for basic listings, ₦5,000/month for premium listings (with additional features like verified reviews and priority customer support).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing review platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the platform using existing web development frameworks.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to gather interest and feedback on the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "Show HN: I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products" indicates a growing interest in review platforms, and Nigeria lacks a similar platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs can be low, and the platform can generate revenue through listings and advertising)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (the platform can generate revenue through listings and advertising, with minimal ongoing development efforts)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can develop and maintain the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (the platform can be launched within a few weeks, with ongoing iteration and improvement)

**3. LibreTaxi Nigeria**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians lack access to affordable and reliable transportation options, and existing taxi services can be expensive and inefficient.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, providing a platform for drivers and riders to connect and share rides.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders and drivers who use the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free for riders and drivers, with optional tips and donations).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing ride-hailing platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the platform using existing web development frameworks.
	3. Reach out to local drivers and riders to gather interest and feedback on the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft" indicates a growing interest in alternative transportation options, and Nigeria can benefit from a similar platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 10/10 (the platform can be developed and maintained with minimal investment, using open-source technologies)
	+ Passive Income: 5/10 (the platform can generate revenue through donations and tips, but may require ongoing development and marketing efforts)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can develop and maintain the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (the platform can be launched within a few weeks, with ongoing iteration and improvement)
