**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with apps that log them out and don't allow them to log back in due to bugs.
3. **SOLUTION**: An Android app that automatically saves and restores login credentials for other apps, ensuring seamless login experiences.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Premium users who want ad-free experience and additional features (₦500/month).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) for basic users, ₦500/month for premium users.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most affected apps and user demographics.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for AutoLogin.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android SDK.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store highlights the need for a reliable login solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (no significant capital required, but some development costs).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app ads and premium subscriptions, but some manual work required).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a solo developer, but marketing and support may require additional team members).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but may require iterations based on user feedback).
