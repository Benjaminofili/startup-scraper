After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. RideHype**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideHype
2. **PROBLEM**: Uber's algorithm can be unfair to drivers, treating them like "non-thinking drones" and affecting their earnings.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects drivers with passengers, offering a more driver-friendly alternative to Uber, with features like flexible pricing and better commission rates.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers and drivers (commission-based)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (passenger), 10% commission per ride (driver)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the needs of Nigerian drivers and passengers.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	* Step 3: Onboard a small group of drivers and passengers for testing and feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about Uber on the Play Store indicates a need for alternative ride-hailing services that prioritize driver welfare.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some initial investment for marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 4/10 (some manual work required for customer support and driver management)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team required for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (several weeks to launch)
