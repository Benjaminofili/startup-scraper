After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users when they've exceeded a certain amount of time on social media, encouraging them to take breaks and focus on their goals.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android Studio, integrating APIs for social media tracking.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their screen time.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, using free design tools and AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
