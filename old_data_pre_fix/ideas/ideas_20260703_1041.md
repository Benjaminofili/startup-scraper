After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Digital Credit Score Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to repair their damaged credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform offering personalized credit score repair advice, credit monitoring, and debt management tools.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month or ₦20,000 per year.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score repair landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic website with credit score repair advice and resources.
	3. Reach out to potential partners, such as credit bureaus or financial institutions.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial website development and marketing costs are low)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 can manage the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
