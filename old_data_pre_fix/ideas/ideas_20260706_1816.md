* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks credit scores, provides personalized advice, and offers resources to improve credit health.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing credit score tracking apps and their features.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the CreditPal app.
 3. Develop a basic prototype using Android and web technologies.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" are struggling with credit score issues, indicating a need for a credit score tracking solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (utilizing existing AWS free tier and open-source technologies).

	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype).
