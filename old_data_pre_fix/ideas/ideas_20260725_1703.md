After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Digital Credit Coach**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, like the 25M who saw his credit score drop from 700+ to 580.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and educational resources.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly young adults (₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 - ₦5,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit score landscape.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking and educational resources.
 3. Partner with a credit scoring agency to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is prevalent, and existing solutions are often expensive or inaccessible.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but partnerships may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 can manage the initial launch)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)

* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerian families (₦2,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦1,500 - ₦3,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing e-paper dashboard solutions and identify key features for the Nigerian market.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the FamilyBoard app.
 3. Develop a basic MVP with core features like news sharing and task management.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of digital adoption in Nigeria, families are looking for innovative ways to stay connected and organized.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, and potential for partnerships with news outlets)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for content curation and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (a small team of 2-3 can manage the initial launch)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)
