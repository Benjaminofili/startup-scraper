**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing features like website blocking, app tracking, and personalized recommendations for productivity.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, young professionals, and individuals who want to improve their productivity and mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Conduct market research and analyze competitors.
	* Day 3-4: Design the app's user interface and user experience.
	* Day 5-7: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaints about doomscrolling and the need for productivity tools are increasing, making it an ideal time to launch a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model with potential for automation).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with an MVP).
