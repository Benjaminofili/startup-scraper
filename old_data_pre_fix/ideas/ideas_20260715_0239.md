After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score recovery plans and alerts for credit report changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the CreditPal app using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score recovery solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing may require some investment).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and credit report analysis).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with the current team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype).
