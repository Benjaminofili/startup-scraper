**1.** **[IDEA NAME]**: CreditPal
**PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score management, monitoring, and improvement tips.
**WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
**PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
**WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, and there's a lack of personalized credit management solutions in the Nigerian market.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and data access)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (quick development possible with an MVP approach)
