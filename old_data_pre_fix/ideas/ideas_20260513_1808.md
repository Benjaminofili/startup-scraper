After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cash back on their daily expenses.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back rewards (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with local stores to offer cash back rewards.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cash back apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for marketing and development)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back rewards and advertising revenue)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic app)
