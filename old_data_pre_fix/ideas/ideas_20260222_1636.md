**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips and resources to help them manage their screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media and concerned about their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Create a content calendar for social media promotion and engage with potential users to gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage, making it an opportune time to launch a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing content updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional design and content creation support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)
