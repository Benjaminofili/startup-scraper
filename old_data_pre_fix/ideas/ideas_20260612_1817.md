After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market with zero-capital and passive income potential. Here are the ideas:

**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score management, tips, and alerts to help users improve their creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦0 (basic features with ads).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify potential partners.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and the benefits of using CreditPal.
* **WHY NOW**: The user complaints about credit scores (e.g., "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?") indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but potential for partnerships and advertising revenue).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for premium features and customer support).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version quickly, but may require more time for premium features).
