After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. LibreRide**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are dissatisfied with current ride-hailing services like Uber, seeking alternative options.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay per transaction, with optional subscription plans for frequent users.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (average), ₦5,000 monthly subscription.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
 2. Develop a basic web and Android app using the AWS free tier.
 3. Recruit local drivers and promote the service on social media.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about Uber's service, and there's a demand for alternative ride-hailing options.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (using open-source platforms and free tier resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for driver management and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members as the service grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)
