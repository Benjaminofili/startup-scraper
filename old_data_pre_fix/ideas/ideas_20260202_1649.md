* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards for customers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research local businesses and potential partners.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	3. Create a social media presence to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed, leveraging AWS free tier)
