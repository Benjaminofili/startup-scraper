* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit report changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history, as well as financial institutions looking to promote credit literacy.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research on existing credit score management apps in Nigeria.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the CreditPal app.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier services.
* **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit are concerned about credit scores, and a credit management app can help address this issue.
