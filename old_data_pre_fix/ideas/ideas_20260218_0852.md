**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
2. **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service from companies, such as the Samsung store, leaves customers feeling disrespected.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review companies based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Companies will pay for premium features, such as detailed analytics and alerts, to improve their customer service.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium features.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer service issues in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local businesses to gather interest and feedback on the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a platform to hold companies accountable for their customer service.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)

	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched quickly, but refining the app and gathering user feedback will take time)
