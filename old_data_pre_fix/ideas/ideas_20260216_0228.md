After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Lanter**
* **IDEA NAME**: Lanter
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians face poor customer service from e-commerce platforms and physical stores.
* **SOLUTION**: A review and rating platform for Nigerian businesses, allowing customers to share their experiences and help others make informed decisions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses listed on the platform, who can pay for premium features and advertising.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular e-commerce platforms and physical stores in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic web scraper to collect existing reviews from various sources.
 3. Design a user-friendly interface for customers to leave reviews and ratings.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore review and the Reddit complaint about poor customer service highlight the need for a reliable review platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (utilize existing resources and free tier AWS)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual work for moderation and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with the current team size)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. DoomFree**
* **IDEA NAME**: DoomFree
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians, like many others, struggle with doomscrolling and mental health.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their screen time, providing personalized recommendations for mental wellness and productivity.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual users who pay for premium features and personalized coaching.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing screen time tracking apps and identify areas for improvement.
 2. Develop a basic algorithm to track screen time and provide personalized recommendations.
 3. Design a user-friendly interface for the mobile app.
* **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post about an app to stop doomscrolling highlights the growing concern about mental health and screen time.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (utilize existing resources and free tier AWS)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can be automated with in-app purchases)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with the current team size)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within days)

**3. LibreRide**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians face high costs and limited options for transportation.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, allowing users to request rides from verified drivers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Drivers who pay a small commission per ride.
* **PRICE**: 10% commission per ride.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing ride-hailing platforms and identify areas for improvement.
 2. Develop a basic algorithm to match drivers with riders.
 3. Design a user-friendly interface for the mobile app.
* **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post about LibreTaxi highlights the need for affordable and reliable transportation options.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (requires some investment in marketing and driver verification)
	+ Passive Income: 5/10 (requires manual work for driver verification and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (may require additional team members for driver verification and customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (may take months to launch and gain traction)
