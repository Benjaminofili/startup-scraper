After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **PROBLEM**: People struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, as seen in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and improve their credit scores by providing personalized advice and alerts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-45 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features such as credit score tracking and alerts.
	3. Partner with a credit bureau or financial institution to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is prevalent, and with the growing awareness of financial literacy, there's a need for a user-friendly credit score tracking app.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and revenue)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months with a focused development effort)
