**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: OpenNote
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for an open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app for Android and web, with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals who want a free, customizable note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional donations (₦500/month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android and web technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of remote learning and work, note-taking apps are in high demand, and an open-source solution can attract a large user base.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no initial investment needed)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by a small team)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (depending on the complexity of features)
