Based on the user complaints and problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. RideHail Feedback**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideHail Feedback
* **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers in Nigeria often reject rides, leaving passengers frustrated and stranded.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows passengers to rate and provide feedback on their ride-hailing experiences, promoting accountability and better service.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) could pay for access to feedback data to improve their services.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (for ride-hailing companies to access feedback data)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the ride-hailing landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the app using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint on the PlayStore about inhuman drivers and ride rejection highlights the need for a solution to improve ride-hailing experiences.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to maintain and update the app)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but requires some development and marketing efforts)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a small team)
