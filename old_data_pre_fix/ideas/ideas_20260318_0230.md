After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized productivity tips and reminders.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals seeking to improve their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing solutions and identify key features to include.
	* Design a user-friendly interface using Android and web development skills.
	* Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier resources.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about wasting time on social media (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost development, existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (2-3 team members can handle development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development, testing, and launch)

**2. LibreRide Nigeria**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide Nigeria
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face difficulties with transportation, and existing ride-hailing services can be expensive.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting drivers and riders in Nigeria.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders (optional, with a small commission per ride) and drivers (through a small subscription fee).
5. **PRICE**: ₦100/ride (optional, with a ₦500/month subscription for drivers)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Develop a basic platform using web and API development skills.
	* Reach out to potential drivers and riders for feedback and testing.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternatives to expensive ride-hailing services (e.g., "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low-cost development, existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (some manual work for support, commission-based revenue)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (2-3 team members can handle development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (quick development, testing, and launch)
