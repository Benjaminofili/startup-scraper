**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste hours scrolling through social media, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing reminders to take breaks and engage in physical activities.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are heavy social media users.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, using existing social media APIs.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app, focusing on tracking and reminder features.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage (e.g., "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic version, using free social media APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
