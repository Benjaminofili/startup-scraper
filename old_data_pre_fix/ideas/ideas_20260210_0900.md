After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreShop**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreShop
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are looking for free and open-source alternatives to popular e-commerce platforms.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source e-commerce platform for buying and selling products, similar to LibreTaxi.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual sellers and small businesses.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) for basic listings, ₦500/month for premium features.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source e-commerce platforms and identify the best one to fork or use as inspiration.
	* Step 2: Set up a basic platform using the AWS free tier and customize it for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to attract early adopters and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaint about "bad service" from a Samsung store highlights the need for alternative platforms.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (using open-source software and AWS free tier reduces costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for moderation and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for customization)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly using existing open-source software)
