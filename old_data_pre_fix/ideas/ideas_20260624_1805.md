After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 spent).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores and online platforms.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app with a user-friendly interface.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
