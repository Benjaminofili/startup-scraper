After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App (Open-Source Alternative to Notion)**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free, open-source digital note-taking app to organize their notes and ideas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and small business owners in Nigeria.
5. **PRICE**: Free (open-source), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and user experience for the app.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using existing open-source libraries and frameworks.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking apps is increasing, and an open-source alternative to Notion can fill the gap in the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (existing open-source libraries and frameworks can be used)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (premium features and donations can generate revenue)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers can build and maintain the app)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)
