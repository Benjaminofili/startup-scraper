After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, which affects their financial stability.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score recovery plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (premium features) or ₦500/credit report.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking and educational resources.
 3. Partner with a credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit recovery solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (premium features and affiliate marketing potential)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a small team, but can be maintained by one person)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature development takes time)

**2. DishWash**
* **IDEA NAME**: DishWash
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals, particularly those in the service industry, struggle to find reliable and efficient ways to manage their work schedules and finances.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects dishwashers and other service industry workers with employers, providing scheduling tools, payment tracking, and financial management resources.
* **WHO PAYS**: Employers and employees in the service industry.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month (employer subscription) or ₦500/month (employee premium features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the needs of service industry workers and employers.
 2. Develop an MVP with basic job matching and scheduling features.
 3. Partner with local businesses to test the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "I’m a dishwasher at 27, where do I go from here?" indicate a need for career development and financial management tools.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 5/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing development)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (initial development requires a small team, but can be maintained by one person)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature development takes time)
