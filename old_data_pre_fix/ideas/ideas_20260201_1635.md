**1. Doomscrolling Detector**

3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are heavy social media users.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, using existing APIs for social media integration.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app, focusing on tracking and alerting users about their social media usage.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time (e.g., "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing APIs, minimal infrastructure costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but requires design, development, and marketing efforts)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, with a basic version)
