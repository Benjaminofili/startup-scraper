4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-35) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about excessive social media usage and its negative effects on their mental health, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
