**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
* **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
* **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users set goals, track progress, and provides reminders to take breaks and engage in physical activities.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are frequent social media users.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
 2. Design a basic prototype of the app using existing resources (e.g., AWS free tier).
 3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness and generate interest.
* **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage, making it an opportune time to launch a solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low-cost development using existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based, but requires ongoing development and marketing)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
