**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and rewarding cashback apps for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at local stores, restaurants, and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps and their offerings.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Partner with 5 local businesses to offer exclusive cashback deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income and save money, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue through advertising and commissions)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards and advertising revenue)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more staff as the user base grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP within weeks, but may require ongoing development and iteration)
