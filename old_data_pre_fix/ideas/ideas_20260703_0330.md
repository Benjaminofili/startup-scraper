**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	2. Develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
	3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about passive saving apps and cashback rewards.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
