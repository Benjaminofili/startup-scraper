Based on the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals who want to improve their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to stay focused (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but some manual work for updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts required)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. LibreRide (Alternative to Uber/Lyft)**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face high costs and poor service quality when using ride-hailing apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting drivers and passengers directly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers (per ride) and drivers (commission-based)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/ride (average fare)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi).
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the web developer on the team.
	* Step 3: Partner with local driver associations to promote the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for affordable and reliable transportation alternatives (e.g., "LibreTaxi – A free and open source alternative to Uber and Lyft").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some development costs, but can use existing open-source resources)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (commission-based, but requires ongoing maintenance)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a team to manage drivers, passengers, and platform development)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can take months to develop and launch)
