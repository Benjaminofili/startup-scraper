**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy platforms to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards to users on their purchases.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local businesses in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for cashback and rewards is high, as seen in the Reddit posts about passive cashback apps.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some initial investment for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch within a few weeks)
