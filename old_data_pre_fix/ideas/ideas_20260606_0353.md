After analyzing the problems from real users, I have identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic app with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and the desire for passive income, a cashback app can capitalize on this trend.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for app development and marketing)
   - Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
