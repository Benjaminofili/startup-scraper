### 1. **CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases and expenses.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses looking to save money and earn rewards.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing cashback apps and identify potential partners.
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	* Create a social media presence and content marketing strategy.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" and "10 Ways to Save Money Passively" posts.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some development costs)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (weeks to launch, depending on development speed)
