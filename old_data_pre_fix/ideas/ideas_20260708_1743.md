**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals track and improve their credit scores, which can be damaged by others, like the user's mother in the Reddit post.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to monitor their credit scores, receive alerts for changes, and provides tips for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged or low credit scores, particularly those in the 25-45 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦2,000/year.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score tracking landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as credit score tracking and alerts.
 3. Partner with a credit bureau or financial institution to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post highlights the importance of credit scores and the need for individuals to take control of their financial health.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
