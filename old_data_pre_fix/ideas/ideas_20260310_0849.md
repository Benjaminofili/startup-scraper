After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly app interface using existing APIs.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic algorithm to track and analyze social media usage.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about excessive social media usage, and there's a growing need for digital wellness solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing APIs, minimal development cost)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. LibreRide (Nigerian Ride-hailing Alternative)**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable ride-hailing services.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform, connecting drivers with passengers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Passengers (per ride) and drivers (commission-based).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/ride (average fare)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing services in Nigeria, identifying areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic platform using open-source software (e.g., LibreTaxi).
	* Step 3: Onboard a small group of drivers for testing and feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for alternatives to expensive ride-hailing services, and the Nigerian market is ripe for disruption.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 5/10 (some development cost, potential partnerships with drivers)
	* Passive Income: 4/10 (commission-based, some manual work for management)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of 2-3 for development and management)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within months)
