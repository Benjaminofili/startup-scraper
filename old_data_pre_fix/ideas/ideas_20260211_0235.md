After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Alternative to Google Products (NaijaHub)**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian users are looking for alternative products to Google services due to privacy concerns and data ownership.
* **SOLUTION**: NaijaHub is a web-based platform offering a suite of open-source, privacy-focused tools, including a search engine, email client, and cloud storage.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual users, small businesses, and organizations seeking data sovereignty.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most in-demand Google alternative services.
	2. Set up a basic website using open-source tools like WordPress or Ghost.
	3. Integrate existing open-source alternatives (e.g., DuckDuckGo for search, Tutanota for email) into the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are seeking alternatives to Google products due to concerns over data privacy and ownership, as seen in the HackerNews complaints.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source tools reduces development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for customer support and platform maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initially, a small team can manage, but may require more members as the platform grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)

**2. Customer Review Platform for Nigerian Businesses (NaijaReviews)**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to share and discover reviews of local businesses.
* **SOLUTION**: NaijaReviews is a web and mobile app allowing users to leave reviews and ratings for Nigerian businesses, similar to Yelp or Google Reviews.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses can pay for premium listings and advertising, while users can access the platform for free.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month (basic listing) or ₦5,000/month (premium listing).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular review platforms and their features.
	2. Develop a basic web and mobile app using existing frameworks like React or Flutter.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to encourage them to claim their listings and provide feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for reliable review platforms, as seen in the PlayStore complaints about poor customer service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (some development costs, but can leverage existing frameworks)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (advertising and premium listings can generate revenue with minimal manual work)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (initially, a small team can manage, but may require more members as the platform grows)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, but may require more time to refine)
