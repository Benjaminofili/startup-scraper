**1. Digital Dashboard for Nigerian SMEs**
* **IDEA NAME**: DashNaija
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerian small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) struggle to organize and visualize their data, leading to inefficiencies and poor decision-making.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, web-based dashboard that integrates with popular Nigerian business tools, providing a centralized platform for SMEs to track key metrics and performance indicators.
* **WHO PAYS**: Nigerian SME owners and managers
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (basic plan), ₦20,000 per month (premium plan)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify key pain points and requirements for Nigerian SMEs.
	2. Design a minimum viable product (MVP) dashboard with essential features.
	3. Develop a landing page to showcase the product and collect interest from potential customers.
* **WHY NOW**: Many Nigerian SMEs are looking for ways to digitize and streamline their operations, and a user-friendly dashboard can help them make data-driven decisions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing resources and free tier AWS)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and customization)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a focused effort)
