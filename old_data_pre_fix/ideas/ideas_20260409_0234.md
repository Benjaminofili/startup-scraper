**1. DoomScrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users to their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as usage tracking, alerts, and personalized tips.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling on HackerNews ("Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass") indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
