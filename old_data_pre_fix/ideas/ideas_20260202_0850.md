**1. Alternative Food Delivery Review Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: FoodReviewNG
* **PROBLEM**: Uber Eats and other food delivery services are shady, and users need a reliable platform to review and discover trustworthy food delivery options.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app and web platform where users can review and rate food delivery services, providing an alternative to existing review platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Food delivery services and restaurants will pay for premium listings and advertising on the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium listings, ₦500 per transaction for advertising.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify target audience and competitors.
	2. Design a basic prototype of the app and web platform.
	3. Reach out to food delivery services and restaurants to gauge interest in premium listings and advertising.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about Uber Eats being shady on the PlayStore suggests a need for an alternative review platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs can be low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the platform, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic prototype, but may require iterations and improvements)
