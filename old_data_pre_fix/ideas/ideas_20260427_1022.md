Based on the problems and complaints from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**

1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling mindlessly on social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips and resources to help them manage their time better.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, including features such as usage tracking, alerts, and resource recommendations.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android Studio and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for tools to help them manage their social media usage, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, as the app can be developed using free resources and open-source libraries)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions can generate passive income, but some manual work may be required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers and a marketer can handle the project)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched within a few weeks, as it's a relatively simple project)

**2. LibreTaxi Nigeria**

1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreTaxi Nigeria
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face difficulties in finding reliable and affordable transportation options, especially in rural areas.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, tailored to the Nigerian market, allowing users to book rides and connect with local drivers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses looking for affordable transportation options.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (commission-based model) or ₦5,000 per month (subscription-based model for frequent users).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research the Nigerian transportation market, including existing players and regulations.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the app using the LibreTaxi open-source code and adapt it to the Nigerian market.
	* Step 3: Partner with local drivers and transportation companies to offer a wide range of options to users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The need for affordable and reliable transportation options in Nigeria is growing, and the success of ride-hailing services like Uber and Lyft in other markets demonstrates the potential for a similar service in Nigeria.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some initial investment may be required to develop the app and partner with drivers, but the open-source nature of the project reduces costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (the commission-based model can generate significant passive income, but some manual work may be required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (a small team of 3-4 developers, a marketer, and a few partners can handle the project)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (the app can be launched within a few months, as it requires some development and partnership efforts)

**3. Rotten Tomatoes for Nigerian Products**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy reviews of local products, making it difficult to make informed purchasing decisions.
3. **SOLUTION**: A platform that allows users to review and rate Nigerian products, providing a trusted source of information for consumers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses and manufacturers looking to promote their products and improve their reputation.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (listing fee) or ₦10,000 per year (premium listing with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the types of products Nigerians are interested in reviewing and the existing review platforms.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform using a content management system like WordPress or a custom-built solution.
	* Step 3: Partner with local businesses to list their products and encourage users to leave reviews.
7. **WHY NOW**: The success of review platforms like Rotten Tomatoes demonstrates the value of trusted reviews in helping consumers make informed decisions, and the Nigerian market lacks a similar platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some initial investment may be required to develop the platform and partner with businesses, but the potential for revenue is high)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (the listing fees and premium listings can generate significant passive income, with minimal manual work required)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers, a marketer, and a few partners can handle the project)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the platform can be launched within a few weeks, as it's a relatively simple project)
