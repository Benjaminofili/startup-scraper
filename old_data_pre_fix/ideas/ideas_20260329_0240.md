After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with frequent logouts and login issues on various apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: An Android app that automatically saves and fills login credentials for users.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who frequently use multiple apps and value convenience.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research existing password manager apps and identify areas for improvement.
	* Day 3-4: Design a simple and user-friendly interface for AutoLogin.
	* Day 5-7: Start building the app using Android Studio and integrate a secure password storage system.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store highlights the need for a reliable login solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but existing resources can be utilized)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. DurableProductReview**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DurableProductReview
2. **PROBLEM**: Consumers struggle to find reliable and durable products, leading to wasted money and time.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web platform that aggregates reviews and ratings for durable products, similar to Rotten Tomatoes.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers seeking trustworthy product reviews and manufacturers looking to promote their durable products.
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month for manufacturers to list their products or ₦500/year for consumers to access premium reviews.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research existing review platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	* Day 3-4: Design a user-friendly interface for DurableProductReview and plan the review aggregation system.
	* Day 5-7: Start building the web platform using existing web development skills and AWS free tier resources.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for durable products is increasing, and a reliable review platform can fill this gap.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (can be developed with minimal costs using existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through affiliate marketing and manufacturer listings)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for content creation)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months)
