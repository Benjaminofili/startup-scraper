After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases, but existing platforms are not tailored to the local market.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at local stores, restaurants, and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback platforms and their business models.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a user-friendly interface.
 3. Partner with 5 local businesses to offer cashback on their products.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback platforms are gaining popularity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).

 1. Conduct market research to understand user savings habits and pain points.

* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and digital savings platforms are becoming increasingly popular.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
