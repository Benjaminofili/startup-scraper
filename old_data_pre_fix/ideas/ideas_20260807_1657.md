After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreRide - Alternative to Uber/Lyft**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians need a free and open-source alternative to expensive ride-hailing services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects drivers and riders, with features like real-time tracking, payment processing, and ratings.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay a small commission per ride, while drivers pay a monthly subscription fee.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride (commission), ₦5,000 per month (driver subscription)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi) and adapt them to the Nigerian market.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Reach out to potential drivers and riders for feedback and testing.
* **WHY NOW**: The success of LibreTaxi and the need for affordable transportation options in Nigeria make this a timely opportunity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (adaptation and development required, but existing open-source platforms can be leveraged)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and marketing)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team can manage development and operations)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (MVP can be launched within weeks)

* **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**:

	+ Team Size: 9/10 (solo developer or small team can manage development and operations)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (MVP can be launched within days)
