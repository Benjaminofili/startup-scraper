After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards for purchases made at partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on sales (₦50 per transaction)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	3. Reach out to 5 partner stores to discuss commission rates.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partner management)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch in weeks)

* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and integrate with Nigerian credit bureaus.
	2. Develop a basic web and mobile app prototype.
	3. Create a content marketing plan to attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, and there's a lack of credit management tools in Nigeria.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some capital needed for credit bureau integration)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (premium features and advertising)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team with credit expertise)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch in months)
