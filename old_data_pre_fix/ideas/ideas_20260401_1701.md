**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**
* **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
* **PROBLEM**: Users get stuck in infinite scrolling on social media, leading to wasted time and decreased productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users set time limits, track screen time, and provides reminders to take breaks and engage in physical activities.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, young professionals, and individuals who want to improve their mental health and productivity.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface and develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling on HackerNews ("Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass") indicates a growing concern about this issue.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based, but requires periodic updates and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
