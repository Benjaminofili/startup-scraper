After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1.** 
* **[IDEA NAME]**: CustomerRespect
* **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service from e-commerce platforms and retail stores.
* **SOLUTION**: A review and rating platform for Nigerian businesses, allowing customers to share their experiences and provide feedback.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses and e-commerce platforms looking to improve their customer service and reputation.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for basic listing, ₦20,000 per month for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify target businesses and customer pain points.
	2. Develop a basic website and review system using existing resources (AWS free tier).
	3. Reach out to 10 local businesses to pilot the platform and gather feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint on PlayStore about poor customer service highlights the need for a platform that holds businesses accountable.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development can be done with existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for moderation and support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
