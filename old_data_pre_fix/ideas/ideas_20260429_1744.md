**1. Digital Product Review Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to find trustworthy reviews of digital products.
* **SOLUTION**: A user-generated review platform for digital products, such as software, apps, and online courses.
* **WHO PAYS**: Digital product creators and sellers who want to increase their product's visibility and credibility.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium listings.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify popular digital products in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic platform using existing open-source review software.
 3. Reach out to digital product creators to offer free listings and gather feedback.
* **WHY NOW**: The success of Rotten Tomatoes-style platforms (as seen in the HackerNews post) and the lack of a similar platform in Nigeria make this a timely opportunity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can start with open-source software and minimal marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers for scaling)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic platform within weeks)
