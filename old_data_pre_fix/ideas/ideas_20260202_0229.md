**1. Alternative Ride-Hailing Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideReview
2. **PROBLEM**: Uber and other ride-hailing services have poor customer service and shady practices, leaving users without a reliable platform to share their experiences.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where users can rate and review their ride-hailing experiences, providing a community-driven alternative to traditional review platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers (ride-hailing companies and related businesses) and potentially, a small subscription fee for premium features.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (ad-free experience) or ₦50 per transaction (for ride-hailing companies to access user reviews).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian ride-hailing market and identify potential partners.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app, focusing on user experience and review functionality.
	* Step 3: Reach out to potential advertisers and partners to gauge interest and discuss revenue models.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about Uber's shady practices on the Play Store highlights the need for a reliable review platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (ad revenue and subscription fees, but some manual work required)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional members for growth)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version quickly, with iterations and improvements following)
