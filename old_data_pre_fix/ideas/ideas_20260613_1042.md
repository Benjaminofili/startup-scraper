**1. CashBackNG**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cash back.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates cash back offers from local businesses and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cash back, and businesses that partner with CashBackNG to offer deals.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with a 5% commission on cash back earned.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research local businesses and online platforms to partner with.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about passive cash back apps.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated, with potential for high earnings)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires ongoing development)
