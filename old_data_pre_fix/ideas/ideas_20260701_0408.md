After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Credit Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, affecting their financial stability.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform offering personalized credit repair advice, credit monitoring, and dispute resolution services.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month or ₦20,000 per year.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit repair landscape.
	2. Develop a basic website with credit repair resources and a lead capture form.
	3. Partner with a credit reporting agency to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" highlight the need for credit repair services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial investment required for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for dispute resolution)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team with 2-3 members)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (launch possible within 2-3 months)
