**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
* **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
* **PROBLEM**: Users waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as screen time tracking, personalized recommendations, and reminders.
	3. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer's expertise and test it with a small group of users.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
