After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score monitoring, tips, and alerts to help users improve their creditworthiness.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with a credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and revenue)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and partnerships)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, with continuous iteration and improvement)
