After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RespectMe
2. **PROBLEM**: Customers feel disrespected by companies, leading to negative reviews and a loss of trust.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review companies based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Companies will pay to access customer feedback and improve their customer service.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for basic feedback reports.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer complaints.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface for customers to leave feedback.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local businesses to gauge interest in the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about bad service and disrespect for customers highlights the need for a platform to hold companies accountable.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to maintain the app and provide reports)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and sales)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
