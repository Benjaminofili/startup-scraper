**1. Demerit System for Ride-hailing Apps**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideSafe
2. **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers often drop fares they've accepted, causing inconvenience to passengers.
3. **SOLUTION**: Develop a mobile app that introduces a demerit system to deter drivers from dropping fares, ensuring a smoother ride-hailing experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) and drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per driver per month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing apps and their APIs to understand integration possibilities.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the demerit system and its user interface.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a demerit system to improve the ride-hailing experience.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing APIs and infrastructure)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual monitoring and support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional support for marketing and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
