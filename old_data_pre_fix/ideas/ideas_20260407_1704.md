3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized suggestions for alternative activities.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as screen time tracking and personalized suggestions.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest in the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about social media addiction, making this a timely solution.
