**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media scrolling time, providing personalized recommendations for alternative activities.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-35) who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to improve their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as scrolling time tracking and personalized recommendations.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest in the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to mitigate its effects (e.g., "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, but some marketing expenses)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but some manual work required for support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly with an MVP, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
