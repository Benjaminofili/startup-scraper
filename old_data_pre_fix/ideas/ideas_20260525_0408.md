* **[IDEA NAME]**: NaijaSaver

* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money and earn passive cashback rewards through partnerships with local businesses.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who want to save money and earn passive income.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
 2. Design and develop a basic mobile app for users to track their savings and rewards.
 3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and attract initial users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the economic uncertainty and high cost of living in Nigeria, people are looking for ways to save money and earn extra income.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with free resources)
