3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly app interface using existing templates.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and web development skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required).

	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts may require additional help).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version).
