**1. [IDEA NAME]**: CreditPal
**PROBLEM**: Users struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit report changes.
**WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
**PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with local financial institutions to access credit data and provide more accurate scores.
**WHY NOW**: With the increasing importance of credit scores in Nigeria, users are looking for ways to manage and improve their creditworthiness.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and data access)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature development may take time)
