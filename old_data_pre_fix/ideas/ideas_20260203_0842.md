* **WHO PAYS**: Small to medium-sized SaaS businesses in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month (basic plan) or ₦10,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify the most common pain points in existing landing page builders.
 2. Design a basic prototype of the EasyLanding platform using existing open-source tools.
 3. Reach out to 10 potential customers to gather feedback and validate the idea.
