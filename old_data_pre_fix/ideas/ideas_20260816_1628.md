1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile and web app for note-taking, organization, and collaboration, with features like tagging, searching, and offline access.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, professionals, and small businesses in Nigeria who need a cost-effective note-taking solution.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) with optional donations or premium features for ₦500/month.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using Figma or Sketch.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android, web, and API technologies.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and Notion/Outline users are looking for free alternatives.
