Based on the user complaints and the team's resources, here are the top 5 startup opportunities:

**1.**
* **IDEA NAME**: SafeLogin
* **PROBLEM**: Users are experiencing login issues and being logged out of their accounts due to bugs in recent app updates.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, secure, and reliable login manager app that helps users manage their login credentials and provides a seamless login experience.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual app users, particularly those who use multiple apps and services.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦50 per transaction.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular apps and services that users struggle with login issues.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the login manager app.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
* **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store and the complaints about login issues on Reddit and HackerNews indicate a need for a reliable login solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, using existing resources)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2.**
* **IDEA NAME**: DurableMart
* **PROBLEM**: Users are looking for a platform to review and compare durable products, similar to Rotten Tomatoes for movies.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to review, rate, and compare durable products, providing a comprehensive platform for informed purchasing decisions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individual users, as well as manufacturers and sellers of durable products who can advertise on the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦1,000/month for premium features or ₦500 per product listing.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing review platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the web and mobile app.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using the web and Android developers on the team.
* **WHY NOW**: The Show HN post about a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products indicates a demand for such a platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (some capital needed for marketing and advertising)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through advertising and premium features)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of developers and marketers)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (may take several weeks to launch)
