After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Builder**

1. **IDEA NAME**: EasyLanding
2. **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and wasted marketing efforts.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly landing page builder with pre-designed templates and a drag-and-drop interface.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Small businesses and entrepreneurs in Nigeria who want to create professional-looking landing pages without breaking the bank.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦10,000 per month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research existing landing page builders and identify key features to include in EasyLanding.
	* Day 3-4: Design a basic prototype using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
	* Day 5-7: Test the prototype with a small group of users and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from the Reddit user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a better solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment in marketing).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and template updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some time for testing and iteration).
