2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about the negative effects of social media, and there's a growing demand for solutions to mitigate these effects.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for affordable alternatives to traditional ride-hailing services, and the open-source model can provide a competitive advantage.
