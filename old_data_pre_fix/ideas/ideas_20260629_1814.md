After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **NaijaSave**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find affordable and reliable alternatives to traditional shopping methods.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects users with local salvage grocery stores, enabling them to find discounted products and shop smarter.
* **WHO PAYS**: Low-to-middle-income individuals and families in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100 per transaction (basic features).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to identify popular salvage grocery stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for affordable shopping alternatives, and the salvage grocery store concept is gaining traction.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (minimal initial investment required)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for store partnerships and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (premium features) or ₦5,000 per credit repair service.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with credit bureaus in Nigeria to access credit score data.
	2. Develop a basic web platform using the web developer.
	3. Create a content marketing strategy to educate users about credit management.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, and there is a lack of reliable credit repair services in Nigeria.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some initial investment required for partnerships and marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through subscription-based models)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team with expertise in credit management)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within months)
