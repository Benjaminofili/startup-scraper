After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with frequent logouts and login issues on various apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: An Android app that automatically logs users back into their accounts after a logout or crash.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual app users, particularly those who use multiple apps daily.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month or ₦20 per transaction.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Conduct market research and analyze user complaints on the Play Store.
	* Day 3-4: Design and develop a basic prototype of the AutoLogin app.
	* Day 5-7: Test the app with a small group of users and gather feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store has highlighted the need for a reliable login solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (utilizing existing resources and free AWS tier)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
