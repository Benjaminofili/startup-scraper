3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review companies based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Businesses will pay for premium features, such as detailed analytics and alerts.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦1,000 per transaction (pay-per-review)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface using existing templates.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about bad service and disrespect for customers highlights the need for a platform like Respect Meter.
