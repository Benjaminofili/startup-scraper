After analyzing the user complaints and considering the Nigerian market, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on Nigerian retailers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and sign up for the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and partner with 5 local retailers to offer cashback.
	2. Develop a basic app with a user-friendly interface.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with partners)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback revenue with minimal manual work)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch with a basic app and iterate)

* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand Nigerian spending habits.
	2. Develop a basic app with expense tracking and savings goal features.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to manage their finances, and a savings app can help.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, potential for revenue from ads and in-app purchases)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (ad revenue and in-app purchases with some manual work)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch with a basic app and iterate)
