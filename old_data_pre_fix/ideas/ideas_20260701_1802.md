**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals recover from damaged credit scores by providing personalized advice and credit monitoring.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for suspicious activity.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (e.g., the Reddit user whose mother damaged their credit score).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) or ₦5,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the credit score landscape in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
 3. Partner with financial experts to provide personalized advice and coaching.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit user's problem highlights the need for credit score recovery services in Nigeria.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for personalized coaching).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be solo).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (MVP can be launched quickly, but full feature development takes time).
