After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals need a free, open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for digital note-taking.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly note-taking app with organization features and collaboration tools.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals seeking affordable digital note-taking solutions.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface and user experience.
 3. Set up a GitHub repository for collaborative development.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and an open-source alternative can attract a large user base.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (leveraging open-source resources and free development tools)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (premium features and potential ads)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within weeks)
