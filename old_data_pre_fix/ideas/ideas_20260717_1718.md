After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. [IDEA NAME]**: CashBackNG
**PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back from their daily transactions.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases from partner stores and online platforms.
**WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases.
**PRICE**: 5% cash back on transactions (e.g., ₦50 cash back on a ₦1000 purchase).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cash back.
	2. Develop a simple app using the Android developer on the team.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
**WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cash back apps are popular globally.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but no significant capital required)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires some marketing and development effort)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
