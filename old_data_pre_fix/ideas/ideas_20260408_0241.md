After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back on their daily expenses, but existing apps are not tailored to the local market.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at local stores, restaurants, and online platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦50 - ₦500 per transaction (depending on the partner business).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research local businesses and partners for cash back deals.
	* Design a user-friendly app interface using existing templates.
	* Reach out to potential partners for initial discussions.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and existing apps are not meeting their needs.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but partnerships may require some investment).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for partner management and customer support).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the app, but partnerships may require additional help).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (launch can happen within weeks, but partnerships may take time to establish).
