**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users set screen time limits, track their scrolling habits, and provides personalized recommendations to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription) or ₦100 per transaction (in-app purchase for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer's expertise.
	* Step 3: Create a social media marketing campaign to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints on Reddit and HackerNews highlight the need for solutions to address excessive screen time and mental health concerns.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires ongoing development and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with the existing team, but may require additional design and marketing support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires testing and iteration)
