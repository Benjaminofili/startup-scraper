**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users set boundaries and encourages them to take breaks from social media, promoting healthier habits.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to improve their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app, focusing on user experience and core features.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to create engaging content (blog posts, social media posts) that promotes the app's benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaints about doomscrolling and the need for a solution are evident in the user feedback, making it an opportune time to launch an app that addresses this issue.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing resources, minimal initial investment)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing content creation and marketing)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and content creation)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
