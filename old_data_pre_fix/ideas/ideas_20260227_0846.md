**1. Digital Product Review Platform**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ReviewHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian consumers lack a reliable platform to find honest reviews of digital products, leading to poor purchasing decisions.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to review and rate digital products, with a focus on Nigerian market needs.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Digital product sellers and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium listings, ₦500 per transaction for sponsored reviews.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify popular digital products in Nigeria.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic web app using AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the platform and attract initial users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "I built a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products" shows that review platforms can be successful, and the Nigerian market lacks a similar solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost web and mobile app development)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (automated review and rating system, but some manual moderation required)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible with AWS free tier resources)
