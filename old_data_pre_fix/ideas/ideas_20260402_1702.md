After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's skills and resources. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback apps that offer significant rewards.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides cashback on everyday purchases, partnered with local businesses.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to make purchases.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research local businesses to partner with.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web developers.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are in demand.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some marketing expenses).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage partnerships and customer support).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff as the app grows).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype).

**2. SaveNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG

* **PRICE**: 1% monthly fee on savings (₦100 per ₦10,000 savings).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing savings platforms and regulations.
	2. Develop a web-based platform using APIs and web developers.
	3. Create a marketing campaign to promote the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and digital savings platforms are in demand.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some marketing expenses).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (mostly automated, but some manual work required for customer support).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff as the platform grows).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within months with a basic prototype).
