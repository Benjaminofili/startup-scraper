After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are dissatisfied with the current ride-hailing services, such as Uber, due to poor routing systems and high costs.
3. **SOLUTION**: LibreRide is a free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, allowing users to request rides from verified drivers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses looking for affordable and reliable transportation.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride ( competitive with existing services)
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Register the business and obtain necessary licenses.
   - Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the AWS free tier.
   - Step 3: Recruit verified drivers and launch a marketing campaign on social media.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about Uber's poor routing system and the HackerNews discussion about LibreTaxi indicate a need for alternative ride-hailing services.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 6/10 (some initial investment for licenses and marketing)
   - Passive Income: 4/10 (requires ongoing management and driver recruitment)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more staff as the business grows)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP quickly, but full development may take time)
