After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital and passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered stores, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earned (₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and list potential partner stores.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android and web developers.
 3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but some marketing expenses)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback system)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for partner management)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)

**2. SaveNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG

* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money automatically by rounding up their transactions or setting aside a fixed amount regularly.
* **WHO PAYS**: Users ( subscription-based, ₦500/month).
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand user savings habits.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android and web developers.
 3. Integrate with local banks for seamless transactions.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and automated savings tools are gaining popularity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (no initial capital needed)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated savings system)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)
