After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing cashback apps and identify potential partners.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	* Step 3: Reach out to local businesses to discuss partnership opportunities.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and mobile payments in Nigeria, a cashback app can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing infrastructure)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for partner management)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but can be managed with existing resources)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick launch possible with a basic prototype)
