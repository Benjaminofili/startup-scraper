After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Helps individuals recover from damaged credit scores by providing personalized advice and credit monitoring.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for suspicious activity.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores (e.g., the user who had their credit score reduced to 580).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) or ₦5,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on credit score recovery services in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the CreditPal app using existing APIs for credit score tracking.
 3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and the benefits of using CreditPal.
* **WHY NOW**: The user complaint about a damaged credit score highlights the need for a credit recovery service in Nigeria.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic prototype and iterate).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for marketing and customer support).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks, but may take months to refine).
