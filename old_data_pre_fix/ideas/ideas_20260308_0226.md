**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals (₦500-₦1000 per month)
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android Studio, integrating APIs for social media tracking.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to reduce their screen time (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using free APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases or subscriptions, but some manual work for updates)
