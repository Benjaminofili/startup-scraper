**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media scrolling time, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-35) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features like time tracking, personalized recommendations, and motivational quotes.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for development and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and content creation)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused development effort)
