After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market with zero-capital and passive income potential. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app for daily transactions.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback platforms and their business models.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier resources.
 3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals and discounts.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and digital payments in Nigeria, a cashback platform can help consumers save money and earn rewards.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may arise)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards and commission-based revenue)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initially, a small team can manage the platform, but growth may require more staff)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (launch can happen within weeks, but partnerships and marketing take time)
