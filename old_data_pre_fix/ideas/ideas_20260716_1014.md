After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. [IDEA NAME]**: CashBackNG
**PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
**WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
**PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback reward per transaction (no monthly fee).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype with a user-friendly interface.
	3. Reach out to partner stores to discuss commission structures.
**WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	- Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for app development and marketing).
	- Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards).
	- Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing).
	- Time to Market: 7/10 (launch possible within weeks, but partner store onboarding may take time).

**2. [IDEA NAME]**: SaveNG
**PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money due to limited financial discipline and lack of automated savings tools.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money automatically by rounding up their purchases or setting aside a fixed amount regularly.
**WHO PAYS**: Users pay a small monthly fee (e.g., ₦500) for the service.
**PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing savings apps and their features.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype with automated savings functionality.
	3. Conduct user testing to refine the app's user experience.
**WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and automated savings tools are gaining popularity.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	- Investment: 9/10 (minimal investment needed for app development).
	- Passive Income: 8/10 (recurring monthly fees).
	- Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed for development and marketing).
	- Time to Market: 8/10 (launch possible within weeks).
