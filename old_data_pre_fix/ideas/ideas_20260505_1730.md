Based on the user complaints and problems presented, here are the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback platforms for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic landing page to promote the app and collect email addresses.
	* Step 3: Design a logo and create social media accounts to build a community.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, a cashback platform can help Nigerians save money and earn rewards.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and development)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage partner stores and customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can manage the platform)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch within a few weeks with a basic version)

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and find reliable savings platforms.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money by offering automated savings plans and investment options.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Users pay a small monthly fee (₦500) for premium features.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and develop a basic savings plan algorithm.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Step 3: Partner with a local bank or financial institution to manage user funds.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the increasing awareness of savings and investment in Nigeria, a user-friendly savings platform can help Nigerians achieve their financial goals.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in development and marketing)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (mostly automated, but some manual work required for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can manage the platform)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch within a few weeks with a basic version)
