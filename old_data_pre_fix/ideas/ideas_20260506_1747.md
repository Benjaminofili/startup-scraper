* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and financial institutions.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback platforms and their revenue models.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using Android and web developers.
 3. Establish partnerships with local businesses and financial institutions.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing infrastructure and partnerships)
