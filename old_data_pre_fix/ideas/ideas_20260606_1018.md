**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple mobile app that tracks and alerts users to their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
	* Step 3: Test the app with a small group of users to gather feedback and iterate.
7. **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" indicates a growing interest in solutions to combat excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, can use existing social media APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but may require ongoing development and support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
