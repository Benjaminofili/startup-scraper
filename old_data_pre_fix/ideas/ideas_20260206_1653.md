**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized productivity tips and reminders.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly app interface using existing AWS resources.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic algorithm to track social media usage and provide personalized feedback.
7. **WHY NOW**: The "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS resources, minimal development costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work required for maintenance)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
