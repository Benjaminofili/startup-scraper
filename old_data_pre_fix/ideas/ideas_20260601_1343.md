3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partnered with local businesses.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with 5 local businesses in Nigeria.
   - Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
   - Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, partnerships with local businesses).
