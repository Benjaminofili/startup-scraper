After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App (NaijaNotes)**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with organizing their notes and study materials, leading to poor academic performance.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app for Android and web, with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students in Nigerian universities (initially targeting 100,000+ students in Lagos-based universities).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app purchase) or ₦2,000/year (subscription model).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research and gather feedback from 100 Nigerian students on their note-taking habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app, focusing on user experience and essential features.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the AWS free tier and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is increasing, and the open-source alternative to Notion or Outline on HackerNews inspired this idea.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (using AWS free tier and open-source resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, but requires marketing efforts)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires testing and iteration)

	* Step 1: Research existing personal finance apps and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface and develop a basic prototype.
	* Step 3: Integrate with popular Nigerian banks and payment gateways for seamless transactions.
7. **WHY NOW**: The need for personal finance management is increasing, and the success of apps like Mint and You Need a Budget (YNAB) on HackerNews inspired this idea.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (using AWS free tier and open-source resources)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions, with potential for partnerships with banks)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and partnerships)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires testing and iteration)
