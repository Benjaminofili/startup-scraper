After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. Digital Credit Repair Service**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to repair their damaged credit scores, often due to errors or unforeseen circumstances.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform offering personalized credit repair plans, credit monitoring, and educational resources.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000/month (basic plan) to ₦20,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit repair landscape.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Create a social media presence to raise awareness about the service.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for personalized plans)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (initially, a small team can manage the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (depending on the complexity of the MVP)

**2. E-paper Dashboard for Nigerian Families**
* **IDEA NAME**: FamilyBoard
* **PROBLEM**: Families struggle to stay organized and connected, often relying on multiple apps and platforms.
* **SOLUTION**: A customizable, web-based e-paper dashboard for families to share information, track events, and stay connected.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerian families with multiple members.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) to ₦10,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing e-paper dashboard solutions and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a unique value proposition for the Nigerian market.
	3. Design a user-friendly interface for the dashboard.
* **WHY NOW**: The idea is inspired by the HackerNews post "I built Timeframe, our family e-paper dashboard".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, potential for passive income)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (small team can manage the platform)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (relatively quick development and launch)
