**1. LibreNotes**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps, lacking a free and open-source alternative.
* **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app for Android and web, with features similar to Notion or Outline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free), with optional donations.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and their features.
	2. Set up a GitHub repository for LibreNotes and create a basic prototype.
	3. Share the prototype on social media and collect feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and open-source alternatives to Notion and Outline is high, as seen in the "Docs – Open source alternative to Notion or Outline" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 10/10 (no capital needed)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly with a basic prototype)
