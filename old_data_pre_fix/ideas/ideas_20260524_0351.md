**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Users struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface using existing Android development skills.
	* Step 3: Integrate a basic tracking feature using Android's built-in APIs.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is highlighted in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post, indicating a need for a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing Android development skills and free APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires occasional updates and maintenance)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed by a single developer)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
