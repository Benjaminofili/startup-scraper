After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with mindless scrolling on social media, wasting time and affecting their mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android Studio, focusing on the core functionality.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about garbage SaaS landing pages and seeking alternatives to Google Products, indicating a desire for innovative solutions that promote digital well-being.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work required)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)

**2. LibreRide Nigeria**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide Nigeria
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable transportation options, seeking affordable alternatives.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing platform, similar to LibreTaxi, tailored to the Nigerian market.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Drivers and passengers who use the platform, with optional in-app payments for premium features.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), ₦100-500 (optional in-app payments)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing platforms in Nigeria, identifying areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using the LibreTaxi open-source code.
	* Step 3: Engage with potential drivers and passengers to gather feedback and validate the idea.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are seeking alternatives to Uber and Lyft, and LibreTaxi's open-source solution can be adapted for the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing open-source code, minimal costs)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (in-app payments, some manual work required)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by 2-3 team members)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within 3-4 months)
