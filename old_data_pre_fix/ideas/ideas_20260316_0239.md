**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are complaining about the negative effects of excessive social media usage, indicating a need for a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but some manual work required for maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
