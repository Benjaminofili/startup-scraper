After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative to Google Products**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google products due to privacy concerns and data ownership.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform offering a suite of open-source, privacy-focused tools and services, including search, email, and cloud storage.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses seeking data privacy and security.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing open-source alternatives to Google products.
 2. Set up a basic website using AWS free tier.
 3. Create a social media presence to raise awareness about NaijaHub.
* **WHY NOW**: The recent Discord announcement requiring face scans or ID for full access has sparked concerns about data privacy, making alternative solutions more appealing.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (using open-source solutions and AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers for scaling)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks)
