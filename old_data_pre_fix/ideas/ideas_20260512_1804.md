Based on the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a basic UI/UX for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Start building the app using Android Studio, focusing on tracking and alert features.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews have expressed frustration with their social media habits, and a solution like Doomscrolling Detector can help them stay focused.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)

**2. Nigerian Product Review Platform**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a reliable platform to review and compare durable products, making informed purchasing decisions difficult.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web platform (using AWS free tier) where users can review and rate products, with a focus on Nigerian market and products.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Online shoppers, particularly those aged 25-45, who want to make informed purchasing decisions.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free for users, revenue from affiliate marketing and sponsored reviews)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing review platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Design a basic website using a website builder (e.g., WordPress).
	* Step 3: Start populating the platform with initial product reviews and ratings.
7. **WHY NOW**: The success of review platforms like Rotten Tomatoes (mentioned on HackerNews) can be replicated in the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (affiliate marketing, sponsored reviews)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members, with some manual work for content creation)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months)
