After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
2. **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score tracking, monitoring, and improvement recommendations.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history, particularly those in the 18-35 age range.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing credit scoring systems in Nigeria and identify potential partnerships.
	* Design a user-friendly interface for the mobile app.
	* Develop a basic prototype to test with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of poor credit scores is evident in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and revenue).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and scoring updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (core team of 2-3, with potential for expansion).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (3-6 months to launch, depending on development and testing).
