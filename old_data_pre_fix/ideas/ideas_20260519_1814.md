**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
 2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using the Android developer.
 3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals and discounts.
* **WHY NOW**: Many users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about passive saving apps and cashback rewards.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but may require some investment for marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (fully automated cashback rewards).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks, but may take months to refine and expand).
