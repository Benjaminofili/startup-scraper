**1. Doomscrolling Stopper**

1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Stopper
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for reducing screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young professionals and students in Nigeria who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as screen time tracking and personalized recommendations.
	* Step 3: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is highlighted in the HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass", indicating a need for a solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources, minimal marketing budget)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a focused effort)
