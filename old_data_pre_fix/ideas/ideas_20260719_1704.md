After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit report changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) seeking to improve their financial health.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦0 (basic features with ads).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand Nigerian credit score management needs.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the CreditPal app using existing APIs.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" highlights the importance of credit score management.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (some development costs, but existing APIs can be leveraged)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional hires for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few months with a basic prototype)
