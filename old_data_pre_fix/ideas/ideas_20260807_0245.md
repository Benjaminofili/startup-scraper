After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Students and professionals in Nigeria struggle with organizing their notes and finding a suitable alternative to Notion or Outline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals in Nigeria, particularly those in the education and consulting sectors.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦2,000/year (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the note-taking habits of Nigerian students and professionals.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with core features like note-taking, tagging, and searching.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest in the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital note-taking solutions is high, and existing alternatives like Notion or Outline may not be affordable or suitable for the Nigerian market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing and server costs).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (the app can generate revenue through subscription models, but may require manual work for customer support and updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 developers and a marketer can handle the initial development and launch).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched within a few weeks, but may require ongoing development and updates).
