After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or using certain services.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards for online purchases, bill payments, or other transactions.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cash back rewards.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cash back).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing cash back platforms and their partnerships.
	* Develop a basic app prototype with a user-friendly interface.
	* Reach out to potential partners (e.g., online stores, bill payment services) to discuss integration.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cash back apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back rewards).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (depends on partnership negotiations and app development).

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and need a convenient, automated solution.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money automatically by rounding up transactions or setting aside a fixed amount regularly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to save money.
5. **PRICE**: ₦100 per month (or 1% of saved amount per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing savings apps and their features.
	* Develop a basic app prototype with automated savings functionality.
	* Partner with banks or financial institutions to enable secure transactions.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and automated savings apps are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for passive income).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated savings and interest on saved amounts).
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (depends on partnership negotiations and app development).

**3. CreditFixNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CreditFixNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians need help improving their credit scores and managing debt.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, debt management tools, and personalized advice for improving creditworthiness.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to improve their credit scores.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (or ₦5,000 per year for premium features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing credit score tracking and debt management apps.
	* Develop a basic app prototype with credit score tracking and debt management features.
	* Partner with credit bureaus or financial institutions to access credit data.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are concerned about their credit scores, and credit management apps are in demand.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (initial development costs, potential for revenue from premium features).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated credit score tracking and debt management).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (depends on partnership negotiations and app development).

**4. SellNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SellNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to sell their old devices or unwanted items, but lack a convenient platform.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that enables users to sell their old devices, electronics, or other items quickly and securely.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Sellers who use the app to sell their items ( commission-based).
5. **PRICE**: 10% commission on sold items (e.g., ₦1,000 on a ₦10,000 sale).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing selling platforms and their fees.
	* Develop a basic app prototype with a user-friendly interface for buying and selling.
	* Establish partnerships with logistics providers for secure transactions.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to sell their unwanted items, and mobile marketplaces are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, potential for revenue from commissions).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated sales and commission earnings).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (depends on partnership negotiations and app development).

**5. PriceCompareNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: PriceCompareNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to compare prices of goods and services across different stores or online platforms.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that enables users to compare prices of items across various stores, online marketplaces, or service providers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to compare prices ( potentially with ads or affiliate marketing).
5. **PRICE**: Free to use, with potential revenue from ads or affiliate marketing.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing price comparison platforms and their features.
	* Develop a basic app prototype with price comparison functionality.
	* Establish partnerships with stores, online marketplaces, or service providers to access pricing data.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and price comparison apps are in demand.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, potential for passive income from ads or affiliate marketing).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated price comparisons and potential ad revenue).
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (depends on partnership negotiations and app development).
