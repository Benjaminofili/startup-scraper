Based on the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative to Google Products (Search Engine)**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaSearch
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google products due to concerns about data privacy and security.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly search engine that indexes Nigerian websites and provides relevant search results.
* **WHO PAYS**: Advertisers (local businesses) and users (optional premium features)
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) - ₦500 (premium features)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing open-source search engine solutions (e.g., Apache Solr).
  2. Set up a basic search engine on AWS free tier.
  3. Index a small set of Nigerian websites to test the search engine.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaints about Google products and the desire for alternatives create a perfect opportunity for a local search engine.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (some development required, but open-source solutions available)
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (ad revenue potential, but requires significant user base)
  - Team Size: 4/10 (requires at least 2-3 team members for development and maintenance)
  - Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched in a few weeks, but requires ongoing development and improvement)

  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched in a few weeks)
