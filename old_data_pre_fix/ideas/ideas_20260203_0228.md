**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste hours scrolling through social media, negatively impacting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users to their excessive social media scrolling, providing tips and resources to help them manage their screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals aged 18-35, students, and young professionals who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Create a content calendar to provide users with helpful tips and resources to manage their screen time.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are looking for solutions to manage their social media addiction (e.g., "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass").
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing AWS free tier and Play Store account)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, but requires ongoing content creation)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional content creators)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic prototype)
