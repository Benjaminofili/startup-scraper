**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and alerts.
**WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (25-45 years old) who are concerned about their credit scores.
**PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100/credit report (one-time payment).
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and existing solutions.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the CreditPal app using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores and the upcoming CreditPal app.
**WHY NOW**: The problem of credit score management is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
