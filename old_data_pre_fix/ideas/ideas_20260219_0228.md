**1. RideShield**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideShield
* **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing services often cancel bookings at the last minute, leaving customers stranded.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows users to rate and review ride-hailing services, promoting accountability and reliability.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies will pay for premium listings and advertising on the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month for premium listings
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular ride-hailing services in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the app, focusing on user reviews and ratings.
 3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss partnership opportunities.
* **WHY NOW**: User complaints on the Play Store highlight the need for a reliable and accountable ride-hailing experience.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the platform, but may require additional help for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (the MVP can be launched quickly, but full features may take longer to develop)
