After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with frequent logouts and login issues on various apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that automatically logs users back into their accounts after a logout or crash.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual app users, with a focus on students and young professionals.
5. **PRICE**: ₦50 per month or ₦10 per transaction.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most affected apps and user demographics.
	* Step 2: Design and develop a basic prototype of the AutoLogin app.
	* Step 3: Test the app with a small group of users to gather feedback and iterate.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store and user complaints about login issues highlight the need for a solution like AutoLogin.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, but potential costs for marketing and maintenance)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
