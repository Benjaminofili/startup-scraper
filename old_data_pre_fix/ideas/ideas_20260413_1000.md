After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and budget, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Review**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LandingPageNaija
2. **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and frustrated customers.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based review and feedback platform for SaaS landing pages, providing actionable insights for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: SaaS companies and entrepreneurs with landing pages.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per review (one-time payment).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Develop a basic web platform using AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 2: Create a social media campaign to promote the service and gather feedback from potential customers.
	* Step 3: Develop a review template and start offering free reviews to the first 10 customers.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint from a user who fixed 6 SaaS landing pages and found them to be "garbage" highlights the need for a review and feedback platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost web development)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (manual review work required)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible)

**2. Open-Source Alternative to Notion or Outline**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking and organization platform with features similar to Notion or Outline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (open-source, free to use).
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic version of the platform using AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 3: Create a community around the platform to gather feedback and contribute to development.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline highlights the demand for such a platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (no initial investment required)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (donations or contributions may be possible)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (development time required)

**3. Interactive Graphs and Globes for Flight Tracking**
1. **IDEA NAME**: FlightTrackerNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Airline pilots and enthusiasts lack interactive and engaging ways to track flights and visualize data.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform providing interactive graphs and globes for flight tracking, using real-time data.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Airline pilots, enthusiasts, and aviation companies.
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month (subscription-based).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Develop a basic web platform using AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 2: Integrate with flight data APIs to provide real-time information.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the service and gather feedback from potential customers.
7. **WHY NOW**: The showcase of interactive graphs and globes for flight tracking on HackerNews highlights the interest in such a platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some development costs required)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 6/10 (development time required)

**4. Rotten Tomatoes-Style Platform for Durable Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: ProductReviewNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Consumers lack a reliable platform to review and compare durable products, such as electronics and appliances.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based review platform for durable products, providing aggregated ratings and reviews.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Product manufacturers and retailers (for premium listings and advertising).
5. **PRICE**: ₦10,000 per month (premium listings), ₦5,000 per month (basic listings).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 2: Integrate with product data APIs to provide comprehensive information.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the service and gather feedback from potential customers.
7. **WHY NOW**: The showcase of a Rotten Tomatoes-style platform for durable products on HackerNews highlights the interest in such a platform.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (some development costs required)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (advertising and premium listings)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 5/10 (development time required)

**5. Anti-Doomscrolling App**
1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrassNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users struggle with doomscrolling and lack a convenient way to track and limit their screen time.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their screen time, providing reminders and incentives to take breaks.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individual users (in-app purchases or subscriptions).
5. **PRICE**: ₦1,000 per month (subscription-based), ₦500 per in-app purchase.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Develop a basic mobile app using Android resources.
	* Step 2: Integrate with device APIs to track screen time and provide personalized feedback.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the service and gather feedback from potential customers.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling and the showcase of an app to stop doomscrolling on HackerNews highlight the need for such an app.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost mobile app development)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (in-app purchases and subscriptions)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible)

These ideas prioritize zero-capital, passive income opportunities and focus on the Nigerian market. They also address specific pain points and needs highlighted by user complaints and showcases on Reddit and HackerNews.
