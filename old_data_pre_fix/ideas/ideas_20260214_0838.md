**1. Alternative SaaS Landing Page Builder**
* **IDEA NAME**: EasyLanding
* **PROBLEM**: Many SaaS landing pages are poorly designed, leading to low conversion rates and frustrated customers.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly landing page builder for SaaS companies, with templates and drag-and-drop functionality.
* **WHO PAYS**: Small to medium-sized SaaS businesses in Nigeria.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month (basic plan) or ₦10,000 per month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify target SaaS companies in Nigeria.
	2. Design a basic template for the landing page builder using existing resources (e.g., AWS free tier).
	3. Reach out to potential customers to gather feedback and validate the idea.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint about poorly designed SaaS landing pages on Reddit highlights the need for a user-friendly solution.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing resources and free tier services)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and template updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks with a basic template and minimal features)
