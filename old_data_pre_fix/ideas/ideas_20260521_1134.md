**1. CashBackNG**

* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income due to limited financial options.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily expenses, such as groceries and transportation, by partnering with local businesses.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards, as well as local businesses that partner with CashBackNG to offer exclusive deals.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use), with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research local businesses and partners to integrate into the app.
	2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
	3. Develop a basic version of the app using the team's existing resources.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing and partnerships may be required)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to manage partnerships and customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff as the app grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some time to develop and test)
