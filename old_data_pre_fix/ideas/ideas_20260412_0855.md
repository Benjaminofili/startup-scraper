5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:

	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app using existing resources (e.g., Android developer).
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and gather feedback from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint "I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" on HackerNews indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage, making this a timely solution.
