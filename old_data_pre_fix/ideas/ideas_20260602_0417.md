Based on the user complaints and problems presented, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1.** 
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit report changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history, as well as financial institutions looking to offer credit products.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
  2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
  3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships and revenue)
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for coaching and support)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional staff for growth)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, with continuous improvement)

* **IDEA NAME**: SellIt

* **WHO PAYS**: Buyers and sellers of used devices, as well as advertisers looking to reach this audience.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/transaction (seller fee), ₦0 (buyer fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing marketplaces and identify areas for improvement.
  2. Develop a basic MVP with core features (listing, searching, and transaction processing).
  3. Establish partnerships with local device repair shops for verification and authentication services.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "If you're thinking of selling your old devices for money, think twice before using..." indicate a need for trustworthy marketplaces.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, with potential for revenue from transaction fees)
  - Passive Income: 8/10 (automated transaction processing, with some manual work for support)
  - Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a small team, with potential for growth)
  - Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks, with continuous improvement)
