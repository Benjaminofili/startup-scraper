After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME:** CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM:** Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION:** A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
* **WHO PAYS:** Individuals and businesses who want to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE:** 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK:**
	1. Research existing cashback platforms and their offerings.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a user-friendly interface.
	3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive cashback deals.
* **WHY NOW:** Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES:**
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing infrastructure)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires partnerships)
