After analyzing the problems from real users on Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
* **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and limits social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who are active on social media.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	2. Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	3. Develop a prototype using Android Studio and integrate a usage tracking library.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is increasingly relevant, and users are seeking solutions to manage their social media usage.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (uses existing AWS free tier and open-source libraries).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (requires occasional updates and maintenance).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 team members).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months).

**2. LibreRide Nigeria**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with expensive and unreliable transportation options.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay a small fee per ride (₦200-₦500), while drivers earn a commission.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use, with optional in-app payments).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research existing transportation options in Nigeria and identify potential partners.
	2. Design a basic UI/UX for the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	3. Develop a prototype using Android Studio and integrate a mapping library.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for affordable and reliable transportation is high, and users are seeking alternatives to existing services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 5/10 (requires some initial investment for marketing and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 4/10 (requires ongoing maintenance and support).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team of 2-3 members).
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within 3-6 months).
