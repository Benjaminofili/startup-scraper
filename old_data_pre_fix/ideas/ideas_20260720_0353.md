**1. [IDEA NAME] CreditPal**
**PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and repair their credit scores, especially when affected by others.
**SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score monitoring, repair guidance, and alerts for suspicious activity.
**WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in their 20s and 30s.
**PRICE**: ₦2,000/month or ₦5,000/year.
**FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the app using Android and web technologies.
	3. Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the importance of credit scores.
**WHY NOW**: The complaint from the 25M individual whose mother damaged their credit score highlights the need for a credit management solution.
**FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (subscription-based model, but some manual work required for support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (initial development requires a team, but maintenance can be solo)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but marketing efforts will take time)
