**1. IDEA NAME:** RouteOpti
**PROBLEM:** Users are looking for ways to reduce their transportation costs by optimizing routes.
**SOLUTION:** A mobile app that helps users find the most cost-effective routes for their trips, taking into account traffic, road conditions, and other factors.
**WHO PAYS:** Individuals and businesses looking to reduce their transportation costs.
**PRICE:** ₦500 per month or ₦50 per transaction.
**FIRST WEEK:**
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current transportation landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic route optimization features.
	3. Partner with local transportation companies to gather data and feedback.
**WHY NOW:** Users are complaining about high transportation costs, and there's a need for a solution that can optimize routes and reduce expenses.
