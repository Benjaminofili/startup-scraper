Based on the user complaints and problems presented, here are the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a reliable platform to earn passive cashback on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards to users on their purchases.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 5 local businesses in Nigeria.
	* Develop a basic mobile app using Android and web developers.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks).
