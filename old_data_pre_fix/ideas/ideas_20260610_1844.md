After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back on their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at partner stores.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cash back on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria.
   - Develop a basic app with the Android developer.
   - Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some investment in marketing).
   - Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system).
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch in a few weeks with a basic app).
