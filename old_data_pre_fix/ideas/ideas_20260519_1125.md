**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income due to limited financial resources.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items, made through partner stores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app, as well as partner stores that pay a commission for each sale made.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases (e.g., ₦50 cashback on a ₦1,000 purchase).
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research and partner with local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	2. Develop a basic mobile app using existing APIs and frameworks.
	3. Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" and "10 Ways to Save Money Passively" posts.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, existing APIs)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (quick development and launch possible)
