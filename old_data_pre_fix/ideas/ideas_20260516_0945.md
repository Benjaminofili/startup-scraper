**1. Demerit System for Ride-hailing Apps**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideSafe
* **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers often drop fares without a valid reason, causing inconvenience to passengers.
* **SOLUTION**: Develop a mobile app that introduces a demerit system to deter drivers from dropping fares without a valid reason.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) would pay for the integration of the demerit system into their platforms.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for each driver on the platform.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the current pain points of ride-hailing companies and drivers.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the demerit system.
 3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
* **WHY NOW**: The complaint on the Play Store about introducing a demerit system highlights the need for a solution to this problem.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:

	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional resources for marketing and sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few months with a focused development effort)
