After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. LibreRide**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are looking for affordable and reliable alternatives to Uber and Lyft.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source ride-hailing app that connects drivers with passengers.
* **WHO PAYS**: Passengers pay a small commission per ride.
* **PRICE**: ₦50-₦100 per ride.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms (e.g., LibreTaxi).
	2. Set up a development environment and start customizing the platform for the Nigerian market.
	3. Reach out to potential drivers and passengers to gather feedback and interest.
* **WHY NOW**: With the dissatisfaction of Uber users (as seen in the PlayStore complaint), there's an opportunity to fill the gap with a more affordable and community-driven alternative.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging open-source platforms reduces development costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for driver and passenger support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more developers and support staff as the platform grows)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, with continuous iteration and improvement)
