After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. RideShare Optimizer**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideShare Optimizer
* **PROBLEM**: Users are unsure how ride-hailing services like Uber calculate routes for shared rides, leading to confusion and potential overcharging.
* **SOLUTION**: An app that provides transparent route calculations and estimates for shared rides, helping users make informed decisions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing users, particularly those who frequently use shared rides.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month or ₦50 per transaction.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand user pain points and preferences.
	2. Develop a basic algorithm to estimate routes and costs for shared rides.
	3. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are already complaining about the lack of transparency in ride-hailing services, and a solution that addresses this issue can gain traction quickly.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing and maintenance).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work may be required to update algorithms and respond to user feedback).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can develop and maintain the app).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version).
