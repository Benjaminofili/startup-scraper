**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype using Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Integrate AWS free tier services for data storage and analytics.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is highlighted in the HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass," indicating a need for a solution.
