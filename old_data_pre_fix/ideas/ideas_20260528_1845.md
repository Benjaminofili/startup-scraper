After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, and some have had their scores negatively affected by others.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track their credit score, provides tips for improvement, and offers alerts for potential score-affecting activities.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with a stable income, aged 25-45, who are concerned about their credit score.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦2,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit score tracking landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit score data.
* **WHY NOW**: The user complaints highlight the importance of credit scores, and with the growing middle class in Nigeria, there's a need for a reliable credit score tracking service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships to reduce costs)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and data updates)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional staff for customer support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few months with a focused development effort)
