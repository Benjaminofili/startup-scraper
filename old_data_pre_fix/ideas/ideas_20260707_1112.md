2. **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to recover from damaged credit scores, like the 25M user whose mother took his credit score from 700+ to 580.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score recovery plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly young adults (18-35 years old).
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) or ₦5,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing credit score recovery platforms and identify key features to include.
	* Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier services.
	* Reach out to financial experts to collaborate on content creation.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of damaged credit scores is prevalent, and users are seeking solutions, as seen on Reddit.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low-cost development, potential partnerships with financial institutions).
