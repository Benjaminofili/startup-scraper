After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop through the app, as well as partner merchants who pay for advertising and promotions.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 spent).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a simple user interface.
	3. Partner with 5 local merchants to offer exclusive deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" post.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be necessary).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards and commission-based revenue).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the app, but partnerships and marketing may require additional help).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (launch can happen within weeks, but partnerships and user acquisition may take time).
