**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their social media usage, providing personalized tips to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly app interface using existing Android development skills.
	* Step 3: Integrate a basic tracking algorithm using APIs to monitor social media usage.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are complaining about the negative effects of excessive social media usage, and there's a growing demand for digital wellness solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (uses existing Android development skills and free APIs)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some manual maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a single developer with some support from the marketer)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic version)
