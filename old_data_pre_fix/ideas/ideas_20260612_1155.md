After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to earn passive cash back on their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at partner stores and online platforms.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and online platforms.
* **PRICE**: 5% cash back on every purchase (₦50 per transaction, with a minimum payout of ₦1,000).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores and online platforms.
 2. Develop a basic app with the Android developer.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and the need for passive income, CashBackNG can capitalize on the demand for cash back apps.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (minimal investment needed for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**2. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to maintain good credit scores and need guidance on credit management.
* **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform that provides credit score tracking, credit monitoring, and personalized credit management advice.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who want to improve their credit scores.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (basic plan) or ₦2,000 per month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and develop a basic credit score tracking system with the web developer.
 2. Create a content marketing strategy to promote the platform.
 3. Partner with a credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: With the increasing importance of credit scores, CreditPal can help Nigerians manage their credit and improve their financial health.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some investment needed for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (recurring subscription-based model)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months)
