After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Alternative to Google Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google products due to concerns about data privacy and security.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform offering a suite of open-source, privacy-focused tools, including search, email, and cloud storage.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses seeking secure and private online services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Research and select open-source alternatives to Google products.
   - Step 2: Set up a basic website using AWS free tier and integrate the selected tools.
   - Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about NaijaHub.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for alternative products is increasing, and Nigeria is a significant market with a growing online population.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (using open-source tools and AWS free tier reduces costs)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and support)
   - Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly using existing open-source tools)

4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking to improve their mental well-being and productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦200/month (basic plan) to ₦1,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).

7. **WHY NOW**: The issue of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly recognized, and Nigerians are looking for solutions to improve their online habits.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 9/10 (can be developed with minimal costs using existing resources)
   - Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through subscription-based models)
   - Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
   - Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched quickly using existing development skills)
