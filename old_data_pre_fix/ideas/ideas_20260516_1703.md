4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and concerned about their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's preferences and pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features, such as scrolling tracking and alerts.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest in the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly common, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media habits.
