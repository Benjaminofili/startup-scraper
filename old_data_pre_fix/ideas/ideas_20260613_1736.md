After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive cash back on their daily expenses.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back on purchases made at partner stores, with a focus on Nigerian markets and brands.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and sign up for the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cash back earned (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cash back).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cash back.
	* Develop a basic app with the Android developer.
	* Create a social media campaign with the marketer to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cash back apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial costs for marketing and development, but can start small)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cash back system)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires some team effort for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)

**2. LibreSpend**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreSpend
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a free and open-source alternative to expensive personal finance management tools.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web-based platform for tracking expenses, creating budgets, and setting financial goals, with a focus on Nigerian users.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (free and open-source, with optional donations).
5. **PRICE**: Free.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing open-source personal finance tools and adapt them for Nigerian users.
	* Develop a basic web platform with the web developer.
	* Create a community forum for users to provide feedback and suggestions.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for free and open-source alternatives to expensive financial tools.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 10/10 (no initial costs, can use existing open-source tools)
	* Passive Income: 5/10 (optional donations, but no guaranteed revenue)
	* Team Size: 5/10 (requires some team effort for development and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch a basic platform within days)

**3. CreditCoach**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CreditCoach
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to maintain good credit scores and lack access to credit education resources.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, education, and coaching, with a focus on Nigerian credit systems.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who sign up for the app and want to improve their credit scores.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (or ₦5,000 per year) for premium features.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research Nigerian credit systems and develop a basic credit score tracking system.
	* Partner with a local credit bureau to provide accurate credit scores.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to improve their credit scores and access better financial opportunities.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 7/10 (some initial costs for development and marketing, but can start small)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (recurring subscription fees)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires some team effort for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but requires partnerships with credit bureaus)
