**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per ₦1000 cashback).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their features.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	3. Partner with local businesses to offer exclusive deals.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the "16 Apps That Will Earn You Passive Cash Back" and "10 Ways to Save Money Passively" posts.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for high returns)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch an MVP within weeks, but requires ongoing development)
