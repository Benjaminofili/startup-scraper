**1.** 
* **[IDEA NAME]**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income due to limited financial resources.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily expenses, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who use the app to earn cashback rewards.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 cashback reward = ₦2.50 commission).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their features.
	2. Develop a basic prototype of the CashBackNG app using the Android developer.
	3. Partner with local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit posts about passive saving apps and cashback rewards.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging existing infrastructure)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and development)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
