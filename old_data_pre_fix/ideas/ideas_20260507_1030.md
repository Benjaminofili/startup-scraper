After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a reliable platform to earn passive cashback rewards for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that partners with local businesses to offer cashback rewards to users for their purchases.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and list potential business partners.
 2. Develop a basic app prototype using existing APIs.
 3. Create a social media campaign to generate buzz.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing APIs and partnerships).
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards).
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (requires some business development and marketing efforts).
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks).

**2. SaveNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and lack access to automated savings tools.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that helps users set savings goals and automatically deducts a fixed amount from their accounts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Users (subscription-based).
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month (basic plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand user savings habits.
 2. Develop a basic web app prototype.
 3. Integrate with local banks for automated transactions.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, as seen in the Reddit threads about passive saving and budgeting.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low development costs).
	+ Passive Income: 10/10 (subscription-based revenue).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires some development and marketing efforts).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within months).
