After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. Doomscrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: Doomscrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through their social media feeds without realizing it.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple Android app that tracks and alerts users about their screen time, providing personalized recommendations to reduce doomscrolling.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing design tools.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic prototype using Android Studio and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly common, and users are looking for solutions to help them stay focused.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be developed with existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (requires some maintenance and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 people)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
