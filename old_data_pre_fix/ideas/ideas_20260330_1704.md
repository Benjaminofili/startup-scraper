After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback apps for online shopping.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback on online purchases from popular Nigerian e-commerce sites.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Online shoppers in Nigeria, particularly students and young adults.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback earnings (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with 3-5 Nigerian e-commerce sites.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for cashback apps is high, with 16 apps mentioned in the Reddit complaints.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but may require some marketing expenses).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with a small team).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks).
