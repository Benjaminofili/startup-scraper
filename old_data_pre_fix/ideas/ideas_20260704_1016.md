**1. Credit Score Tracker**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditScoreNG
* **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to maintain a good credit score, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and provides tips to improve credit scores.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor credit scores, ₦500/month.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on existing credit score tracking apps.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
 3. Develop a basic prototype using AWS free tier.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" are struggling with credit score issues.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (uses AWS free tier)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (requires occasional updates and maintenance)
   - Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by 1-2 people)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)
