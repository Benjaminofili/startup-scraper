After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to repair and maintain their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score monitoring, reporting, and repair services, with personalized advice and alerts.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with damaged credit scores, particularly those in the 25-45 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan), ₦5,000/month (premium plan).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and identify potential partners.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score monitoring features.
 3. Establish a social media presence to raise awareness and generate interest.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of credit score damage is highlighted in the Reddit post "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?"
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are relatively low)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and credit score analysis)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be launched with a small team, but may require additional staff for growth)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few months with a focused development effort)
