After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who make purchases through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% cashback on all purchases (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	2. Develop a basic prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to partner for cashback offers.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with businesses)
	+ Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development, marketing, and business development)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic version within weeks, but requires continuous improvement)
