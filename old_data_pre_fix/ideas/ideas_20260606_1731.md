After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for the Nigerian market. Here they are:

**1. Digital Product Review Platform**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaReviews
* **PROBLEM**: Consumers in Nigeria lack a reliable platform to find honest reviews of durable products before making a purchase.
* **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that allows users to rate and review products, with a focus on durability and value for money.
* **WHO PAYS**: Consumers and businesses looking to advertise on the platform.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month for businesses, free for consumers.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify the most popular product categories in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a basic review system.
 3. Reach out to influencers and bloggers in Nigeria to promote the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for honest product reviews is high, and existing platforms like Rotten Tomatoes are not focused on the Nigerian market.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (can start with a basic MVP)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (will require some manual work to moderate reviews)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but will need to grow to handle user acquisition and moderation)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)
