After analyzing the problems from real users, I have identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **Digital Savings Platform**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money passively.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money automatically through a rounded-up change feature or a regular savings plan.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians looking to save money.
* **PRICE**: ₦50 - ₦100 per month (subscription-based).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the savings habits of Nigerians.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app.
 3. Develop a basic prototype using the AWS free tier.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of digital payments in Nigeria, there is a growing need for digital savings solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, leveraging AWS free tier)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model, potential for automated savings)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (small team needed, but requires some expertise in finance and development)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but requires testing and iteration)

**2.** **Alternative Credit Scoring**
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to access credit due to limited credit history.
* **SOLUTION**: A platform that uses alternative data sources (e.g., social media, phone usage) to provide credit scores for individuals and small businesses.
* **WHO PAYS**: Financial institutions, lenders, and individuals seeking credit.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦1,000 per credit score report.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research alternative credit scoring models and their applications in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a data collection framework using social media and phone usage data.
 3. Establish partnerships with financial institutions to pilot the platform.
* **WHY NOW**: The need for alternative credit scoring is growing, especially in Nigeria where traditional credit scoring models are limited.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some capital needed for data collection and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 7/10 (potential for automated credit scoring and reporting)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed, but requires expertise in finance, data science, and development)
	+ Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within a few months, but requires testing and iteration)

* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals seeking to upskill or reskill, as well as businesses looking to train their employees.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 - ₦10,000 per course.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to identify in-demand digital skills in Nigeria.
 2. Develop a content creation framework for courses and training programs.
 3. Establish partnerships with industry experts to provide instruction and mentorship.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for digital skills is growing rapidly in Nigeria, driven by the increasing adoption of technology.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 7/10 (low capital needed, leveraging online platforms and partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (potential for automated course sales and subscriptions)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed, but requires expertise in digital skills and education)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but requires content creation and testing)

**5.** **Buy Nothing Group Alternative**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are dissatisfied with the existing Buy Nothing groups, citing issues with trust, safety, and convenience.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects people who want to give away or receive items for free, with a focus on building a trusted and safe community.
* **WHO PAYS**: None (free to use, with potential for advertising or sponsored content).
* **PRICE**: Free.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the needs and pain points of Buy Nothing group users.
 2. Design a user-friendly interface for the app, with a focus on trust, safety, and convenience.
 3. Develop a basic prototype using the AWS free tier.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for alternative Buy Nothing groups is growing, driven by the increasing dissatisfaction with existing platforms.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (low capital needed, leveraging AWS free tier and free-to-use model)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (potential for advertising or sponsored content, but requires significant user base)
	+ Team Size: 5/10 (small team needed, but requires expertise in development and community building)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, with rapid iteration and testing)
