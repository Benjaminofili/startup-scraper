After analyzing the user complaints and problems from various sources, I have identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. RideSafe**
1. **IDEA NAME**: RideSafe
2. **PROBLEM**: Riders are dissatisfied with the current ride-hailing services, citing poor treatment of drivers and increased prices.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that connects riders with drivers, promoting fair prices and better treatment of drivers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders and drivers (commission-based)
5. **PRICE**: ₦50-₦100 per ride (commission)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the needs and preferences of Nigerian riders and drivers.
	* Step 2: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) for the RideSafe app using the existing Android and web development skills.
	* Step 3: Launch a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaints about Uber on the PlayStore and the desire for alternatives to Google Products on HackerNews indicate a need for a new ride-hailing solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can start with minimal investment, leveraging existing resources)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and marketing)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional members for growth)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
