Based on the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps, seeking an open-source alternative to Notion or Outline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features like organization, tagging, and collaboration.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦500-₦1000 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Set up a GitHub repository and create a basic app structure.
	* Step 3: Design a user-friendly interface and begin building the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to Notion and Outline is increasing, and Nigerian students can benefit from a free, customizable note-taking solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (no initial investment needed, using AWS free tier)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (depending on the complexity of features)
