**1. Alternative to Google Products**
1. **IDEA NAME**: NaijaHub
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerian users are looking for alternatives to Google products due to concerns about data privacy and security.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that offers a suite of open-source, end-to-end encrypted alternatives to Google Products, such as search, email, and cloud storage.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses in Nigeria who value data privacy and security.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) to ₦2,000/month (premium plan).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
    * Day 1-2: Research existing open-source alternatives to Google products and evaluate their feasibility for the Nigerian market.
    * Day 3-4: Set up a basic website and social media presence to promote the service.
    * Day 5-7: Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints on HackerNews and Reddit indicate a growing demand for alternatives to Google products, and the Nigerian market is ripe for a local solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
    * Investment: 8/10 (₦0 needed, but some costs for marketing and server maintenance)
