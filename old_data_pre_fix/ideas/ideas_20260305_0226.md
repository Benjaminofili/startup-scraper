After analyzing the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints (zero budget, Nigerian market, and focus on passive income). Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable cashback and rewards platforms for their daily purchases.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback and rewards for users who shop at partnered stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Partnered stores and brands pay for advertising and customer acquisition.
* **PRICE**: 5% commission on each transaction (₦50 per ₦1000 spent).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research and list potential partner stores in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract early users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, and CashBackNG can capitalize on this trend.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial investment needed, but may require some marketing budget).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (automated cashback and rewards system).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but may require more time to refine and expand).
