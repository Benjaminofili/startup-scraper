**1. LibreNotes**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle with expensive and limited note-taking apps, seeking an open-source alternative to Notion or Outline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free, open-source note-taking app with features similar to Notion or Outline, tailored to Nigerian students' needs.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, educators, and professionals seeking affordable note-taking solutions.
5. **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦100-₦500 per month).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
	* Step 2: Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source frameworks.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to gather feedback and interest from potential users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and Nigerian students are seeking affordable solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (leveraging existing open-source frameworks and free resources)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team)
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within weeks)
