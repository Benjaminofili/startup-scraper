**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
2. **PROBLEM**: Customers feel disrespected by companies, and there's no easy way to provide feedback.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review companies based on their customer service experience.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Companies will pay for premium features, such as detailed analytics and alerts for negative reviews.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for premium features.
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to identify the most common customer pain points.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and user-friendly app interface.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team.
7. **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint about bad service and disrespect for customers highlights the need for a platform like RespectMeter.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing and maintenance costs may arise)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for moderation and customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback)
