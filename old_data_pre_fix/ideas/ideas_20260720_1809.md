After analyzing the user complaints and problems from HackerNews and Reddit, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that cater to the Nigerian market, focusing on zero-capital, passive income opportunities. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
* **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
* **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
* **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback reward per transaction (no monthly fee).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Research and partner with 5 local stores in Nigeria to offer cashback rewards.
 2. Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer on the team.
 3. Create a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are gaining popularity.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (no initial capital needed, but some marketing expenses).
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (automated cashback rewards, but some manual work for partner management).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within a few weeks).
