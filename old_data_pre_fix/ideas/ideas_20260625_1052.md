**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals, aged 18-45, who regularly make online transactions.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing cashback platforms and their revenue models.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using AWS free tier services.
	* Partner with local online merchants to offer exclusive deals.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, a cashback platform can capitalize on this trend and provide a unique value proposition.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (leveraging AWS free tier and existing infrastructure)
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)
