After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. DoomScrolling Detector**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScrolling Detector
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that tracks and alerts users about their excessive social media usage, providing tips to reduce screen time.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (in-app subscription).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits.
	* Step 2: Design a simple, user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using Android and APIs.
7. **WHY NOW**: The complaint about doomscrolling on HackerNews ("Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass") indicates a growing concern about excessive social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (in-app subscription, some manual work for updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but marketing efforts may require additional help).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks).
