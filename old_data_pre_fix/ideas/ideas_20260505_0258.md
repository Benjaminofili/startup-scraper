After analyzing the user complaints and problems, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% - 10% cashback reward per transaction (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic mobile app using the Android developer.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to earn passive income, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, but some marketing expenses).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated cashback rewards, but some manual work for partner management).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team to manage partnerships and marketing).
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks, but requires ongoing development).
