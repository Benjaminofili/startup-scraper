After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1.** **CreditPal**
* **PROBLEM**: Poor credit score management leads to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, monitoring, and improvement tips.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or no credit history (₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per month).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000 per month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research on existing credit score management apps in Nigeria.
	2. Define the app's features and user interface.
	3. Assign a developer to start building the app.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for passive income).
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support).
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (at least 2-3 team members needed for development and marketing).
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within 2-3 months).
