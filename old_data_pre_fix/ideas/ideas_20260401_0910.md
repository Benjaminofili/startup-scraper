**1. DoomScroll Shield**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScroll Shield
2. **PROBLEM**: Many users struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young professionals and students in Nigeria, aged 18-30, who are active on social media.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a user-friendly interface for the app, incorporating features such as usage tracking, goal setting, and personalized recommendations.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using the Android developer on the team, focusing on core features and functionality.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of excessive social media scrolling is increasingly relevant, as seen in the "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" post on HackerNews.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment for marketing and server costs).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (the app can generate revenue through subscription fees, but may require ongoing maintenance and updates).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can develop and maintain the app, but may require additional help for marketing and customer support).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (the app can be launched quickly, but may require some time for testing and iteration).
