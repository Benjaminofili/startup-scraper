**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: TouchGrass
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users set goals, track progress, and receive reminders to take breaks and engage in physical activities.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30) who are active on social media and willing to pay for a tool to improve their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a simple and user-friendly interface for the app.
	* Step 3: Develop a basic version of the app using existing APIs and frameworks.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is becoming increasingly prevalent, and users are seeking solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (can be developed with minimal investment)

	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)
