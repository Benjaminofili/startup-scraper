**1. RideHail Feedback**
* **IDEA NAME**: RideHail Feedback
* **PROBLEM**: Ride-hailing drivers in Nigeria often reject rides, leaving passengers stranded and frustrated.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that allows passengers to rate and provide feedback on their ride-hailing experiences, helping to improve driver behavior and accountability.
* **WHO PAYS**: Ride-hailing companies (e.g., Uber, Bolt) could pay for access to the feedback data to improve their services.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 per month for basic analytics, ₦2,000 per month for premium insights.
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the ride-hailing landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) of the app using the Android developer.
	3. Reach out to ride-hailing companies to discuss potential partnerships.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore complaint highlights the need for a solution to address the poor behavior of ride-hailing drivers.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some marketing budget)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to maintain the app and analyze feedback)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and partnerships)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some time to develop partnerships with ride-hailing companies)
