After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cashback rewards when shopping online or offline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, partner stores, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (₦50 per transaction).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local stores to offer cashback rewards.
	* Develop a basic app using the Android developer on the team.
	* Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but no significant capital required)
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (some manual work required for partner management and customer support)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team can manage the app, but more developers may be needed for scaling)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic app within weeks)
