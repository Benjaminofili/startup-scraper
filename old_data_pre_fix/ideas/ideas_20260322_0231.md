**Problems Analyzed:** 150

After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students:

### 1. **EduReview**
- **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students struggle to find reliable reviews of educational products and services.
- **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app for reviewing and rating educational institutions, courses, and resources.
- **WHO PAYS**: Students, parents, and educational institutions (for premium listings).
- **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic), ₦2,000/month (premium).
- **FIRST WEEK**:
  1. Conduct market research to identify the most in-demand features.
  2. Design a basic prototype of the web and mobile app.
  3. Create a content calendar for social media marketing.
- **WHY NOW**: The complaints about poorly designed SaaS landing pages and the need for open-source alternatives highlight the demand for reliable educational resources.
- **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing AWS free tier and Play Store account).
  - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content moderation).
  - Team Size: 7/10 (can be managed with the current team size).
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks).

### 2. **NaijaTaxi**
- **PROBLEM**: Nigerians face difficulties in finding reliable and affordable transportation options.
- **SOLUTION**: A mobile app for hailing taxis and ride-sharing, with a focus on safety and affordability.
- **WHO PAYS**: Riders (per trip) and drivers (commission-based).
- **PRICE**: ₦200/ride (minimum), 10% commission for drivers.
- **FIRST WEEK**:
  1. Research existing taxi-hailing services in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
  2. Develop a basic prototype of the mobile app.
  3. Establish partnerships with local taxi drivers and unions.
- **WHY NOW**: The mention of LibreTaxi, an open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, highlights the demand for affordable and reliable transportation options.
- **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 5/10 (some investment required for marketing and driver acquisition).
  - Passive Income: 4/10 (manual work required for customer support and driver management).
  - Team Size: 6/10 (may require additional team members for driver management).
  - Time to Market: 6/10 (may take months to launch and gain traction).
