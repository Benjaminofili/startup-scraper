After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. DoomScroll**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScroll
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people waste time scrolling through social media, affecting their productivity and mental health.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing features like app blocking, time tracking, and personalized recommendations.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, young professionals, and individuals who want to improve their productivity and mental well-being.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	* Step 2: Design a minimal viable product (MVP) with basic features.
	* Step 3: Develop a landing page to collect email addresses and gauge interest.
7. **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to help them manage their social media usage.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, but some marketing expenses)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support and updates)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed with a small team, but may require additional help for marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic MVP)

**2. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face difficulties with transportation, and existing ride-hailing services can be expensive.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, allowing users to find and share rides with others.
4. **WHO PAYS**: None (free service, with optional donations or sponsored content).
5. **PRICE**: Free
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing open-source ride-hailing platforms and adapt them for the Nigerian market.
	* Step 2: Develop a community around the platform to encourage user contributions and feedback.
	* Step 3: Launch a pilot program in a specific city or region to test the service.
7. **WHY NOW**: The need for affordable transportation options is pressing, and users are looking for alternatives to existing services.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low development costs, as the platform can be built on existing open-source software)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (some manual work required for community management and updates)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for development and community management)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few months with a pilot program)
