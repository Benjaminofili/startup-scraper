After analyzing the problems from real users on HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Open Source Note-Taking App**
* **IDEA NAME**: NoteHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many users are looking for open-source alternatives to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, open-source note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and collaboration.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and professionals who need a free, customizable note-taking solution.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional donations (₦0 - ₦500/month)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing open-source note-taking apps and identify key features to include.
  2. Set up a GitHub repository and start building the app using existing open-source libraries.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the rise of digital note-taking, there's a growing demand for open-source, customizable solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 9/10 (free open-source libraries and GitHub repository)
  - Passive Income: 8/10 (donations and potential ads)
  - Team Size: 8/10 (can be built by 1-2 developers)
  - Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched in weeks)

**2. Alternative to Google Photos**
* **IDEA NAME**: PhotoSafe
* **PROBLEM**: Users are looking for end-to-end encrypted alternatives to Google Photos for secure photo storage.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, end-to-end encrypted photo storage app with features like automatic uploading and sharing.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and families who value photo security and privacy.
* **PRICE**: Free (up to 1GB storage), ₦500/month (up to 10GB storage)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing end-to-end encryption libraries and photo storage solutions.
  2. Set up an AWS free tier account for storage and start building the app.
  3. Create a landing page to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With growing concerns about photo security and privacy, there's a demand for secure, encrypted photo storage solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 8/10 (free encryption libraries and AWS free tier)
  - Passive Income: 9/10 (subscription-based model)
  - Team Size: 7/10 (requires 1-2 developers and a marketer)
  - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched in weeks)

**4. LibreTaxi Alternative for Nigerian Transport**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaRide
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian commuters lack a reliable, affordable, and safe transportation platform.
* **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, with features like real-time tracking and in-app payments.
* **WHO PAYS**: Commuters who want a safe and affordable transportation solution, and drivers who want to increase their earnings.
* **PRICE**: ₦50 - ₦200 per ride (depending on distance)
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
  1. Research existing transportation platforms and identify key features to include.
  2. Set up a web app using existing open-source libraries and start building the platform.
  3. Create a social media campaign to gather interest and feedback from potential users.
* **WHY NOW**: With the growth of the Nigerian transportation sector, there's a need for a reliable, affordable, and safe transportation platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
  - Investment: 7/10 (requires some initial investment for marketing and driver onboarding)
  - Passive Income: 8/10 (transaction fees)
  - Team Size: 6/10 (requires 2-3 developers, a marketer, and a driver coordinator)
  - Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched in months)
