2. **PROBLEM**: Users struggle to find affordable alternatives for grocery shopping and household items.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that aggregates discounts, coupons, and affordable alternatives for everyday items, with a focus on Nigerian markets.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians, students, and families looking to save money on household expenses.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦50/per transaction (affiliate commissions).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing coupon and discount platforms in Nigeria.
	* Develop a basic app prototype using the Android developer.
	* Reach out to local businesses to partner for discounts and promotions.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and the Nigerian market lacks a comprehensive platform for discounts and coupons.
