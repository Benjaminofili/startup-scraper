**1. DoomScroll**
1. **IDEA NAME**: DoomScroll
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with excessive social media scrolling, affecting their mental health and productivity.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing personalized recommendations for improvement.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Young adults (18-30 years old) who are active on social media and concerned about their mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (subscription-based model)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research to understand the target audience's social media habits and concerns.
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype of the app, focusing on user experience and key features.
	* Step 3: Develop a content marketing strategy to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users on Reddit and HackerNews are discussing the negative effects of excessive social media usage, indicating a growing need for solutions like DoomScroll.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing expenses may be required)
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (subscription-based model, but some manual work may be needed for customer support)
	* Team Size: 7/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can handle development and marketing)
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)

**2. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face difficulties with transportation, including high costs and lack of reliable options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers and promoting affordable transportation.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders who use the platform, with optional tips for drivers.
5. **PRICE**: ₦0 (free to use, with optional tips)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research existing transportation options in Nigeria and identify areas for improvement.
	* Step 2: Develop a basic prototype of the platform, focusing on user experience and key features.
	* Step 3: Partner with local drivers and organizations to promote the platform.
7. **WHY NOW**: The success of LibreTaxi on HackerNews indicates a growing interest in alternative transportation solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs are low, and the platform can be launched with minimal investment)
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (the platform can generate revenue through tips and advertising)
	* Team Size: 6/10 (a small team of 2-3 people can handle development and marketing, but partnerships with drivers and organizations may require additional support)
	* Time to Market: 7/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype, but partnerships and promotions may take longer)
