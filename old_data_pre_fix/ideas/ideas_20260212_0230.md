**1. Alternative to Google Products (NaijaSearch)**
* **IDEA NAME**: NaijaSearch
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians are looking for alternatives to Google Products due to concerns about data privacy and security.
* **SOLUTION**: A search engine and productivity tool suite that offers a free, open-source, and secure alternative to Google Products, tailored to the Nigerian market.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and small businesses in Nigeria who value data privacy and security.
* **PRICE**: ₦0 (free) for basic services, ₦500/month for premium features.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the needs of Nigerian users.
 2. Set up a basic website and social media presence to promote the idea.
 3. Start building a community of users and developers to contribute to the project.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for alternative search engines and productivity tools is increasing, and Nigeria is a large market with a growing need for secure and private online services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but may require some investment in marketing and infrastructure)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for maintenance and updates, but can be automated to some extent)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require some time for development and testing)
