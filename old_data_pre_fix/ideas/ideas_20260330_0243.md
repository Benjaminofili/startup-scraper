**1. BugFix**
1. **IDEA NAME**: BugFix
2. **PROBLEM**: Many app users experience frustrating bugs and glitches, such as logging out and being unable to log back in.
3. **SOLUTION**: A simple, user-friendly app testing and feedback platform that connects developers with beta testers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: App developers and companies looking to test their apps.
5. **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month for basic testing services.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Create a simple website using AWS free tier to promote the service.
   - Step 2: Reach out to local app developers and companies to offer free trial testing services.
   - Step 3: Develop a basic app testing and feedback platform using existing APIs.
7. **WHY NOW**: The most recent app update on the Play Store has a bug, indicating a need for reliable app testing services.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (uses existing resources and free tier services)
   - Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for testing and feedback)
   - Team Size: 8/10 (can be managed with a small team)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks)

**3. LibreRide**
1. **IDEA NAME**: LibreRide
2. **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians face challenges with transportation, including high costs and limited options.
3. **SOLUTION**: A free and open-source alternative to Uber and Lyft, connecting riders with local drivers.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Riders pay a small fee per ride, while drivers earn a commission.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500 per ride.
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Step 1: Research existing ride-hailing services and their limitations.
   - Step 2: Develop a basic prototype using existing APIs and web development skills.
   - Step 3: Partner with local drivers to test the service.
7. **WHY NOW**: The LibreTaxi post on HackerNews indicates a growing interest in open-source transportation solutions.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 6/10 (requires some investment in marketing and driver partnerships)
   - Passive Income: 4/10 (requires manual work for driver management and customer support)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires a team to manage drivers and customer support)
   - Time to Market: 6/10 (can be launched within a few months)
