After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the team's resources and the Nigerian market. Here are the ideas:

**1. CreditPal**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditPal
* **PROBLEM**: Individuals struggle to manage and improve their credit scores, leading to financial difficulties.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides credit score tracking, personalized financial advice, and alerts for credit report changes.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor or fair credit scores, particularly those in the 25-40 age range.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium features) or ₦100 per credit report request.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the current credit scoring landscape in Nigeria.
	2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic credit score tracking features.
	3. Partner with a local credit bureau to access credit data.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit management tools.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for partnerships)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require additional hires)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic MVP within weeks)
