**1.** **Financial Literacy App**
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with managing their finances and credit scores.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that provides personalized financial advice, credit score tracking, and educational resources.
* **WHO PAYS**: Middle-class Nigerians (₦50,000 - ₦200,000 monthly income) seeking to improve their financial literacy.
* **PRICE**: ₦500 - ₦2,000 per month (subscription-based).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's financial concerns.
 2. Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with basic features.
 3. Partner with financial experts to create engaging content.
* **WHY NOW**: The problems from real users highlight the need for financial guidance, as seen in "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?".
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (leveraging existing resources and expertise).
