Based on the user complaints and problems from Reddit and HackerNews, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Doomscrolling Prevention App**

1. **IDEA NAME**: ScrollLess
2. **PROBLEM**: Many people, especially students, waste a lot of time scrolling through social media, leading to decreased productivity and increased stress.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users track and limit their social media usage, providing features like website blocking, time tracking, and personalized productivity tips.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Students and young professionals aged 18-30 who want to improve their productivity and mental health.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Conduct market research and analyze competitors (e.g., Freedom, SelfControl).
	* Step 2: Design a basic prototype using Android Studio and AWS free tier resources.
	* Step 3: Create a social media campaign to raise awareness about the app and its benefits.
7. **WHY NOW**: The HackerNews post "Show HN: I built an app to stop me doomscrolling by touching grass" indicates a growing interest in solutions to combat mindless scrolling.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, using existing resources).
	* Passive Income: 7/10 (subscription-based model, but requires ongoing development and support).
	* Team Size: 6/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing and maintenance).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks, but may require iterations based on user feedback).

4. **WHO PAYS**: Students, parents, and schools seeking to improve academic performance.
5. **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) or ₦5,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Step 1: Research popular subjects and topics in the Nigerian education system.

	* Step 3: Design a basic platform using web development frameworks like React or Angular.
7. **WHY NOW**: The demand for online learning is increasing, and a platform catering to Nigerian students can fill a significant gap in the market.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 6/10 (requires content creation and partnerships, but can be developed with low costs).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (subscription-based model with potential for high revenue).
