After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Digital Productivity Coach**
* **IDEA NAME**: FocusHub
* **PROBLEM**: Many people struggle with staying focused and avoiding doomscrolling on social media.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users stay focused and productive by providing personalized coaching, reminders, and rewards.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students, freelancers, and remote workers who need to stay focused and productive.
* **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan), ₦1,000/month (premium plan)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Conduct market research to understand the target audience's needs and preferences.
	2. Design a basic prototype of the app using Figma or Adobe XD.
	3. Develop a content calendar for social media marketing and user engagement.
* **WHY NOW**: The problem of doomscrolling and lack of focus is prevalent, and users are looking for solutions to stay productive.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (low capital needed, can start with a basic prototype)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for content creation and user support)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for marketing and user support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch a basic prototype within weeks)

* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/course (basic plan), ₦5,000/course (premium plan)
* **FIRST WEEK**:
	1. Research popular online course platforms and identify gaps in the market.
	2. Develop a content strategy and create a list of potential course topics.
	3. Partner with industry experts to create high-quality course content.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for online learning is increasing, and Nigerians are looking for affordable and accessible ways to acquire new skills.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 6/10 (some capital needed for course creation and marketing)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (can generate revenue through course sales and affiliate marketing)
	+ Team Size: 6/10 (can start with a small team, but may need to expand for course creation and marketing)
	+ Time to Market: 7/10 (can launch a basic platform within months)
