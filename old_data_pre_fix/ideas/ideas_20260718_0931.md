After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. Credit Score Repair Guide**
* **IDEA NAME**: CreditFix
* **PROBLEM**: Many Nigerians struggle with poor credit scores, limiting their access to loans and financial services.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app offering personalized credit score improvement plans, credit monitoring, and financial education.
* **WHO PAYS**: Individuals with poor credit scores, particularly those in urban areas (e.g., Lagos, Abuja).
* **PRICE**: ₦2,000/month (basic plan) or ₦5,000/month (premium plan with personalized coaching).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to understand the Nigerian credit scoring system and common pain points.
	2. Develop a basic credit score improvement plan and content for the app.
	3. Design a user-friendly interface for the app using existing templates.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "My mother took my 700+ credit to a 580. What should I do?" indicate a need for credit score repair services.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs are low, but marketing may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for personalized coaching, but basic plans can be automated)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the app, but may require additional staff for marketing and support)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic plan)

* **WHO PAYS**: Freelancers ( commission-based) and clients (service fees).
* **PRICE**: 10% commission on freelance work completed through the platform ( ₦1,000 minimum per transaction).
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Research existing freelance platforms and identify areas for improvement.
	2. Develop a basic platform using existing frameworks (e.g., WordPress, Laravel).
	3. Create a social media campaign to attract initial users.
* **WHY NOW**: Users like "I've failed as a freelancer" and "My experience getting my first few clients, and 5 tips" indicate a need for a reliable freelance platform.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (initial development costs are low, and the platform can be launched with minimal investment)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (commission-based model can generate passive income, but some manual work required for support)

	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic plan)
