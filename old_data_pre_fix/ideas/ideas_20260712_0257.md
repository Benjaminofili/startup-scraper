After analyzing the problems and ideas from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the details for each idea:

**1. LibreNotes**
* **IDEA NAME**: LibreNotes
* **PROBLEM**: Nigerian students need a free and open-source alternative to Notion or Outline for note-taking and organization.
* **SOLUTION**: A simple, web-based note-taking app with features like tagging, searching, and organization.
* **WHO PAYS**: Students and educators in Nigeria, potentially with ads or sponsored content.
* **PRICE**: Free, with optional premium features for ₦500/month.
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
 1. Conduct market research on existing note-taking apps in Nigeria.
 2. Define the core features and user interface for LibreNotes.
 3. Set up a basic website and GitHub repository for the project.
* **WHY NOW**: The demand for open-source alternatives to popular apps is increasing, and Nigerian students are looking for affordable solutions.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 9/10 (no initial capital needed)
	+ Passive Income: 8/10 (ads and sponsored content can generate revenue)
	+ Team Size: 8/10 (can be developed and maintained by a small team)
	+ Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within weeks)
