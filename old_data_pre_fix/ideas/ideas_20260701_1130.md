**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians want to earn passive cash back when shopping online or offline.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cash back rewards for purchases made at partner stores, both online and offline.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Partner stores pay a commission for each sale made through the app.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cash back on purchases (e.g., ₦500 cash back on a ₦10,000 purchase).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with 5 local stores to offer cash back rewards.
   - Develop a basic app using the AWS free tier and Android/web development skills.
   - Promote the app on social media to gain initial users.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping in Nigeria, users are looking for ways to save money and earn rewards.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
