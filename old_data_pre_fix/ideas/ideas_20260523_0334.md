After analyzing the user complaints and considering the team's resources and constraints, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for online purchases, bill payments, and other transactions.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals, businesses, and organizations that want to incentivize customers to use their services.
5. **PRICE**: ₦50-₦200 per transaction (depending on the partner merchant).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research and partner with local businesses to offer cashback rewards.
	* Develop a basic mobile app using existing APIs and frameworks.
	* Launch a social media campaign to promote the app and attract users.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money and earn passive income, as seen in the Reddit threads about cashback apps and passive saving.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (some initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with partner merchants).
	* Passive Income: 9/10 (automated cashback rewards and potential for affiliate marketing).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (initial development requires a small team, but can be maintained by a solo developer).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks, but requires ongoing marketing and partnership efforts).
