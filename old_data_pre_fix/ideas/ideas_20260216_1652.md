4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals and businesses concerned about data privacy, willing to pay for a premium search experience.
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (individuals), ₦5,000/month (businesses)
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing open-source search engines (e.g., DuckDuckGo) and evaluate their feasibility for the Nigerian market.
	* Set up a basic website and social media presence to promote the service.
	* Reach out to potential partners (e.g., cybersecurity experts) to collaborate on the project.
7. **WHY NOW**: The user complaints on HackerNews and Reddit highlight the need for alternative, privacy-focused solutions.
