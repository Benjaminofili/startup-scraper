Based on the user complaints and the team's resources, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here they are:

**1. AutoLogin**
1. **IDEA NAME**: AutoLogin
2. **PROBLEM**: Users are frustrated with constant logouts and login issues on their favorite apps.
3. **SOLUTION**: An Android app that automatically logs users back into their accounts after a logout or crash.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Premium users who want ad-free experience and extra features (₦500/month).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (premium), free (basic).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Day 1-2: Research existing solutions and identify potential partners.
	* Day 3-4: Design the app's UI/UX and create a prototype.
	* Day 5-7: Develop a minimal viable product (MVP) and test it with a small group of users.
7. **WHY NOW**: The recent app update bug on the Play Store highlights the need for a reliable login solution.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (low development costs, potential for partnerships).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for customer support).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can be developed by a small team, but may require additional help for marketing).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched quickly, but may require iterations based on user feedback).
