After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities that align with the given constraints. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to save money and earn passive income.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for daily purchases, such as groceries and household items.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers who shop at partner stores and online platforms.
5. **PRICE**: 5% cashback on purchases (no monthly fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Research and partner with local stores and online platforms.
   - Develop a basic app using the existing Android developer.
   - Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money passively, and cashback apps are popular globally.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 8/10 (low investment needed for marketing and partnerships)
   - Passive Income: 9/10 (cashback rewards are a form of passive income)
   - Team Size: 6/10 (requires a small team for marketing and partnerships)
   - Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within weeks)

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack a convenient and automated way to save money.
3. **SOLUTION**: A web and mobile app that helps users save money automatically by rounding up their purchases or setting aside a fixed amount regularly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Individuals who want to save money.
5. **PRICE**: ₦100 per month (flat fee).
6. **FIRST WEEK**: 
   - Develop a basic web app using the existing web developer.
   - Integrate with local banks for secure transactions.
   - Create a social media campaign to promote the app.
7. **WHY NOW**: Users are looking for ways to save money, and automated savings apps are gaining popularity.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
   - Investment: 9/10 (low investment needed for development and marketing)
   - Passive Income: 10/10 (flat monthly fee provides passive income)
   - Team Size: 5/10 (requires a small team for development and marketing)
   - Time to Market: 9/10 (can be launched within weeks)
