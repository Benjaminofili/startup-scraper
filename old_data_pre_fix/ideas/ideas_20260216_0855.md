**1. Customer Respect Feedback App**
* **IDEA NAME**: RespectMeter
* **PROBLEM**: Poor customer service from businesses, such as the one experienced by the PlayStore user, can lead to a loss of trust and loyalty.
* **SOLUTION**: A mobile app where customers can rate and review businesses based on their customer service experience.
* **WHO PAYS**: Businesses will pay a monthly subscription fee to access customer feedback and improve their services.
* **PRICE**: ₦5,000 per month
* **FIRST WEEK**: 
	1. Conduct market research to identify the most common customer service pain points in Nigeria.
	2. Design a user-friendly app interface and develop a basic prototype.
	3. Reach out to local businesses to gather interest and feedback on the app.
* **WHY NOW**: The PlayStore user's complaint highlights the need for a platform that holds businesses accountable for their customer service.
* **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	+ Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs can be low, but marketing and maintenance may require some investment)
	+ Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required to maintain the app and engage with businesses)
	+ Team Size: 7/10 (a small team can manage the app, but may require additional help for marketing and sales)
	+ Time to Market: 8/10 (can be launched within a few weeks with a basic prototype)
