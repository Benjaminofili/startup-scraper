After analyzing the problems from real users, I've identified the top 5 startup opportunities for Nigerian university students. Here are the ideas:

**1. CashBackNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: CashBackNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians struggle to find reliable and trustworthy cashback apps for their daily purchases.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that offers cashback rewards for purchases made at local stores, restaurants, and online platforms.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers, businesses, and advertisers.
5. **PRICE**: 5% commission on cashback rewards (e.g., ₦50 cashback on a ₦1000 purchase).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Research existing cashback apps and their business models.
	* Develop a minimum viable product (MVP) with a user-friendly interface.
	* Partner with 5 local businesses to offer exclusive cashback deals.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the rise of online shopping and mobile payments in Nigeria, a reliable cashback app can capitalize on this trend.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 8/10 (initial development costs, but potential for revenue sharing with businesses).
	* Passive Income: 6/10 (some manual work required for business partnerships and customer support).
	* Team Size: 7/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members for growth).
	* Time to Market: 8/10 (can launch an MVP within weeks, but may require iterations for improvement).

**2. SaveNG**
1. **IDEA NAME**: SaveNG
2. **PROBLEM**: Nigerians lack access to simple, automated savings tools for their daily expenses.
3. **SOLUTION**: A mobile app that helps users save money automatically through rounding up purchases or setting aside a fixed amount regularly.
4. **WHO PAYS**: Consumers ( subscription-based model).
5. **PRICE**: ₦500/month (basic plan) or ₦1,000/month (premium plan with additional features).
6. **FIRST WEEK**:
	* Conduct market research to understand Nigerian savings habits.
	* Develop a basic savings algorithm and user interface.
	* Integrate with local payment gateways for seamless transactions.
7. **WHY NOW**: With the increasing adoption of mobile payments, a user-friendly savings app can help Nigerians develop healthy financial habits.
8. **FEASIBILITY SCORES**:
	* Investment: 9/10 (low initial costs, with potential for revenue through subscription fees).
	* Passive Income: 8/10 (automated savings algorithm, but some manual work required for customer support).
	* Team Size: 8/10 (can start with a small team, but may require more members for growth).
	* Time to Market: 9/10 (can launch an MVP within weeks, with minimal iterations required).
